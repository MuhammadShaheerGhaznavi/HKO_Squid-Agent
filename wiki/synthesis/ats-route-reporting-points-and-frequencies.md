## Content
Across ATS and PBN routes, certain waypoints are designated as compulsory ATS reporting points for all aircraft, including: IKELA, ELATO, SIKOU, BEKOL, NOMAN, DOTMI, TAMOT, SABNO, KAPLI, LELIM, ENVAR, EPKAL, DOSUT, DULOP, ASOBA, and others. Other waypoints are compulsory reporting points for non-jet aircraft only. Controlling units are primarily Hong Kong Radar, with primary (PRI) and secondary (SRY) frequencies varying by route segment. Examples: A1 uses 125.325 MHz (PRI) and 132.775 MHz (SRY) for IKELA-IDOSI; A461 uses 132.15 MHz (PRI) and 129.9 MHz (SRY) for NOMAN-BIGEX, and 123.8 MHz (PRI) and 124.05 MHz (SRY) for BIGEX-BEKOL below FL250; B330 uses 127.1 MHz (PRI) and 123.7 MHz (SRY); G581 uses 121.3 MHz (PRI) and 128.125 MHz (SRY); L642 uses 122.95 MHz (PRI) and 135.6 MHz (SRY) for EPKAL-ENBOK, and 125.8 MHz (PRI) and 132.775 MHz (SRY) for ENBOK-EPDOS; M750 uses 118.925 MHz (PRI) and 128.125 MHz (SRY); M771 uses 122.95 MHz (PRI) and 135.6 MHz (SRY) for DOSUT-DULOP, and 125.8 MHz (PRI) and 132.775 MHz (SRY) for DULOP-DUMOL; M772 uses 122.95 MHz (PRI) and 135.6 MHz (SRY); P901 uses 125.325 MHz (PRI) and 132.775 MHz (SRY) for IKELA-IDOSI, and 127.1 MHz (PRI) and 123.7 MHz (SRY) for IDOSI-BIGEX; Q1 uses 125.8 MHz (PRI) and 132.775 MHz (SRY). These frequencies are used for ATC communication and are subject to change. See [[ATS Routes Overview]] and [[PBN Routes in Hong Kong FIR]].
