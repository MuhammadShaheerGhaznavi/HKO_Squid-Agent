## Content
Hong Kong civil aviation is governed by several key ordinances and regulations. The primary legislation is the Civil Aviation Ordinance (Cap. 448), which implements the Chicago Convention and its Annexes. The Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995 (Cap. 448C) is the highest operating regulation, supported by CADs, HKARs, and AICs. Other important regulations include: Air Transport (Licensing of Air Services) Regulations (Cap. 448A) for scheduled/non-scheduled air services; Hong Kong Civil Aviation (Investigation of Accidents) Regulations (Cap. 448B) for accident investigation; Air Navigation (Dangerous Goods) Regulations (Cap. 448C Sch. 16) for dangerous goods; Air Navigation (Flight Prohibition) Order (Cap. 448E) for prohibited airspace; Hong Kong Air Navigation (Fees) Regulations (Cap. 448D) for fees; Civil Aviation (Insurance) Order (Cap. 448F) for insurance; Dangerous Goods (Consignment by air) (Safety) Regulations (Cap. 384A) for dangerous goods consignment; Civil Aviation (Aircraft Noise) (Certification) Regulations (Cap. 312A) for noise certification; The Aviation Security Regulations (Cap. 494A) for restricted area permits; Air passenger Departure Tax Ordinance (Cap. 140) for departure tax; Prevention and Control of Disease Ordinance (Cap. 599) for disease control; Civil Aviations (Births, Deaths and Missing Persons) Ordinance (Cap. 173) for records; Hong Kong Airport (Control of Obstructions) Ordinance (Cap. 301) for building height restrictions; Civil Aviation (Aircraft Noise) Ordinance (Cap. 312) for noise control; Dangerous Goods (Consignment by Air) (Safety) Ordinance (Cap. 384) for dangerous goods; Airport Authority Ordinance (Cap. 483) for airport management; Aviation Security Ordinance (Cap. 494) for security, giving legal force to Tokyo Convention 1963, Hague Convention 1970, Montreal Convention 1971, and Montreal Protocol 1988; Carriage by Air Ordinance (Cap. 500) for carriage conventions. International treaties applicable to Hong Kong include the Chicago Convention 1944, International Air Services Transit Agreement 1944, Tokyo Convention 1963, Hague Convention 1970, Montreal Convention 1971, Montreal Supplementary Protocol 1988, Convention on the Marking of Plastic Explosives 1991, Warsaw Convention 1929, Hague Protocol 1955, Guadalajara Convention 1961, and Montreal Convention 1999. See [[Aircraft Radio and Navigation Equipment Requirements in Hong Kong FIR]] for operational requirements under these laws.
