## Content
In the Hong Kong FIR, most ATS and PBN routes are classified as Class A airspace, with upper limits often UNL and lower limits at 8000 ft AMSL or higher. Some route segments have Class C airspace, such as A461 between BIGEX and BEKOL (4500 ft to 9000 ft AMSL) and B330 between BIGEX and TAMOT (6000 ft to 9000 ft AMSL). Lateral limits are typically 50 NM for en-route segments, but 12 NM for some terminal transition segments. Compulsory ATS reporting points are designated for all aircraft at FIR boundaries and key waypoints (e.g., IKELA, ELATO, SIKOU, BEKOL, NOMAN, TAMOT, DOTMI, SABNO, KAPLI, LELIM, ENVAR, EPKAL, DOSUT, DULOP, ASOBA). Other waypoints are compulsory reporting points for non-jet aircraft only. For airspace classification within or above Hong Kong CTR/UCARAs, refer to ENR 1.4 and ENR 2.1. RVSM airspace (FL290-410) requires reference to FLAS in ENR 1.8 para 8.
