## Content
The Flight Level Assignment Scheme (FLAS) is applicable to the South China Sea airspace and adjacent areas, developed with ICAO Asia/Pacific RVSM Task Force guidance. Flights departing or entering Hong Kong FIR are allocated specific flight levels based on route and direction. Key FLAS tables include:
- **Hong Kong-Manila FIR**: A461/M501 and A583: HK to Manila: F290, F330, F370, F410; Manila to HK: F300, F340, F380. M772: Departing Jakarta (Halim Perdanakusuma, Soekarno-Hatta) landing HK/PRC: F300, F380; Departing Borneo (Bintulu, Kuching, Sibu, Brunei, Labuan, Miri) landing HK: F300, F380.
- **Hong Kong-Guangzhou FIR**: A461: Departing HK landing Guangzhou: Primary S0420, Secondary S0450; Departing HK transiting Guangzhou: S0690; Transiting both: S0890*, S0950, S1010, S1070, S1130, S1190. B330: Transiting both: S0840, S0920, S0980, S1040, S1100, S1160, S1220*. W68: Transiting HK landing Guangzhou: Primary S0450, Secondary S0420. R473: Transiting Guangzhou landing HK: F190, F210, F230; Departing Guangzhou transiting HK: F230, F250.
- **Hong Kong-Sanya FIR**: A1/P901: Sanya to HK: F270, F290, F330, F370, F390 (no pre-coordination 1601-2300 UTC; F390 available H24 for landing HK/Macao/Shenzhen or overflying via BEKOL), F410, F450; HK to Sanya: F280, F300, F340, F380, F400, F430. L642: HK to Sanya: F280, F310, F320, F350, F360, F390 (no pre-coordination 2301-1600 UTC), F400. M771: Sanya to HK: F270, F310, F320, F350, F360, F390 (no pre-coordination 2301-1600 UTC), F400. A202: Sanya to HK: Departing Sanya: S0810, S0890; Points beyond ASSAD: S1010, S1070, S1130, S1190, S1250 (S1250 for overflying without prior coordination); HK to Sanya: Landing Sanya: S0840; Points beyond ASSAD: S1040, S1160, S1220. R339: HK to Sanya: Points beyond Nanning: S0980, S1040, S1100, S1160, S1220.
- **Hong Kong-Shantou**: A470: Depart Shantou for HK: S0420; Exit HK landing Shantou: S0450.
- **Hong Kong-Nanning**: A202/R339: HK to Nanning: Landing Boao: S0660, S0720; Landing Beihai/Nanning: S0720, S0780.
- **Hong Kong-Taipei**: A1: Taipei to HK: per ICAO Annex 2 Appendix 3 Table a); HK to Taipei: at or below F270 per same. G581: Taipei to HK: per Table a); HK to Taipei: at or below F250 per Table a). M750: HK to Taipei: at or above F270 per Table a); F290 not available 2300-1159 UTC. G863: Taipei to HK: within RVSM: F300, F340, F380, F400; outside RVSM: per Table a); HK to Taipei: within RVSM: F290, F330, F370, F390 (for Taipei destinations only), F410; outside RVSM: per Table a).
- **Hong Kong-Xiamen**: A470: Exit Xiamen to HK: S0660, S0720, S0780; Exit HK landing Xiamen/Jinjiang/Meixian: S0510, S0630, S0750*; landing Fuzhou/Wuyishan/Wenzhou: S0630, S0750.
- **Hong Kong-Shanghai**: A470: Exit Shanghai landing HK/Macao: F280, F300; Exit Shanghai transiting HK: F280, F300, F360, F380; Exit HK landing Hangzhou/Yiwu/Ningbo: S0810*, F330; landing other aerodromes: S0810*, F330, F350, F390. M503: Departing Shanghai Pudong/Qingdao/Yantai/Dalian landing HK: F300; Departing HK/Macao landing Shanghai Pudong: F330; landing Qingdao/Yantai/Dalian: F330, F350.
- **Hong Kong-Zhanjiang**: A202/R339: Zhanjiang to HK: Departing Zhanjiang/Haikou: S0570; HK to Zhanjiang: Landing Zhanjiang/Haikou: S0600.
Note: * indicates subject to prior co-ordination.
