## Content
Hong Kong is party to the following international treaties and conventions:
1. Convention on International Civil Aviation, Chicago, 1944 (Chicago Convention)
2. International Air Services Transit Agreement, Chicago, 1944
3. Convention on Offences and Certain Other Acts Committed on Board Aircraft, Tokyo, 1963 (Tokyo Convention)
4. Convention for the Suppression of Unlawful Seizure of Aircraft, The Hague, 1970 (The Hague Convention)
5. Convention for the Suppression of Unlawful Acts Against the Safety of Civil Aviation, Montreal, 1971 (Montreal Convention 1971)
6. Protocol for the Suppression of Unlawful Acts of Violence at Airports Serving International Civil Aviation, Montreal, 1988 (Montreal Supplementary Protocol 1988)
7. Convention on the Marking of Plastic Explosives for the Purpose of Detection, Montreal, 1991
8. Convention for the Unification of Certain Rules Relating to International Carriage by Air, Warsaw, 1929 (Warsaw Convention)
9. Protocol to Amend the Warsaw Convention, The Hague, 1955 (Hague Protocol 1955)
10. Convention, Supplementary to the Warsaw Convention, for the Unification of Certain Rules Relating to International Carriage by Air Performed by a Person Other Than the Contracting Carrier, Guadalajara, 1961
11. Convention for the Unification of Certain Rules for International Carriage by Air, Montreal, 1999 (Montreal Convention 1999)
