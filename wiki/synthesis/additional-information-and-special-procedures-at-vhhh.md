## Content
Low-level TCAS alerts may occur due to VFR/SVFR traffic. Aircraft with 'weight-on-wheel' switch must keep transponder on until fully parked. Reduced Engine Taxi Out (RETO) is permitted only for Airbus A320 series with specific engine types, thrust not exceeding 40% N1, and requires AAHK approval. Maritime facilities west of HKIA: Dayushan (Lantau) Anchorage No. 1 and No. 2, Longgu West Fairway, Tonggu Fairway, Lingding Fairway. Tall vessels up to 170 m air-draft may enter Anchorage No. 1 during contingency. Vessels in Longgu West Fairway (2.8 NM from RWY 07L/25R) or Anchorage No. 2 (1.3 NM) affect procedures: for heights above 49 m up to 55 m, RNP suspended, ILS available; above 55 m up to 63 m, RNP suspended, ILS available, increase climb gradient to 3.8% until 700 ft; above 63 m up to 66 m, RNP and ILS suspended, LOC available, climb gradient 4.3%; above 110 m up to 170 m, RNP and ILS suspended, LOC available, climb gradient 5.0% for RWY 25R SIDs and 3.6% for RWY 25C SIDs. Air-draft measured from 2.65 m above mean sea level. Lighting provisions on Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge (HZMB), Hong Kong Link Road (HKLR), and Hong Kong Boundary Crossing Facilities (HKBCF) include lights outlining artificial islands, street lights, and high mast lights. Coordinates of artificial islands: A at 221657N 1135031E, B at 221659N 1134707E. Pilots should remain vigilant to avoid distraction.
