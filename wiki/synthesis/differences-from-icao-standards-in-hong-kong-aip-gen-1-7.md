## Content
The Hong Kong AIP GEN 1.7 lists differences from ICAO SARPs. Key differences include:
- **Personnel Licensing (Annex 1):** Minimum ages for PPL (Gliders) and CPL (Gliders) are 17 and 18; for PPL (Balloons/Airships) and CPL (Balloons) also 17 and 18; Flight Engineer's Licence minimum age is 21. PPL holders with flight instructor rating may be paid for instruction within same flying club. Cross-country flight experience requirements may be endorsed if not met.
- **Rules of the Air (Annex 2):** Definitions for acrobatic manoeuvres, aeronautical radio station, ATC unit, apron, cloud ceiling, flight crew, ground visibility, special VFR flight, and taxiing are provided. VFR rules specify cloud clearance and visibility minima for different airspace classes and altitudes. Special VFR is permitted only in Hong Kong CTR with ATC clearance. VMC clearances are not offered; timed approaches not authorized; light signals not used; SLOP not implemented.
- **Meteorological Service (Annex 3):** No differences.
- **Aeronautical Charts (Annex 4):** Some charts are not True North orientated; aerodrome charts are runway orientated. Horizontal scale is 1:15,000 instead of 1:10,000. World Aeronautical Chart 1:1,000,000 not produced; Local Flying Chart scale 1:100,000; Area Chart scale 1:800,000.
- **Units of Measurement (Annex 5):** No differences.
- **Operation of Aircraft (Annex 6):** Aerial work is not defined as per ICAO. Hand-held microphone use prohibited below FL150 in controlled airspace. Recency requirement for pilot-in-command is 13 months instead of 12.
- **Facilitation (Annex 9):** Character width for M and W differs. Visas valid up to 3 months. PNR not in place. Hong Kong is a non-tariff port; duty only on alcoholic liquors, tobacco, hydrocarbon oil, and methyl alcohol. CBNI declaration required for amounts over HKD120,000.
- **Aeronautical Telecommunications (Annex 10):** Several localizers and glide-paths at HKIA do not meet standard coverage criteria.
- **Air Traffic Services (Annex 11):** Class G airspace requires continuous two-way radio communication. Definitions for ATC unit, flight crew, incident, operator, special VFR flight, and taxiing are provided.
- **Search and Rescue (Annex 12):** SAR system does not include a legal framework; some helicopters not equipped with homing device; International Code of Signals not carried.
- **Aerodromes (Annex 14):** No-entry bar not provided; low intensity obstruction lights on rescue vehicles are red.
- **Aeronautical Information Services (Annex 15):** No metadata collected for complete data chain; digital data sets not available.
- **Environmental Protection (Annex 16):** Multiple differences filed in Chinese for CORSIA.
- **Aviation Security (Annex 17), Dangerous Goods (Annex 18), Safety Management (Annex 19):** No differences.

For full details, refer to AIP GEN 1.7.
