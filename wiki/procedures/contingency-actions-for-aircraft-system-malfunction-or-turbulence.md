## Content
Contingency actions are provided for various scenarios in RVSM airspace:
- **Scenario 1**: Loss/degradation of all primary altimetry or inability to maintain CFL due to turbulence/loss of altitude control. Pilots maintain CFL, watch for traffic, alert nearby aircraft via lights and broadcasts on 121.5/123.45 MHz, notify ATC. Controllers obtain intentions, provide separation if possible, or accommodate exit from RVSM.
- **Scenario 2**: Failure/loss of accuracy of one primary altimetry (e.g., >200 ft difference). Pilots cross-check standby altimeter, notify ATC of loss of redundancy.
- **Scenario 3**: Loss of redundancy in primary altimetry. If remaining system works, couple to autopilot, notify ATC.
- **Scenario 4**: Primary altimeters diverge by more than 200 ft. Determine defective system, couple functioning altimeter; if unable, follow Scenario 5.
- **Scenario 5**: All automatic altitude control systems fail. Maintain CFL manually, watch for traffic, notify ATC, consider exiting RVSM or executing contingency manoeuvre.
- **Scenario 6**: All primary altimetry unreliable/fail. Maintain CFL using standby altimeter, alert nearby aircraft, consider declaring emergency, notify ATC.
- **Scenario 7**: Turbulence greater than moderate affecting ability to maintain flight level. Watch for traffic, notify ATC, request flight level change if needed, or execute contingency manoeuvre.
Controllers in all scenarios assess traffic, provide separation, notify adjoining facilities, and monitor the situation.
