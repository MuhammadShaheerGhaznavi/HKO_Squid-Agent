## Content
Operators of aircraft passing through Hong Kong FIR without landing are contractually obliged to pay En-Route Navigation Charges (ENC) to the HKSAR Government. Charges are calculated per nautical mile flown, based on the date of flight (local time when the flight exits the airspace). Rates: from 1 Sep 2019 to 31 Aug 2020: HK$4.60 per nm (Gazette Notice No. 4684/2019, published 26 July 2019); from 1 Sep 2020 to 31 Aug 2021: HK$5.00; from 1 Sep 2021 onwards: HK$5.50. Distance flown is computed using standard calculated distances between reporting points; variations for weather or other reasons are not considered. Payment must be made within 14 days of the demand note; demand notes are issued twice a month (covering 1st-15th and 16th-30th/31st). Operators with unsatisfactory payment records may be required to provide a banker's guarantee equivalent to one month's anticipated charges. Military aircraft overflights are exempt. Payment is in Hong Kong Dollars, via wire transfer or bank draft. For wire transfer: Bank: The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corp. Ltd., Address: No. 1 Queen's Road Central, Hong Kong, Swift Code: HSBC-HKHH-HKH, Account No.: 002-268126-008, Account Holder: The Government of the HKSAR Treasury No. 1 Collection Account (Attn: Civil Aviation Department). All bank charges are borne by the remitter; exact amount in HKD is required to avoid underpayment. Enquiries: encbilling@cad.gov.hk.
