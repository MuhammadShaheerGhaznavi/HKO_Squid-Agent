## Content
Operators of aircraft passing through Hong Kong FIR without landing are contractually obliged to pay En-Route Navigation Charges to the HKSAR Government. Charges are calculated per nautical mile flown at rates specified in Gazette Notices. The rates are: from 1 September 2019 to 31 August 2020: HK$4.60 per nautical mile (Gazette Notice No. 4684/2019, published 26 July 2019); from 1 September 2020 to 31 August 2021: HK$5.00; from 1 September 2021 onwards: HK$5.50. The 'date of flight' is the local time date on which the flight exits the airspace under CAD control. Distance flown is computed by standard calculated distances between reporting points along the route; variations for weather or other reasons are not considered. Payment must be made within fourteen days of the demand note, unless otherwise specified. Demand notes are issued twice a month covering services from 1st to 15th and 16th to 30th/31st. Operators with unsatisfactory payment records may be required to provide a banker's guarantee equivalent to one month's anticipated charges. Military aircraft overflights are exempt from these charges. Payment is in Hong Kong Dollars, via wire transfer or bank draft. For wire transfer: Bank: The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corp. Ltd., address No. 1 Queen's Road Central, Hong Kong, Swift Code HSBC-HKHH-HKH, Account No. 002-268126-008, Account Holder: The Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, Treasury No. 1 Collection Account (Attn.: Civil Aviation Department). All bank charges are borne by the remitter; exact amount in HKD is required to avoid underpayment. Enquiries: encbilling@cad.gov.hk.
