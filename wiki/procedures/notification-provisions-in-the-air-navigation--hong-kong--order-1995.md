## Content
The Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995 specifies that certain provisions are notified via NOTAMs, AIP, or other official publications. Key notifications include:
- **Article 35(2) (Radio Frequencies)**: Notified in ENR 2.1.
- **Articles 71(1)(a) and 73 (Aerodromes)**: Licensed aerodromes notified in AD 1.1.
- **Article 98 (Class A and C airspace)**: Notified in ENR 2.1.
- **Article 98 (Control Area and Control Zone)**: Notified in ENR 2.1.
- **Article 98 (Danger Area)**: Notified in ENR 5.1.
- **Article 14, Schedule 6 para 2(1)(c) (ILS equipment)**: Notified in GEN 1.5.
- **Article 14, Schedule 6 para 2(2) (SSR transponder)**: Notified in GEN 1.5.
- **Rule 21 (Flight in Class A airspace)**: Notified in ENR 1.2 and ENR 1.3.
- **Rule 26 (Semi-circular rule; altimeter setting)**: Notified in ENR 1.7.
- **Rule 27(3) (Holding and instrument approach)**: Notified in ENR 1.5.
- **Rule 28 (Position reports)**: Notified in ENR 1.1.
- **Rule 35(2) (Radio frequencies for ATC at aerodromes)**: Notified in VHHH AD 2.18.
