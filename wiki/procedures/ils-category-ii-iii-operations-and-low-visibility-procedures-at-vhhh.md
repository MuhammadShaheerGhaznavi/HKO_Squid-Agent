## Content
Foreign operators wishing to conduct ILS CAT II/III operations at VHHH must submit a completed proforma and their State's CAT II/III authorisation to the Director-General of Civil Aviation. They must comply with the more restrictive minima. Low Visibility Procedures (LVP) are established for visibility less than RVR 550 m or ceiling less than 200 ft. When LVP are in force, pilots are advised via ATIS. CAT III approaches are available on request. Runway ILS equipment: RWY 07R, 07C, 25L have CAT II ILS; RWY 07L and 25C have CAT III ILS. Holding positions: For RWY 07R/25L, TWY J1, J2, J10, J11 are combined CAT I/II; TWY K is combined CAT I/II for RWY 25L; separate CAT I and II on K1 and K for RWY 07R. For RWY 07C/25C, TWY A1, A2 are combined CAT I/II; A11, A12 are combined CAT I/II/III; separate CAT I and II on F1/E and F2/F for 07C; separate CAT I and II/III on F7/F and F8/E for 25C. For RWY 07L/25R, TWY C1, C2 are combined CAT I/II/III; C11, C12 are CAT I. Runway entry for departure: normal entry via specific taxiways (e.g., RWY 07L via C1, C2; 07C via A1, A2 or F1, F2; 07R via J1, J2 or K1; 25L via J10, J11 or K7; 25C via A11, A12 or F7, F8; 25R via C11, C12). Intersection departures via other taxiways (e.g., 07L via C3; 07C via A4; 07R via J3 or K2; 25L via J9 or K6; 25C via A9; 25R via C10). Practice ILS CAT II/III approaches are allowed but without full safeguarding.
