## Content
Scheduled air services to/from Hong Kong by foreign-registered aircraft require an operating permit from the Director-General of Civil Aviation. Applications must be submitted via the online E-filing system at least one month before commencement. Required documents and checklist are available at http://www.cad.gov.hk/application/checklist_scheduled_service.zip. Non-scheduled air services (charter) also require a permit, applied at least 3 working days before arrival/departure (2 weeks for new/infrequent operators). Applications for both passengers and cargo on non-scheduled flights are not normally considered. Permits are granted if the applicant demonstrates that scheduled services cannot satisfy a genuine demand, and for foreign airlines, reciprocity is required. Permits may include conditions; failure to comply may lead to prosecution. Non-scheduled services cannot be advertised to the general public except for domestic services. Certain passengers/cargo may be carried without charge, including infants under 2, directors/employees of permit-holder, tour conductors (1 per 15 persons), cargo for permit-holder's use, authorized representatives of aeronautical authorities, and for cargo charters, persons with special skills (e.g., security guards). See also [[Entry, Transit and Departure of Aircraft in Hong Kong]].
