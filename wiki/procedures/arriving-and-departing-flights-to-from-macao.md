## Content
Hong Kong ATC provides ATS surveillance service to aircraft on approach, missed approach, and departure to/from Macau International Airport while transiting Hong Kong airspace. For arriving flights, speed restrictions apply: maximum approach turning speed 190 KIAS, maximum missed approach turning speed 185 KIAS. Pilots unable to comply must inform ATC. In case of loss of communication, arriving aircraft shall comply with the STAR procedure and join the approach feed-in procedure for the notified runway. Departing flights must comply with speed restrictions in SIDs; in case of loss of communication, comply with last acknowledged clearance up to next reporting point in SID/TTR, then climb to flight planned cruising level and follow SID/TTR track to Hong Kong TMA exit point.
