## Content
Hong Kong ATC provides ATS surveillance service to aircraft on approach, missed approach, and departure to/from Macau International Airport while transiting Hong Kong airspace. For arrivals, speed restrictions: maximum approach turning speed 190 KIAS, maximum missed approach turning speed 185 KIAS. Pilots unable to comply must inform ATC. In case of loss of communication, arriving aircraft shall comply with STAR and join approach feed-in procedure for notified runway. Departing aircraft shall comply with SID speed restrictions; in loss of communication, comply with last acknowledged clearance up to next reporting point, then climb to flight planned cruising level and follow SID/TTR track to TMA exit point.
