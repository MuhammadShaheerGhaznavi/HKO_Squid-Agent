## Content
IFR arrivals should expect a Standard Instrument Arrival (STAR), with RNP 1 STARs implemented in Hong Kong TMA. Pilots must plan descent profiles per published STARs to ensure vertical separation. Speed control is in force unless cancelled; arriving aircraft fly at 250 KIAS or less below 10,000 ft AMSL, and comply with STAR speed restrictions. Minimum inter-arrival spacing at HKIA is 3.0 NM, subject to wake turbulence. Pilots must inform Approach Control on first contact if unable to comply with specified speeds or if planned final approach speed is below 125 KIAS (Vref plus corrections). If not receiving good instrument indications, pilots should inform ATC and not descend below 4,500 ft until positively identified. In case of 'unable RNP' or loss of GNSS, revert to RNAV 1 (DME/DME/IRU) and inform ATC using phraseology 'UNABLE RNP 1 [DUE TO (reason)]'. If ground aids are unserviceable, ATC will provide alternative clearance or vectoring.
