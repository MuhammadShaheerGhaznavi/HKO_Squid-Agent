## Content
The AMO provides comprehensive meteorological services: Two Aviation Forecasters are on duty H24. TAFs are issued every 3 hours, valid for 30 hours, including max/min temperatures. A 9-12 hour forecast is extracted for VOLMET. Landing forecasts are trend forecasts appended to MET reports. Flight documentation is available on AMIDS two hours before departure, with updates via pop-up messages. Documentation includes significant weather charts, upper wind/temperature data, METAR/SPECI, TAFs, SIGMETs, volcanic ash and tropical cyclone advisories. For long-haul flights, SWH and Wind/Temp charts from WAFCs are supplied; medium-level flights receive SWM charts from AMO. In case of AMIDS failure, documentation is provided via fax. Information is retained for at least 30 days. Operators must notify HKO at least one month in advance of new routes or changes. Flight crew can request additional information via telephone or AMIDS.
