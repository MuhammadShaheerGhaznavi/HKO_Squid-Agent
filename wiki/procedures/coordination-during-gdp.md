## Content
During a GDP, prior coordination between the initiating ATFMU and facilitating ATFMU is needed if a departure cannot comply with the assigned CTOT within the compliance window. Rules of thumb for revised CTOT:
- **Before cabin door closed**: The Airspace User (AU) shall notify Hong Kong ATFMU, which coordinates with the initiating ATFMU.
- **After cabin door closed**:
  - Hong Kong ATC (Clearance Delivery or Ground Control) notifies Hong Kong ATFMU, which coordinates with the initiating ATFMU.
  - The Pilot-In-Command (PIC) must ensure a revised CTOT has been coordinated and inform ATC if not received.
Hong Kong ATFMU will not normally entertain requests for earlier CTOTs, except under exceptional circumstances. See [[GDP CTOT Compliance Window]] and [[Hong Kong ATFMU]].
