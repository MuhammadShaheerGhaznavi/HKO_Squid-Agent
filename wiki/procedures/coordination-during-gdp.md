## Content
Prior coordination between initiating and facilitating ATFMU is needed if departure cannot comply with CTOT. Before cabin door closes, AU notifies Hong Kong ATFMU for revised CTOT. After door closes, ATC (Clearance Delivery or Ground Control) notifies ATFMU; PIC must ensure revised CTOT is coordinated and inform ATC if not received. Hong Kong ATFMU does not normally entertain requests for earlier CTOT except exceptional circumstances. See [[GDP CTOT Compliance Window]].
