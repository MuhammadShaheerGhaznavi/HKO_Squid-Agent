## Content
Departing: pilot, crew, and passengers must pass through normal Immigration and Security channels at Hong Kong Business Aviation Centre or Passenger Terminal Building, then be transported directly to aircraft. Arriving: aircraft taxis to parking area, met by Police Airport Security Unit; all occupants transported for Customs, Immigration, and Quarantine (CIQ) clearance. Helicopters: cross-boundary stopovers park at designated location for CIQ; other flights transport to/from Business Aviation Centre or Passenger Terminal. See [[Hong Kong General Aviation Procedures]].
