## Pilot Briefing Requirements

Before operations at Hong Kong International Airport, flight deck crew must be adequately briefed. Hong Kong International Airport is declared **Category B** for Hong Kong registered operators, indicating the second level of pilot briefing intensity. The minimum suggested requirement is a **self-briefing document** covering:

- Terrain at and within the environs of the airport
- Departure, arrival, missed approach procedures, gradients and DMA limits
- Familiarity with the **Windshear and Turbulence Warning System (WTWS)**
- Hong Kong and specific airport weather characteristics and seasonal variations
- Location of nearby aerodromes **Shenzhen, Macao and Zhuhai**, and the delineation of the **Hong Kong TMA boundary**
- Ground handling and breakaway thrust requirements

A system should ensure flight crew remain current **annually** or prior to each operation if less frequent.

## Ground Handling and Manoeuvring Regulations

### Ground Handling Agent
All operators of international flights must employ a **recognised ground handling agent**.

### Taxiing Regulations
- Overtaking moving aircraft is **prohibited**.
- Aircraft must follow **nose-wheel guide lines** on aprons.
- Marshalling is used **only at stands without Safegate system**.

### Persons Qualified to Taxi
- Licensed pilot
- Qualified ground engineer (with certificate signed by a pilot)
- Pupil under instruction authorised by a flying instructor

### Towing Requirements
- Valid **Airport Restricted Area Permit** with driving endorsement
- Valid driving licence from **Transport Department**
- Valid **Aircraft Towing Runway Crossing (ATRC) certificate**
- Written approval from **tug owner** and **aircraft owner/operator**

### Engine Ground Runs
- **At ground idle power**: max two engines, max **10 minutes** on Passenger or Cargo Apron
- **Above idle power**: must be in **engine run-up facility**
- Runs over **10 minutes** only in approved locations
- All runs supervised by **ground staff**
- Initial request to **Apron Control Centre** (tel. 2910 1112)
- Start-up clearance on **121.775 MHz**
- Anti-collision beacons on during run
