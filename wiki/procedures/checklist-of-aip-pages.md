## Content
The Checklist of AIP Pages lists all pages in the AIP with their publication dates. For example, GEN 0.1-1 dated 30 JAN 20, GEN 0.2-1 dated 9 JUL 26, ENR 1.1-1 dated 28 NOV 24, AD 2-VHHH-1 dated 27 JAN 22. The checklist covers all parts: GEN, ENR, and AD, including detailed pages for Hong Kong International Airport (VHHH) and Macao International Airport (VMMC).
