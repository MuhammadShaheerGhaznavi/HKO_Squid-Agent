## Content
The Checklist of AIP Pages (GEN 0.4) provides a detailed list of all pages in the AIP with their publication dates. For example, GEN 0.1-1 is dated 30 JAN 20, GEN 0.2-1 is 9 JUL 26, and GEN 0.4-1 through 0.4-7 are all 9 JUL 26. The checklist covers all parts: GEN, ENR, and AD. It includes pages for Hong Kong International Airport (VHHH) with numerous SID, STAR, and IAC charts, as well as Macao International Airport (VMMC) and Sheung Wan/ Sky Shuttle Heliport (VHSS). The list is updated with each amendment, ensuring users can verify they have the latest pages.
