## Content
Airline operators must inform AIMC by telephone of any subsequent changes to a filed flight plan so that associated ATS messages (DLA, CHG, CNL) can be sent by AIMC. AIMC will contact the operator's Hong Kong office/handling agent for verification/changes, e.g., rectification of routes and flight levels. Operators shall not send DLA, CHG, or CNL on their own to avoid confusion. For flights inbound to HKIA or transiting Hong Kong FIR, flight plans and changes must be filed by AFTN to VHHKZQZX at the aerodrome of departure or with enroute telecommunications service. For delays exceeding 15 minutes beyond EOBT, amend with DLA or submit new flight plan and cancel old one. See [[Flight Plan Submission Requirements]].
