## Content
The ATC Watch Manager shall suspend RVSM procedures within Hong Kong FIR when there are pilot reports of greater than moderate turbulence. When suspended, vertical separation minima between all aircraft shall be 2,000 ft. Aircraft on primary RNAV routes L642 and M771 have priority for level assignment. Non-RVSM compliant aircraft may not flight plan between F290 and F410 inclusive except for humanitarian or SAR flights, State aircraft with senior State persons, or with prior approval from the Director-General of Civil Aviation. Operators must include in Flight Plan Item 18: STS/HUM, STS/SAR, STS/HEAD, STS/STATE, or STS/NONRVSM. Non-RVSM approved aircraft capable of operating at FL280 or above must insert 'STS/NONRVSM'. RVSM approved aircraft have priority for level allocation, and vertical separation between non-RVSM compliant and other aircraft shall be 2,000 ft. Non-RVSM compliant aircraft may climb above F410 or descend below F290 if they do not maintain intermediate levels while passing through RVSM airspace. RVSM compliant aircraft on delivery may operate with crew trained on Hong Kong RVSM policies and a letter of authorization from the Director-General; notification to MAAR (maar@aerothai.co.th) is required.
