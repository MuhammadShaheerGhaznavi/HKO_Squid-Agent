## Content
Flights not for hire or reward require prior clearance from the Director-General of Civil Aviation. Submit details via E-filing at least 3 working days before arrival/departure, including aircraft type, registration, operator, handling agent, dates/times, confirmation of no fare-paying passengers or commercial cargo, completed Declaration of Compliance with Insurance Order (DCA 41), insurance evidence, and confirmation of radio navigation aids compliance. See [[Hong Kong Air Services Licensing]].
