## Content
ICAO Noise Abatement Departure Procedures (NADP 1 and NADP 2) are adopted for all take-offs on RWY 07. NADP 1 reduces noise near the airport, NADP 2 for distant areas. During the Noise Mitigation Period (1500-2300 UTC), the following measures apply: Tung Chung Pass, Silvermine, Gold Coast Corridor, South Pass, East Pass, and Sha Tin Pass are restricted for certain aircraft (e.g., fixed-wing not permitted on Tung Chung Pass, single-engine aircraft not permitted on East Pass). South Runway (07R/25L) is placed on Standby Mode where possible. Preferential use of RWY 07 when tailwind component ≤5 knots. Southbound SID routes via RAMEN are used for RWY 07 departures. For RWY 25 arrivals, preferential use of RNP Y (AR) APCH procedures and Continuous Descent Approach (CDA) are implemented. CDA starts from 8,000 ft or higher, with low thrust and clean configuration. RWY 25 departures remain on SID track until passing 9,000 ft or south of Lantau Island. Noise mitigating measures are not applicable to special authorized flights, medical/SAR flights, or calibration flights.
