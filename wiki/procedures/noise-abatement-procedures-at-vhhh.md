## Content
**ICAO Noise Abatement Departure Procedures**
- NADP 1 for noise reduction near runway end; NADP 2 for areas more distant. All operators must adopt one for RWY 07 take-offs.

**Noise Mitigation Period**
- Daily 1500-2300 UTC. Measures include:
  - Tung Chung Pass: fixed wing aircraft not permitted
  - Silvermine: fixed wing aircraft not permitted
  - Gold Coast Corridor: fixed wing aircraft not permitted
  - South Pass: fixed wing and single engine helicopters not permitted
  - East Pass: single engine aircraft not permitted
  - Sha Tin Pass: single engine aircraft not permitted
- South Runway (07R/25L) on Standby Mode where possible.
- Preferential use of RWY 07 when tailwind ≤5 knots.
- Southbound SIDs via RAMEN for RWY 07 departures.
- Preferential use of RNP Y (AR) APCH for RWY 25.
- Continuous Descent Approach (CDA) for RWY 25 arrivals.
- Special ATC handling for RWY 25 departures.

**Exemptions**: Special authorised flights, medical/SAR priority, calibration flights.
