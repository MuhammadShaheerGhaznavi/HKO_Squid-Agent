## Content
Nine sets of transmissometers and forward scatterers are installed along the runways for RVR and visibility observation. When RVR or visibility is less than 1,500 m, RVR values at thresholds are included in MET reports, disseminated beyond the airport, used for VOLMET, and displayed to ATC. RVR is reported on a scale: from 50 m to 400 m in increments of 25 m; from 400 m to 800 m in increments of 50 m; from 800 m to 1,500 m in increments of 100 m. Values are rounded down to the nearest lower scale point. RVR values are updated every 10 seconds and averaged over 60 seconds. RVR is passed to aircraft when less than 1,500 m at the commencement of each approach and before take-off, with subsequent changes. When all three positions are reported, values are given as touchdown, mid-point, stop-end (e.g., RVR 650, 600, 500). If only two values, the latter is prefixed with position (e.g., RVR 650, stop-end 500). Values above 1,500 m are reported as 'more than 1,500 m'. ATC is notified of transmissometer serviceability changes, and NOTAMs are issued via AIMC.
