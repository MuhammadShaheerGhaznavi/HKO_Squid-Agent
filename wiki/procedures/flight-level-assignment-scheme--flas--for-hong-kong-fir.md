## Content
The Flight Level Assignment Scheme (FLAS) applies to South China Sea airspace. Flights departing or entering Hong Kong FIR are allocated specific flight levels based on route. Key allocations include:
- **Hong Kong-Manila FIR**: A461/M501 and A583: Hong Kong to Manila: F290, F330, F370, F410; Manila to Hong Kong: F300, F340, F380. M772: Departing Jakarta (Halim Perdanakusuma, Soekarno-Hatta) landing Hong Kong/PRC: F300, F380; Departing Borneo (Bintulu, Kuching, Sibu, Brunei, Labuan, Miri) landing Hong Kong: F300, F380.
- **Hong Kong-Guangzhou FIR**: A461: Departing Hong Kong landing Guangzhou: Primary S0420, Secondary S0450; Departing Hong Kong transiting Guangzhou: S0690; Transiting both: S0890*, S0950, S1010, S1070, S1130, S1190. B330: Transiting both: S0840, S0920, S0980, S1040, S1100, S1160, S1220*. W68: Transiting Hong Kong landing Guangzhou: Primary S0450, Secondary S0420. R473: Transiting Guangzhou landing Hong Kong: F190, F210, F230; Departing Guangzhou transiting Hong Kong: F230, F250.
- **Hong Kong-Sanya FIR**: A1/P901: Sanya to Hong Kong: F270, F290, F330, F370, F390 (no pre-coordination 1601-2300 UTC; F390 available H24 for landing Hong Kong, Macao, Shenzhen or overflying via BEKOL), F410, F450; Hong Kong to Sanya: F280, F300, F340, F380, F400, F430. L642: Hong Kong to Sanya: F280, F310, F320, F350, F360, F390 (no pre-coordination 2301-1600 UTC), F400. M771: Sanya to Hong Kong: F270, F310, F320, F350, F360, F390 (no pre-coordination 2301-1600 UTC), F400. A202: Sanya to Hong Kong: Departing Sanya: S0810, S0890; Points beyond ASSAD: S1010, S1070, S1130, S1190, S1250 (S1250 for overflying without prior coordination); Hong Kong to Sanya: Landing Sanya: S0840; Points beyond ASSAD: S1040, S1160, S1220. R339: Hong Kong to Sanya: Points beyond Nanning: S0980, S1040, S1100, S1160, S1220.
- **Hong Kong-Shantou**: A470: Depart Shantou for Hong Kong: S0420; Exit Hong Kong landing Shantou: S0450.
- **Hong Kong-Nanning**: A202/R339: Hong Kong to Nanning: Landing Boao: S0660, S0720; Landing Beihai/Nanning: S0720, S0780.
- **Hong Kong-Taipei**: A1: Taipei to Hong Kong: per ICAO Annex 2 Appendix 3 Table a); Hong Kong to Taipei: at or below F270 per same. G581: Taipei to Hong Kong: per Table a); Hong Kong to Taipei: at or below F250 per Table a). M750: Hong Kong to Taipei: at or above F270 per Table a); F290 not available 2300-1159 UTC. G863: Taipei to Hong Kong: within RVSM: F300, F340, F380, F400; outside RVSM: per Table a); Hong Kong to Taipei: within RVSM: F290, F330, F370, F390 (for Taipei destinations only), F410; outside RVSM: per Table a). Note: Traffic from Hong Kong to Taipei at F270 or above shall route via M750 DADON G581.
- **Hong Kong-Xiamen**: A470: Exit Xiamen to Hong Kong: S0660, S0720, S0780; Exit Hong Kong landing Xiamen, Jinjiang, Meixian: S0510, S0630, S0750*; landing Fuzhou, Wuyishan, Wenzhou: S0630, S0750.
- **Hong Kong-Shanghai**: A470: Exit Shanghai landing Hong Kong/Macao: F280, F300; Exit Shanghai transiting Hong Kong: F280, F300, F360, F380; Exit Hong Kong landing Hangzhou, Yiwu, Ningbo: S0810*, F330; other aerodromes: S0810*, F330, F350, F390. M503: Departing Shanghai Pudong, Qingdao, Yantai, Dalian landing Hong Kong: F300; Departing Hong Kong/Macao landing Shanghai Pudong: F330; landing Qingdao, Yantai, Dalian: F330, F350.
- **Hong Kong-Zhanjiang**: A202/R339: Zhanjiang to Hong Kong: Departing Zhanjiang/Haikou: S0570; Hong Kong to Zhanjiang: Landing Zhanjiang/Haikou: S0600.
Note: * indicates subject to prior co-ordination.
