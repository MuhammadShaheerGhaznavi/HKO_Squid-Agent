## Content
For BOBCAT ATFM, affected flights must submit slot requests by logging into https://www.bobcat.aero between 0000 UTC and 1159 UTC on the day of flight. Operators without dedicated access should email the application form to Bangkok ATFMU. The slot allocation process has three phases: slot request submission, initial slot allocation, and slot distribution to aircraft operators and ANSPs. PICs and airspace users are responsible for ensuring aircraft readiness to meet CTOT and CTO at Kabul FIR entry waypoint. PICs shall arrange take-off as close as possible to CTOT. For flights from HKIA (A-CDM airport), CTOT is integrated into A-CDM. ANSPs must give priority for take-off to flights with ATFM slots, and CTOT shall be included in initial ATC clearance. See [[BOBCAT ATFM for Kabul FIR]] and [[Coordination Procedure for BOBCAT]].
