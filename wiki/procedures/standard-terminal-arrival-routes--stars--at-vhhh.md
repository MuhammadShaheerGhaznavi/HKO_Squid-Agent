## Content
VHHH has several STARs, including RNAV ABBEY 4A/3B, RNAV BETTY 3A/3B, RNAV CANTO 3A for runways 07L/C/R and 3B for runways 25L/C/R, RNAV SIERA 7A/7C for runways 07L/C/R and 7B/7D for runways 25L/C/R, and RNAV(GNSS) ABBEY 2G, BETTY 2G, CANTO 2G, SIERA 2G for runways 25L/C/R. These STARs are documented with specific chart identifiers.
