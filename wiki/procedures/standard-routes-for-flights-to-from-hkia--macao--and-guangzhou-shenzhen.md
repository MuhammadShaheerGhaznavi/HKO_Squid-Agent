## Standard Routes for Flights to/from HKIA, Macao, and Guangzhou/Shenzhen

### General Requirements
- For flights to/from HKIA, specific routes must be filed in Item 15.
- Operators must not include STAR or SID procedures in flight plans; they must use Terminal Transition Routes.
- Altitude restrictions: cross ENPET, SONNY, CYBER, MAPLE at FL260; route via P901 at FL290 or above (RNP10 required) or A1 at FL280 or below.

### Routes for Guangzhou/Shenzhen
- Entry routes include: A1/G581 ELATO J101 SMT DCT TAMOT (ZGGG), A583 SABNO DCT TOFEE DCT SUKER DCT ALDOM J103 SAPAX DCT BIGEX B330 TAMOT (ZGGG), etc.
- Departure routes from ZGGG/ZGSZ include: SIERA DCT MULET DCT SKATE DCT CONGA V621 ELATO A1/G581, etc.

### Altitude Restrictions for Macao/Guangzhou/Shenzhen
- Cross NEDLE at FL230
- Cross ISBAN at FL200/FL260
- Cross CHALI at FL110/FL260
- Cross COTON at FL120
- Cross GOBBI at FL110
- Cross SABNO at FL340 or below
- Route via P901 at FL290 or above (RNP10) or A1 at FL280 or below.
