## Content
VOLMET broadcasts in English are available on HF and VHF channels. The Hong Kong VOLMET on 128.875 MHz is continuous H24, covering ZGSZ (Shenzhen) METAR/SPECI, VMMC (Macao) METAR/SPECI & Trend FCST, RCTP (Taibei) METAR/SPECI & Trend FCST, and RCKH (Gaoxiong) METAR/SPECI & Trend FCST. D-VOLMET is available on request, including VHHH SIGMET. HF broadcasts on 6679 kHz, 8828 kHz, and 13282 kHz occur from H+15 to H+20 and H+45 to H+50, covering VHHH (with notes), ZGGG (Guangzhou/Baiyun), ROAH (Naha), RCTP, RCKH, RPLL (Manila), RPVM (Mactan), and VHHH FCST/Amend FCST. METAR/SPECI older than two hours are not transmitted. Special air-reports not covered by SIGMET are included in D-VOLMET. VHHH METAR/SPECI wind information refers to the mid anemometer of RWY 07L/25R, and visibility refers to prevailing visibility observed by the Weather Observer.
