## Overflight Permissions in Hong Kong

Prior permission is not required for overflights except for:
- State/military aircraft
- Aircraft carrying munitions of war or dangerous goods
- As notified by the Director-General

Requests for state aircraft must go through diplomatic channels. Requests for munitions/dangerous goods must be received at least 11 clear working days before the first flight, addressed to the Dangerous Goods Office at CAD Headquarters, 1 Tung Fai Road, Hong Kong International Airport, Lantau (Tel: +852 2910 6856/6857/6855, Fax: +852 2795 8469). See [[Hong Kong Airspace Overflight Rules]].

## Other Flights Transiting Hong Kong FIR

For flights transiting Hong Kong FIR not specified in previous paragraphs, specific entry and connecting routes are prescribed. Examples include:
- A470 DOTMI DCT SOUSA DCT CONGA V621 ELATO (A1/G581)
- DOTMI DCT SOUSA DCT CONGA V631 ENVAR (M750)
- DOTMI DCT ENROM DCT NOMAN (A461/M501)
- DOTMI DCT ENROM DCT NOPER DCT UNTUL DCT SABNO (A583)
- DOTMI DCT ENROM DCT RUSBI DCT SULUX DCT EPKAL (L642)
- DOTMI DCT ENROM DCT RUSBI DCT SULUX DCT IGLEG DCT IKELA (A1)
- DOTMI DCT ENROM DCT ALLEY V10 SIKOU (A202/R339)
- A1/G581 ELATO DCT MAGOG DCT DOTMI (A470)
- ELATO J101 PONTI DCT BEKOL (A461)
- G86 KAPLI DCT MADRU DCT SULUX DCT IGLEG DCT IKELA (A1)
- KAPLI DCT ALLEY V10 SIKOU (A202/R339)
- A461 NOMAN DCT SANKU DCT BIMIX DCT DOTMI (A470)
- A583 SABNO DCT LEGOD DCT BEKOL (A461)

Approval from ATS Supervisor is required for routes not in the list. See [[Standard Routes for Flights to/from HKIA]].

## Pre-flight Information Service in Hong Kong

The Pre-flight Information Bulletin (PIB) contains a recapitulation of current NOTAMs, with coverage based on scheduled airlines' first sectors originating in Hong Kong. The service is a self-briefing service available at the AIMC. Aircraft operators and agents can download PIBs and the Hong Kong NOTAM Summary from the CADAS Center Terminal via Private Communication Network. For details on the Private Communication Network, refer to GEN 3.4 para 3.6.
