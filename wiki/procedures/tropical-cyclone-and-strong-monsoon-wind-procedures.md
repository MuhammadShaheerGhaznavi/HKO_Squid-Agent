## Content
Tropical cyclones can occur year-round but have produced gale-force winds (mean >33 kt) at HKIA only from May to November. During Super Typhoon Mangkhut (2018), mean hourly wind reached 61 kt with gusts to 84 kt. HKO issues tropical cyclone warning signals: No. 1 (Standby) - cyclone within 800 km; No. 3 (Strong Wind) - sustained speed 41-62 km/h (22-33 kt) with gusts possibly >110 km/h; No. 8 NW/SW/NE/SE (Gale or Storm) - sustained speed 63-117 km/h (34-63 kt) with gusts possibly >180 km/h; No. 9 (Increasing Gale) - gale increasing; No. 10 (Hurricane) - sustained speed ≥118 km/h (64 kt) with gusts possibly >220 km/h. Precautions: when No. 3 is issued, make aircraft ready, secure loose equipment; when No. 8 is issued, fly away serviceable aircraft, securely picket remaining aircraft, and detail duty personnel for wind shifts. Strong Monsoon Signal is issued when mean wind exceeds 21 kt, mainly in winter from northeast monsoon. Owners are responsible for damage caused by their equipment.
