## Content
Tropical cyclones can occur year-round, with gale-force winds at HKIA from May to November; Super Typhoon Mangkhut (2018) brought mean hourly wind of 61 kt and gusts to 84 kt. HKO issues SIGMETs and Tropical Cyclone Warning Signals: No. 1 (Standby) - cyclone within ~800 km; No. 3 (Strong Wind) - sustained 41-62 km/h (22-33 kt); No. 8 NW/SW/NE/SE (Gale/Storm) - sustained 63-117 km/h (34-63 kt); No. 9 (Increasing Gale/Storm); No. 10 (Hurricane) - sustained ≥118 km/h (64 kt). Precautions: when No. 3 issued, make aircraft ready, secure loose equipment; when No. 8 issued, fly away serviceable aircraft, securely picket others, detail duty personnel. Strong Monsoon Signal is issued when mean wind exceeds 21 kt, mainly in winter from northeast or summer southwest monsoon. Owners are responsible for damage caused by their equipment.
