## Content
**ATC Clearance and Push-back**
- Obtain ATC clearance before engine start (except helicopters and local light aircraft).
- PDC data-link service: send RCD message ≤40 min before EOBT; if no CLD within 5 min, contact Delivery on 122.15 MHz.
- Readback: call sign, destination, route, SID, transponder code.
- Push-back procedures: BLUE, RED, GREEN; non-standard push-back possible.
- Engine start restriction at stands S103, S108, W123: only one engine during 180° push-back.

**SIDs**
- RNP 1 required for all departures (except exemptions). Carriage of certified GNSS mandatory.
- If unable RNP: revert to RNAV 1 (DME/DME/IRU) and inform ATC with phraseology "UNABLE RNP 1".
- Conventional SIDs (RAMEN, RUMSY) for aircraft unable RNAV.
- Climb gradients: e.g., RWY 07R RNP 1 RF: 4.8% or 292 ft/NM until 1200 ft; RWY 07C via TEGUB: 6.8% or 414 ft/NM until 3900 ft; etc.
- Departure speed: cross PORPA, ROVER, HH412, HH421, PRAWN, POVEG at ≥205 KIAS.

**STARs**
- RNP 1 required for arrivals. Reversion to RNAV 1 if unable.
- Loss of communication: comply with last acknowledged clearance, descend to 4,500 ft, join holding, then ILS approach.

**ILS Operations**
- RWY 25R: CAT I; RWY 07R, 25L, 07C: CAT II; RWY 07L, 25C: CAT III.
- No marker beacons; DME equivalents for OMF/MMF.
- Speed restrictions: e.g., cross STELA at 180 KIAS, D4.0 at 160-150 KIAS.
- RWY 25R ILS approach via RNAV transition only; do not engage ILS modes before TOPUN.
- GP signals may be affected by ground traffic during CAT I on 07R/25L.
