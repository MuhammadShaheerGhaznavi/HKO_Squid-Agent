## Content
Monitoring of aircraft navigation performance is a joint responsibility of operators, States of Registry, regulatory authorities, and ATS providers. Detection relies primarily on radar monitoring. Lateral deviations are flagged when an aircraft deviates 15 NM or more from track centreline. Longitudinal deviations are flagged when time separation varies by 3 minutes or more, or distance-based standards vary by 10 NM or more (based on ADS-B, radar, or RNAV distance reports). ATC will advise pilots of deviations and investigate with the operator and State of Registry.
