## Content
Two Aviation Forecasters are on duty H24, issuing TAFs valid for 30 hours every 3 hours, including max/min temperatures. A 9-12 hour forecast is extracted for VOLMET. Landing forecasts are trend forecasts appended to MET reports. AMIDS provides self-briefing, flight planning, and documentation, including upper wind/temperature forecasts, SIGWX, METAR/SPECI, TAF, SIGMET, volcanic ash and tropical cyclone advisories, aerodrome warnings, satellite images, radar info, and space weather. Flight documentation is available two hours before departure, updated as needed. For long haul flights, SWH and Wind/Temp charts from WAFCs are supplied. In case of AMIDS failure, fax is used. Information is retained for at least 30 days. For aircraft in flight, VOLMET broadcasts and D-VOLMET are provided. ATS units receive routine, special, and extra MET reports, trend forecasts, SIGMET, wind shear alerts, and real-time data displays. Search and rescue units receive meteorological information as required.
