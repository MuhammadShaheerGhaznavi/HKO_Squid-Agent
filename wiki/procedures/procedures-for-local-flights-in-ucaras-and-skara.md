## Content
**UCARAs**
- Six areas: Mirs Bay, North Border, New Town, Ninepins, Port Shelter, Tolo.
- Class G with two-way radio requirement.
- Vertical limits: New Town, Port Shelter, Ninepins: SFC to 2,000 ft AMSL; Mirs Bay and Tolo: SFC to 3,000 ft; North Border: SFC to 2,000 ft (normally restricted to 1,000 ft, above requires ATC permission).
- Flight notification to Hong Kong Aerodrome Control.
- Report arrival at Shek Kong or other sites to 'Hong Kong Information' immediately prior to landing; if no contact, telephone 2910 6822.
- Alerting action if no landing report within 30 minutes of ETA.
- Position/altitude reports on entering/leaving each UCARA and every 15 minutes.
- North Border: advise Hong Kong Information at least 2 minutes prior if ≤1,000 ft, 3 minutes if above.
- Radio failure: fixed wing proceed to Shek Kong, standard circuit; helicopters land at suitable site.

**SKARA**
- Prior permission from military authority required.
- Entry/exit routes: Kadoorie Gap, Fire Station Gap, Mai Po, Pagoda, Kam Tin Gap.
- Pagoda is close to North Border; avoid entering without coordination.

**Flight Information Service**
- Provided 0900 local to sunset daily; alerting service only outside hours.
- Frequency 121.0 MHz primary, 122.075 MHz secondary.
- Pilots responsible for collision avoidance.
