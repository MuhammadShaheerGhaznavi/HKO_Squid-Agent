## Content
An ATCU (Air Traffic Control Unit) shall only grant an IFR flight a clearance to maintain own separation and remain in visual meteorological conditions (VMC) when specifically requested by the pilot, and only for the climb, descent, or approach portion of the flight by day. In Hong Kong, such clearances are only granted if the aircraft is at or below FL150. This procedure allows pilots to assume separation responsibility under specific conditions, enhancing operational flexibility while maintaining safety.
