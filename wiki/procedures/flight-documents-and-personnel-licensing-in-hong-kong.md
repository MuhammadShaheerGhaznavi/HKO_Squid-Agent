## Content
Under Article 59 of the Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995, the commander must produce on request: certificates of registration, airworthiness, noise certificate or proof of compliance, flight crew licences, and other documents required by the state of registry. General aviation pilots must hold a valid aircrew licence. Visiting pilots operating Hong Kong registered aircraft on day VFR private flights must validate their foreign licence per CAD54 Part 2 Chapter 1 Section 1.8 or Chapter 2 Section 2.8. Conversion of a foreign professional licence to a Hong Kong licence is possible if the applicant shows genuine need and the licence was not issued for equivalence. The DGCA assesses each case individually. Contact: Personnel Licensing Office, Flight Standards and Airworthiness Division, Civil Aviation Department, 1 Tung Fai Road, Hong Kong International Airport, Lantau, Hong Kong.
