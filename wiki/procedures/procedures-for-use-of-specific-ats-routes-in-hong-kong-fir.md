## Content
ATS route A202 via SIKOU is limited to traffic departing or landing Hong Kong or Macao, or departing/landing Guangzhou, Sanya, Hanoi, or Taibei FIRs. Overflying traffic to/from Bangkok FIR and beyond should normally route via IKELA on A1 or P901. ATS routes A1(E), G581, G86, and RNAV 5 route M750 between Hong Kong and Taibei FIR for flights to/from Hong Kong or Macao, or transiting via SIERA (R473), TAMOT (B330), BEKOL (A461), or DOTMI (A470) have specific time restrictions at FIR boundary: at ELATO westbound no restriction, eastbound FL270 or below no restriction, eastbound FL270 or above at ENVAR; at KAPLI eastbound no restriction. For all other transiting flights, restrictions are: at ELATO westbound not available, eastbound FL270 or below available 1700-0059, eastbound FL270 or above at ENVAR; at KAPLI westbound to ALLEY or MADRU only no restriction, eastbound no restriction. RNAV 2 route M503 is exclusive for RNAV 2 capable aircraft, limited to traffic departing Hong Kong or Macao and landing Shanghai Pudong, Qingdao, Yantai, or Dalian, and traffic departing those cities and landing Hong Kong. Aircraft are not allowed to deviate eastwards beyond M503 under normal circumstances; if M503 is unavailable, affected flights should file via ATS route A470. RNP 4 routes M771 and L642 are normally restricted to traffic arriving/departing Hong Kong or Macao airports and traffic transiting to/from Guangzhou FIR. Northeast/southwest bound traffic to/from Taibei FIR should use N892 and L625; only under special circumstances will flights be considered via M771 or L642 (flight plan via BIGEX). RNP10 route M772 is restricted to traffic departing Jakarta to Hong Kong or PRC airports, and departing Borneo to Hong Kong. RNP10 route Q1 is normally restricted to arriving aircraft at Hong Kong Airport via PBN route M771 or M772, and flights transiting Hong Kong FIR via M771 or M772 for DOTMI and then ATS route A470.
