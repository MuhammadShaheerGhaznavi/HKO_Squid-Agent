## Content
ATS route A202 via SIKOU is limited to traffic departing/landing Hong Kong or Macao, or departing/landing Guangzhou, Sanya, Hanoi, or Taibei FIRs. Overflying traffic to/from Bangkok FIR and beyond should normally route via IKELA on A1 or P901. Routes A1(E)/G581, G86, and RNAV 5 M750 between Hong Kong and Taibei FIR: for flights to/from Hong Kong/Macao or transiting via SIERA (R473), TAMOT (B330), BEKOL (A461), or DOTMI (A470), time restrictions apply at FIR boundary: ELATO westbound no restriction, eastbound FL270 or below no restriction, ENVAR eastbound FL270 or above no restriction, KAPLI eastbound no restriction. For all other transiting flights: ELATO westbound not available, eastbound FL270 or below available 1700-0059 UTC, ENVAR eastbound FL270 or above, KAPLI westbound to ALLEY or MADRU only no restriction, eastbound no restriction. RNAV 2 route M503 is exclusive for RNAV 2 capable aircraft, limited to traffic departing Hong Kong/Macao and landing Shanghai Pudong, Qingdao, Yantai, or Dalian, and vice versa. Deviation eastwards beyond M503 is not allowed normally; if M503 unavailable, file via A470. RNP 4 routes M771 and L642 are restricted to traffic arriving/departing Hong Kong or Macao, and transiting to/from Guangzhou FIR. Northeast/southwest bound traffic to/from Taibei FIR should use N892 and L625; only special circumstances allow M771/L642 via BIGEX. RNP10 route M772 is restricted to traffic departing Jakarta to Hong Kong or PRC airports, and departing Borneo to Hong Kong. RNP10 route Q1 is restricted to arriving aircraft at Hong Kong via PBN M771/M772, and flights transiting via M771/M772 for DOTMI then A470.
