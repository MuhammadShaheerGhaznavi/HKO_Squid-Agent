## Content
Aircraft unable to meet RNP 10 or RNP 4 requirements must flight plan at FL280 or below. Operations above FL280 require ATC approval. Pilots wishing to operate on specified PBN routes at or above FL290 must indicate level requirements in Item 18 as RMK/REQ FL (insert level). Approval is subject to ATC coordination and clearance. ATC units will consider traffic density, communications availability, weather conditions, and other pertinent factors when deciding on approval.
