## Content
Local traffic regulations: Airline operators must obtain prior approval from DGCA for each helicopter type. Single-engine helicopters are not permitted; all operators must comply with Category A vertical takeoff and landing profile. Helicopters limited to overall length (including rotor disk) of 17 m and maximum all up weight of 9,000 kg. Noise abatement restrictions during evening period 1900-2259 LT: total movements on both helipads shall not exceed 34; movements from Eastern pad shall not exceed 22; if total movements are 22 or less, all must use Eastern pad; if total exceeds 22, 22 movements must be on Eastern pad.
