## Content
For both scheduled and non-scheduled flights, aircraft captains must present specific documents for entry and departure. Required documents include: General Declaration (1 copy for Immigration, 3 for Customs) and Cargo Manifest (3 copies for Customs). These are standard ICAO format. For freight and unaccompanied baggage, additional documents (e.g., consular invoices, licences, permits) must be submitted to Customs for checking. The responsibility for correct presentation lies with the aircraft captain. See also [[Scheduled and Non-Scheduled Air Services Permits in Hong Kong]].
