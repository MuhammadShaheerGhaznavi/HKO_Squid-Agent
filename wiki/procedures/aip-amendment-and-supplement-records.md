## Record of AIP Amendments

The Record of AIP Amendments lists all amendments with their publication and effective dates. From 2024 to 2026, amendments were issued regularly. Examples include:

- 1/25: published 28 NOV 2024, effective 23 JAN 2025
- 8/25 (AIRAC): published 12 JUN 2025, effective 7 AUG 2025
- 11/25 (AIRAC): published 4 SEP 2025, effective 30 OCT 2025
- 7/26 (AIRAC): published 14 MAY 2026, effective 9 JUL 2026

The latest amendment is 7/26.
