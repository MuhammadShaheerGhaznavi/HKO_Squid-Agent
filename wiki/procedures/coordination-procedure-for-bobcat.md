## Content
Bangkok ATFMU (VTBBZDZX) must be included in AFTN addressees for NOTAMs regarding planned activities affecting slot availability, and for ATS messages (FPL, DEP, DLA, CHG, CNL) for affected flights. If allocated Kabul FIR slot parameters will not be met, a new slot allocation should be obtained as soon as possible, primarily via aircraft operators/flight dispatchers; otherwise Ground Control or Clearance Delivery may assist. PIC shall include CTOT in initial ATC clearance request. Missed slots increase coordination workload; if still at gate, PIC advises Ground Control and obtains new CTOT. After leaving gate, Aerodrome Control assists PIC in coordination with Bangkok ACC/ATFMU. PIC shall adjust cruise flight to comply with slot parameters. See [[BOBCAT ATFM Operating Procedures]] and [[BOBCAT ATFM for Kabul FIR]].
