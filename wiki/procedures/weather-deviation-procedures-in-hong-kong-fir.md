## Content
Weather deviation procedures apply in the South China Sea airspace, especially outside VHF coverage. Pilots should obtain ATC clearance at the earliest possible time; if not possible, broadcast position and intentions on the frequency in use and 121.5 MHz. To request priority, pilots can state 'WEATHER DEVIATION IS REQUIRED' or use 'PAN, PAN' three times. When communications are established, ATC will issue clearance if no conflicting traffic or if separation can be established; otherwise, they will advise of conflicting traffic and request intentions. If no clearance is available, pilots may deviate from track, broadcasting on 121.5 MHz and 123.45 MHz, watching for traffic, turning on exterior lights. For deviations less than 10 NM, maintain assigned level; for greater than 10 NM, initiate level changes based on track: for eastbound (000°M-179°M), left deviation descend 300 ft, right climb 300 ft; for westbound (180°M-359°M), left climb 300 ft, right descend 300 ft. When returning to track, be established at assigned level within 10 NM of centreline.
