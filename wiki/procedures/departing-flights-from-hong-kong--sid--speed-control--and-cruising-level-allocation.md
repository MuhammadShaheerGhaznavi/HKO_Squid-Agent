## Content
IFR departures should expect a Standard Instrument Departure (SID), with RNP 1 SIDs implemented. On first contact with 'Hong Kong Departure', pilots must state call sign, passing altitude to nearest 100 ft, and assigned altitude. Speed control is in force unless cancelled; departing aircraft fly at 250 KIAS or less below 10,000 ft AMSL, and comply with SID speed restrictions. Aircraft must follow SID tracks accurately and connect to the appropriate terminal transition route on termination. Final cruising level is notified at least 10 minutes prior to crossing TMA boundary, except for traffic via BEKOL, which can expect climb to S0480 or above soon after departure. Aircraft must reach assigned cruising level at or before TMA boundary; failure may result in loss of standard separation. For 'unable RNP' or loss of GNSS, revert to RNAV 1 (DME/DME/IRU) and inform ATC. Aircraft reported 'UNABLE RNP 1' on the ground will be issued a clearance for a non-RF SID using RNAV 1. TMA exit points include BEKOL, DOTMI (SOUSA 42 NM before), ELATO (20 NM before), ENVAR, EPDOS, IDOSI (SURFA 23 NM before), KAPLI (20 NM before), LELIM (BESDA 62 NM before), NOMAN (20 NM before), SABNO (20 NM before), SIKOU (DAGBU 41 NM before).
