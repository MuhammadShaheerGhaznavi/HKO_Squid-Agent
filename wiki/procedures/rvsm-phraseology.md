## Content
Recommended phraseology for RVSM operations includes:
- **CONFIRM RVSM APPROVED**: Controller to ascertain RVSM approval status.
- **Negative RVSM**: Pilot reports non-RVSM approval on initial call, in requests for flight level changes, and in read-backs to clearances within RVSM airspace; also for vertical transit through F290 or F410 (except State aircraft).
- **AFFIRM RVSM**: Pilot reports RVSM approval.
- **NEGATIVE RVSM STATE AIRCRAFT**: Pilot of non-RVSM approved State aircraft reports status.
- **UNABLE CLEARANCE INTO RVSM AIRSPACE, MAINTAIN [or DESCEND TO, or CLIMB TO] FLIGHT LEVEL (number)**: Controller denies clearance.
- **UNABLE RVSM DUE TURBULENCE**: Pilot reports severe turbulence affecting height-keeping.
- **UNABLE RVSM DUE EQUIPMENT**: Pilot reports equipment degradation below RVSM requirements; used on initial contact on all frequencies until resolved.
- **READY TO RESUME RVSM**: Pilot reports ability to resume RVSM operations.
- **REPORT ABLE TO RESUME RVSM**: Controller confirms aircraft regained RVSM approval or pilot ready to resume.
