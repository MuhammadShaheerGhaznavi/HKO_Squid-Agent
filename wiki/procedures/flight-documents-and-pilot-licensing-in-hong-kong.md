## Content
Under Article 59 of the Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995, the commander must produce on request: certificates of registration, airworthiness, noise certificate or proof of compliance, flight crew licences, and other documents required by the country of registration. General aviation, private aircraft, or helicopter pilots must hold a valid aircrew licence. Visiting pilots operating day VFR private flights in Hong Kong registered aircraft must validate their foreign licence per CAD54 Part 2 Chapter 1 Section 1.8 or Part 2 Chapter 2 Section 2.8. The DGCA may impose additional requirements. For conversion of a foreign professional flight crew licence to a Hong Kong licence, the applicant must hold a valid ICAO Contracting State licence, not issued for equivalence, and show genuine need. The DGCA assesses fitness (knowledge, experience, competence, skill, physical/mental fitness). Terms are assessed individually and notified in writing. Contact: Personnel Licensing Office, Flight Standards and Airworthiness Division, Civil Aviation Department, 1 Tung Fai Road, Hong Kong International Airport, Lantau, Hong Kong. Detailed info in CAD 54 and CAD 50. See [[Aircraft Radio and Navigation Equipment Requirements in Hong Kong FIR]] for related operational requirements.
