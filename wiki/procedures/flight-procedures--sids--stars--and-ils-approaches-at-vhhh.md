## Content
All aircraft departing HKIA must obtain ATC clearance prior to engine start. Radius-to-Fix (RF) SIDs (BEKOL, DALOL, LEKEN, PECAN) are default for RWY 07R/07L. RWY 07C departures use 'T' designator SIDs (ADLAD, LARIT, DUMEP, XEMEK, SOSLU) during three-runway operations, and RF SIDs during noise mitigation. RNP 1 is required for SIDs and STARs; aircraft not equipped must revert to RNAV 1 (DME/DME/IRU) or conventional SIDs. TD DME is critical; when unserviceable, RNAV 1 reversion is suspended. Climb gradients are specified for each runway and SID group (e.g., RWY 07R RNP 1: 4.8% or 292 ft/NM until 1200 ft; RWY 07C via TEGUB: 6.8% or 414 ft/NM until 3900 ft). STARs commence from ABBEY, BETTY, CANTO, or SIERA and terminate at IAFs (RIVMI/LUDLA for RWY 25, LIMES for RWY 07). ILS categories: RWY 25R CAT I, RWY 07R/25L/07C CAT II, RWY 07L/25C CAT III. RWY 25R ILS approach requires RNAV transition only; do not select ILS mode before TOPUN. RNP AR APCH procedures require special authorization from Hong Kong CAD. Loss of communication procedures: comply with last acknowledged clearance, then follow SID/STAR to holding pattern at 4,500 ft and execute ILS approach.
