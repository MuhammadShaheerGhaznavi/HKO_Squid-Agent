## Content
The AMO provides a meteorological watch service for the Hong Kong FIR, issuing SIGMETs for specified en-route phenomena: obscured/embedded/frequent thunderstorms with/without hail, squall lines, tropical cyclones with 10-minute mean surface wind ≥34 kt, severe turbulence, severe icing, severe icing due to freezing rain, severe mountain wave, heavy sandstorm, heavy duststorm, volcanic ash, and radioactive cloud. SIGMETs are valid up to 6 hours, disseminated via AFTN and VOLMET. Aerodrome warnings for HKIA are issued for: tropical cyclone (mean wind ≥34 kt), thunderstorm, hail, snow, freezing precipitation, hoar frost or rime, sandstorm, duststorm, strong surface wind (mean >21 kt or gusts >31 kt), squall, frost, volcanic ash, tsunami, volcanic ash deposition, toxic chemicals, and tornado/waterspout. Warnings are in abbreviated plain language and plain language.
