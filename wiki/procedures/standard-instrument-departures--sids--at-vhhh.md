## Content
VHHH has numerous SIDs, many of which are RNAV procedures. For example, RNAV BEKOL SIDs are available for runways 07R, 25L, 07C, 25C, 07L, and 25R, with some requiring RF capability (07R, 07C, 07L). Similarly, RNAV LEKEN, DALOL, and PECAN SIDs are available for all runways, with RF capability required for certain runways. There are also conventional SIDs like RAMEN for runways 07R, 07C, 07L, and RUMSY for runways 25L, 25C, 25R. Other SIDs include ADLAD, LARIT, DUMEP, XEMEK, and SOSLU for runway 07C. Each SID is documented with a specific chart identifier.
