## Radio Communication Failure Procedures in Hong Kong

In case of radio communication failure, aircraft must comply with specific procedures.

### Arriving Aircraft
- **If in VMC:** Continue in VMC and land at the nearest suitable aerodrome.
- **If in IMC:** Proceed according to the STAR to the termination point (LIMES for RWY 07L/07C/07R; RIVMI or LUDLA for RWY 25L/25C/25R), descend per published profile, then enter holding pattern and carry out appropriate ILS or RNP AR approach.
  - **RWY 07:** Enter LIMES holding, descend to 4,500 ft, ILS approach.
  - **RWY 25 via RIVMI:** Enter RIVMI holding, descend to 4,500 ft, ILS approach.
  - **RWY 25 via LUDLA:** Enter LUDLA holding, descend to 4,500 ft, RNP AR approach.
- Arrive over the approach facility as close as possible to ETA.
- **If being radar vectored:** If no transmissions for one minute, signal check; if failure, comply with above, climb to minimum sector altitude if below.

### Departing Aircraft
- Comply with last acknowledged clearance up to next reporting point, then climb to flight planned cruising level and follow SID/TTR to TMA boundary.

### Overflying Aircraft
- **If in VMC:** Land at nearest suitable aerodrome.
- **If in IMC:** Maintain last assigned speed and level for 20 minutes after failure to report at compulsory reporting point, then adjust per flight plan.

### Departing/Overflying under Vectoring
- Maintain last assigned speed and level for 7 minutes after last assigned level reached, transponder set to 7600, or failure to report, whichever later.

### General Actions
- Set transponder to Mode C code 7600.
- In RVSM airspace, ATC considers aircraft non-RVSM compliant and applies 2,000 ft vertical separation.
- For receiver or transmitter failure only, transmit intentions or maintain watch accordingly.

### ATC Actions
- Assume two-way communication failure, maintain separation, transmit information, use surveillance systems, inform operator, and after 30 minutes after last acknowledged EAT/ETA or computed ETA, initiate alerting action. Period reduced if position known or aircraft landed.

## Weather Deviation Procedures in Hong Kong FIR

Weather deviation procedures apply in South China Sea airspace, especially outside VHF coverage.

### Communication
- Pilots should obtain ATC clearance at the earliest possible time; if unable, broadcast position and intentions on the frequency in use and 121.5 MHz.
- Priority can be requested by stating 'WEATHER DEVIATION IS REQUIRED' or using 'PAN, PAN' three times.
- When communications are established, ATC will issue clearance if no conflicting traffic or if separation can be established; otherwise, they will advise of conflicting traffic and request intentions.
- If no clearance is available, pilots may deviate from track, broadcasting on 121.5 MHz and 123.45 MHz, watching for traffic, turning on exterior lights.

### Deviation Procedures
- For deviations less than 10 NM: maintain assigned level.
- For deviations greater than 10 NM: initiate level changes based on track:
  - **Eastbound (000°M-179°M):** left deviation descend 300 ft, right climb 300 ft.
  - **Westbound (180°M-359°M):** left climb 300 ft, right descend 300 ft.
- When returning to track, be established at assigned level within 10 NM of centreline.
