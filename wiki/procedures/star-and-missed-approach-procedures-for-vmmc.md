## Content
For arriving flights, Hong Kong ATC provides ATS surveillance service during approach and missed approach while transiting Hong Kong airspace. Speed restrictions for instrument approach procedures: maximum approach turning speed 190 KIAS, maximum missed approach turning speed 185 KIAS. In case of loss of communication, aircraft shall comply with the STAR procedure and join the approach feed-in procedure for the notified runway. Missed approach procedures: For RWY 34, comply with the published procedure to establish inbound to Hong Kong airspace via MC411 or on track 080°M, climbing to 6,000 ft or as instructed by ATC, then continue or expect radar vector to HAZEL. For RWY 16, climb to 4,000 ft on MCU RDL 344 and depart MCU DVOR on RDL 171 to MCU DME 7.4 NM (ZUH RDL 148/DME 15NM); if MCU DVOR/DME unavailable, climb to 4,000 ft on ZAO RDL 181, then expect further climb and vectors to cross INDUS at 1,800 m. STAR significant points include CHALI, HAZEL, INDUS, MC513, MC601, MC611, RUNLI, and SMT, with navaids SMT DVOR/DME (114.8 MHz, CH 95X), TD DVOR/DME, MCU DVOR/DME, and ZUH VOR/DME (116.7 MHz, CH 114X).
