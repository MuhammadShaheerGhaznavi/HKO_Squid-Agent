## Content
All operators of international flights must employ a recognised ground handling agent. Taxiing regulations are in Rule 33 of Schedule 14 to the Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995. Overtaking moving aircraft is prohibited. Aircraft taxiing must follow nose-wheel guide lines on aprons. Marshalling provided only at stands without Safegate system. Aircraft with radio must contact 'Hong Kong Ground' before manoeuvring. If unable to establish radio contact, parked aircraft must arrange 'Follow Me' escort; landing aircraft must vacate runway and await 'Follow Me'. Persons qualified to taxi: licensed pilot, qualified ground engineer (with certificate), or pupil under instruction. Towing requires tractor driver with valid Airport Restricted Area Permit, driving licence, Aircraft Towing Runway Crossing (ATRC) certificate, and written approvals. Engine ground runs: at ground idle power, max two engines, max 10 minutes on Passenger/Cargo Apron; above idle power must be in engine run-up facility; longer runs in approved locations. All runs supervised. Requests to Apron Control Centre (tel. 2910 1112). Start-up clearance on 121.775 MHz, listening watch maintained, anti-collision beacons on.
