## Content
Hong Kong ATFMU targets distributing CTOT at least 90 minutes before EOBT. For airport constraints (HKIA & MIA), CTOT compliance window is -5/+10 minutes. For waypoint/airspace constraints, window is -5/+5 minutes. ATC releases departures as close as possible to CTOT. Operators must file FPLs at least 3 hours before EOBT to secure CTOT; late filing may cause delay. See [[ATFM Measures]] and [[Coordination during GDP]].
