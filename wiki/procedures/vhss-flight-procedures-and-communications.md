## Content
Traffic circuit at VHSS shall always be over water to the north of the heliport, with downwind leg minimum 800 m off-shore. Final approach tracks are 130°M or 260°M. Missed approach from 130°M: turn left to track 080°M, climb to 500 ft, then left to downwind 285°M, then left to reposition. Missed approach from 260°M: turn right to track 310°M, climb to 500 ft, then right to downwind 105°M, then right to reposition. Departure tracks are 080°M or 310°M. Communications: heliport is within Hong Kong International Airport Control Zone; helicopters must maintain two-way contact with Hong Kong Zone Control on 120.6 MHz primary and 122.075 MHz secondary. Inbound helicopters shall contact Sky Shuttle Heliport Radio (STAR) on 131.0 MHz prior to arrival. ATS communication facilities: FOCC (STAR) 131.0 MHz (00:00-15:30), ZNC (Zone Control) 120.6 MHz H24, 122.075 MHz secondary.
