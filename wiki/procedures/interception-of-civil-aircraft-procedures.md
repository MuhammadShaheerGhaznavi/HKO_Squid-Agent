## Content
When an aircraft is intercepted, the pilot must comply with ICAO provisions. The intercepted aircraft shall immediately: follow instructions from the intercepting aircraft, interpreting visual signals; notify the appropriate ATS unit if possible; attempt radio communication on 121.5 MHz (and 243 MHz if no contact) with identity and nature of flight; if SSR transponder equipped, select Mode A Code 7700 unless otherwise instructed; if ADS-B or ADS-C equipped, select appropriate emergency functionality. If radio instructions conflict with visual signals, request clarification while complying with visual instructions. If radio instructions conflict with other radio instructions, request clarification while complying with the intercepting aircraft's radio instructions. Radio communication phrases for intercepting aircraft include: CALL SIGN (KOL-SA-IN) meaning 'What is your call sign?', FOLLOW (FOL-LO) 'Follow me', DESCEND (DEE-SEND) 'Descend for landing', YOU LAND (YOU LAAND) 'Land at this aerodrome', PROCEED (PRO-SEED) 'You may proceed'. Phrases for intercepted aircraft: CALL SIGN (KOL-SA-IN) 'My call sign is (call sign)', WILCO (VILL-KO) 'Understood', CAN NOT (KANN NOTT) 'Unable to comply', REPEAT (REE-PEET) 'Repeat your instruction', AM LOST (AM LOSST) 'Position unknown', MAYDAY (MAYDAY) 'I am in distress', HIJACK (HI-JACK) 'I have been hijacked', LAND (place name) (LAAND) 'I request to land at (place name)', DESCEND (DEE-SEND) 'I require descent'. Visual signals: Series 1 - intercepting aircraft rocks wings and flashes lights, intercepted aircraft rocks wings to acknowledge; Series 2 - abrupt breakaway means 'You may proceed', intercepted rocks wings; Series 3 - lowering gear and landing lights means 'Land at this aerodrome', intercepted lowers gear and follows; Series 4 - intercepted raises gear and flashes lights over runway means 'Aerodrome inadequate', intercepting responds with Series 1 or 2; Series 5 - regular switching of lights means 'Cannot comply', intercepting responds with Series 2; Series 6 - irregular flashing means 'In distress', intercepting responds with Series 2.
