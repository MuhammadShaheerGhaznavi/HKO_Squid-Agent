## Content
Slot allocation has 3 phases: request submission, initial allocation, distribution. PIC must ensure aircraft ready to taxi to meet CTOT. CTOT integrated into A-CDM at HKIA. ANSPs give priority to flights with slots. CTOT included in initial ATC clearance. Coordination: Bangkok ATFMU (VTBBZDZX) included in AFTN for NOTAMs and ATS messages. If slot will be missed, obtain new slot via operator or ATC. PIC includes CTOT in clearance request. For HKIA departures, be ready 25 minutes before CTOT (excluding 5-min buffer). ATC may delay pushback if taxi time less than STT. See [[BOBCAT ATFM]].
