## Content
Hong Kong ATFMU issues ATFM Notices via email to local stakeholders, providing detailed information on special events, ATFM measures, and operational impacts. The notice includes event description, expected measures, and other relevant info. Stakeholders can join recipient list by emailing hkatfmu@cad.gov.hk. See [[Hong Kong ATFMU]].
