## Content
Hong Kong ATFMU issues a special notice via email called **Hong Kong ATFM Notice** to timely promulgate essential ATFM-related information on special events/activities to local stakeholders. The notice provides detailed information on special events, relevant ATFM measures, and other information impacting flight operations, airport operators, ATC, ground handling agents, etc. A sample notice is shown in the AIP. Local stakeholders can be added to the recipient list by emailing hkatfmu@cad.gov.hk. See [[Hong Kong ATFMU]] and [[ATFM Overview]].
