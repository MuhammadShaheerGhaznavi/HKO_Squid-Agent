## Content
Only SID procedures for Macao International Airport that transit Hong Kong airspace are published in AIP Hong Kong. Hong Kong ATC provides ATS surveillance service to departing aircraft while transiting Hong Kong airspace. Pilots shall comply with speed control restrictions published in the SIDs; if unable, they should inform ATC as soon as possible. In case of loss of communication, aircraft shall comply with the last acknowledged clearance up to the next reporting point in the SID/ Terminal Transition Route procedure, then climb to the flight planned cruising level and follow the SID/ Terminal Transition Route track to the Hong Kong TMA exit point. SID navigation aids include MCU DVOR/DME (116.4 MHz, CH 111X), TD DVOR/DME (116.1 MHz, CH 108X), U NDB (272 kHz), ZAO DVOR/DME (117.2 MHz, CH 119X). Significant points include ALLEY, CONGA, DAKIG, DALOL, DUMEP, LARIT, LEKEN, LUBMO, LUKBU, MC311, MC312, MC313, MC314, MC315, MC411, MC501, MEBKI, MULET, NUDPI, PAPA, PECAN, SEGPO, SKATE, SOSLU, SOUSA, SUDVA, VEDVO, XEMEK, with coordinates and cross references as listed in the AIP.
