## Content
The Record of AIP Amendments (GEN 0.2) lists amendments with publication and effective dates. Recent amendments include 1/25 (pub 28 NOV 2024, eff 23 JAN 2025) through 7/26 (AIRAC, pub 14 MAY 2026, eff 9 JUL 2026).
