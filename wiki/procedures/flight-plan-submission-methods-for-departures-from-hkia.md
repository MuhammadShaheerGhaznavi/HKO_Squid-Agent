## Content
For flights departing HKIA, flight plans can be filed via: (a) Private Communication Network, (b) AFTN (for scheduled flight operations only), or (c) Flight Plan Form DCA6a. The Private Communication Network is the most effective and widely used; it provides an online form mimicking ICAO format, with default address VHHHZPZA, an 'AD/' field for up to 40 AFTN addresses (VHHHZPZA not allowed), and Field 19 for supplementary info (not transmitted normally, but available as SPL on request). The system performs validity checks: syntactic on alpha/digit fields and time format, semantic on aircraft type and location indicators, syntactic on route per ICAO Doc 4444 Appendix 2, checking of AFTN addresses, and sorting of Field 18 entries. AFTN filing is only for scheduled flights, addressed to VHHHZPZA, with 'AD' field at beginning, max 7 addressees per line, and Field 19 not required but must be available. Overseas AFTN addresses must be registered with AIMC (email bo@cad.gov.hk) and must adhere to specific requirements. Flight Plan Form DCA6a must be printed legibly, no manuscript entries, and submitted by hand or fax to 2910 1180. See [[Flight Plan Submission Requirements]].
