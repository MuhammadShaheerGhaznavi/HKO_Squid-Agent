## Content
The Hong Kong FIR requires strict adherence to route syntax for flight plans. For arrivals at HKIA, specific inbound routes are prescribed, e.g., via A470 DOTMI V512 ABBEY, M503 LELIM V591 ABBEY, A1/G581 ELATO V522 ABBEY, M501/A461 NOMAN V533 BETTY, A583 SABNO V542 BETTY, M772 ASOBA M772 DULOP Q1 CARSO V551 BETTY, M771 DOSUT M771 DULOP Q1 CARSO V551 BETTY, A1 IKELA P901 IDOSI V561 CANTO, R339/A202 SIKOU V571 CANTO, and DCT routes from ALLEY or FOXTROT to CANTO. Connecting routes from entry points are also specified, e.g., BEKOL A461, LEKEN V601 DOTMI A470, LEKEN V611 LELIM M503, DALOL V621 ELATO A1/G581, DALOL V631 ENVAR M750, DALOL V642 NOMAN A461/M501, DALOL V642 SKATE DCT KAPLI G86, DALOL V652 SABNO A583, PECAN V10 SIKOU R339/A202, PECAN V11 IDOSI A1 IKELA, PECAN V12 EPDOS L642, PECAN DCT CHALI, PECAN DCT FOXTROT. For Macao arrivals transiting Hong Kong FIR, routes include A470 DOTMI DCT SAMMI J101 SMT, A1/G581 ELATO J101 SMT, A583 SABNO DCT TOFEE DCT SUKER DCT ALDOM J103 ROBIN DCT CHALI, M772 ASOBA M772 DULOP M771 DUMOL J103 ROBIN DCT CHALI, M771 DOSUT M771 DUMOL J103 ROBIN DCT CHALI, A1 IKELA P901 IDOSI DCT DASON J104 CHALI, R339/A202 SIKOU J104 CHALI. For Guangzhou/Shenzhen arrivals, routes include A1/G581 ELATO J101 SMT DCT TAMOT (ZGGG), A583 SABNO DCT TOFEE DCT SUKER DCT ALDOM J103 SAPAX DCT BIGEX B330 TAMOT, M772 ASOBA M772 DULOP M771 DUMOL J103 SAPAX DCT BIGEX B330 TAMOT, M771 DOSUT M771 DUMOL J103 SAPAX DCT BIGEX B330 TAMOT, A1 IKELA DCT MORTU DCT ALDOM J103 SAPAX DCT BIGEX B330 TAMOT, A202/R339 SIKOU J104 CHALI DCT SAPAX DCT BIGEX B330 TAMOT. Approval from ATS Supervisor is required for routes not listed. Operators must not include SID/STAR procedures in flight plans; instead use Terminal Transition Routes. Specific altitude constraints apply, e.g., cross ENPET at FL260, SONNY at FL260, CYBER at FL260, MAPLE at FL260, NEDLE at FL230, ISBAN at FL200/FL260, CHALI at FL110/FL260, COTON at FL120, GOBBI at FL110. Route via P901 at FL290 or above, A1 at FL280 or below, with RNP10 compliance for FL290+. See [[Flight Plan Submission Requirements]].
