## Content
Flights requesting exemption from ATFM measures for reasons other than automatic exemptions (SAR, HEAD, STATE, HUM, HOSP, MEDEVAC, and emergency) must obtain prior approval from appropriate ATS authorities and indicate 'STS/ATFMX' in item 18 of the flight plan. A single ICAO designator for automatic exemption is sufficient; e.g., 'STS/SAR' not 'STS/SAR ATFMX'. A remark should be included in RMK/ for which FIR(s) the ATFMX exemption applies. If authorization is not obtained, ATFMX cannot be used; instead, a remark should be included in RMK/. See [[ATFM Measures]] and [[Flight Planning]].
