## Content
Weather observers at the AMO are on duty H24, making routine observations at half-hourly intervals. Reports are issued as local routine reports (for aerodrome dissemination) and METAR (for beyond the aerodrome). Special observations are made when criteria in ICAO PANS-MET (Doc 10157) para. 2.1.2 are met, including when crosswind components change through 20 kt. Special reports are issued as local special reports and SPECI. The same criteria apply for both, covering mean surface direction, mean surface wind speed, gusts, prevailing visibility, RVR, onset/cessation/change in intensity of weather phenomena, height of base of lowest cloud layer, and amount of cloud below 1,500 ft. Detailed criteria are on AMIDS. Observations are representative of conditions within 8 km of the aerodrome reference point for local reports, and within 16 km for certain phenomena in METAR. Observing systems include anemometers, ceilometers, RVR transmissometers, forward scatterers, TDWRs, LIDARs, and wind profilers, with locations shown in GEN 3.5-HKO-EQ.
