## Content
Weather observers at the AMO are on duty H24, making routine observations at half-hourly intervals, issued as local routine reports (for aerodrome dissemination) and METAR (for beyond aerodrome). Special observations are made when criteria in ICAO PANS-MET (Doc 10157) para 2.1.2 are met, including when crosswind components change through 20 kt. Special reports are issued as local special reports and SPECI. Criteria cover mean surface wind direction/speed, gusts, prevailing visibility, RVR, onset/cessation/change in intensity of weather phenomena, height of lowest cloud base, and cloud amount below 1,500 ft. Detailed criteria are on AMIDS. Observations are representative of conditions within ~8 km of the aerodrome reference point for local reports, and within ~16 km for certain phenomena in METAR. Observing systems include anemometers, ceilometers, RVR transmissometers, forward scatterers, TDWR, LIDARs, and weather buoys, as shown in GEN 3.5-HKO-EQ.
