## Content
Three sets of anemometers are installed near each runway: south runway (RWY 07R TDZ, 07R/25L MID, 25L TDZ), centre runway (07C TDZ, 07C/25C MID, 25C TDZ), and north runway (07L TDZ, 07L/25R MID, 25R TDZ). The official wind reports are based on the anemometer near RWY 07L/25R MID RWY. If this anemometer is unavailable, wind information is substituted from RWY 25R TDZ, RWY 07L TDZ, RWY 25L TDZ, RWY 07R TDZ, or RWY 07R/25L MID RWY, in that order. Winds in aerodrome forecasts refer to the official location. The anemometers are part of a network that includes additional sites at aprons and hilltops, as listed in GEN 3.5-HKO-EQ.
