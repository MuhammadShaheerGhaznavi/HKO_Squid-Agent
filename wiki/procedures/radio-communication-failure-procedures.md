## Content
In the event of radio communication failure, aircraft must comply with specific procedures. Arriving aircraft: if in VMC, land at nearest suitable aerodrome; if in IMC, follow STAR to termination point (LIMES for RWY 07, RIVMI or LUDLA for RWY 25), descend to 4,500 ft, and carry out appropriate ILS or RNP AR approach. For RWY 07, enter LIMES holding; for RWY 25 via RIVMI, enter RIVMI holding; via LUDLA, enter LUDLA holding. Arrivals should arrive over approach facility as close as possible to ETA. Information on landing runway may be obtained from ATIS, D-ATIS, ACARS, satellite phone; ILS and approach lighting for runway in use will be switched on. Arriving aircraft under vectoring: if no transmissions heard for one minute, make signal check; if failure, comply with procedures, climb to minimum sector altitude if below. Departing aircraft: comply with last acknowledged clearance up to next reporting point, then climb to flight planned cruising level and follow SID/TTR to TMA boundary. Overflying aircraft: if in VMC, land at nearest suitable aerodrome; if in IMC, maintain last assigned speed and level for 20 minutes after failure to report at compulsory reporting point, then adjust per flight plan. Departing or overflying under vectoring: maintain last assigned speed and level for 7 minutes after last assigned level reached, transponder set to 7600, or failure to report, whichever is later. SSR code 7600 indicates radio communication failure. In RVSM airspace, ATC will consider aircraft non-RVSM compliant and apply 2,000 ft vertical separation. For receiver or transmitter failure only, transmit intentions or maintain watch accordingly. ATC actions include maintaining separation, transmitting information, and initiating alerting action after 30 minutes if no report.
