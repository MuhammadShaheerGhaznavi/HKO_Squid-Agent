## Content
The LSWDCP is applied by Fukuoka ATMC, Hong Kong ATCC, Ho Chi Minh, Kota Kinabalu, Manila, Naha, Sanya, Singapore, and Taipei ACCs. When activated, a LSWDCP Flight Level Allocation Scheme is applied within RVSM airspace on affected routes in Hong Kong FIR. The scheme assigns: L642 (southwest bound): FL320, FL360, FL400; M771 (northeast bound): FL310, FL350, FL390. Hong Kong ATCC issues a NOTAM when activating the LSWDCP FLAS.
