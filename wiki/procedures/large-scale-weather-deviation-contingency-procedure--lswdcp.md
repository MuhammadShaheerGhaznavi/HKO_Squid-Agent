## Content
The Large Scale Weather Deviation Contingency Procedure (LSWDCP) is applied by Fukuoka ATMC, Hong Kong ATCC, Ho Chi Minh, Kota Kinabalu, Manila, Naha, Sanya, Singapore, and Taipei ACCs. When implemented, a LSWDCP Flight Level Allocation Scheme (FLAS) is applied within RVSM airspace on affected routes in Hong Kong FIR: L642 (southwest bound) uses FL320, FL360, FL400; M771 (northeast bound) uses FL310, FL350, FL390. Hong Kong ATCC issues a NOTAM when activating the LSWDCP FLAS.
