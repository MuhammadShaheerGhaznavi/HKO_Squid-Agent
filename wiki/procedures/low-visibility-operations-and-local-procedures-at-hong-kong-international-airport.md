## Low Visibility Operations (LVO)

Low Visibility Procedures (LVP) are established for visibility less than RVR 550 m or cloud ceiling less than 200 ft. When LVP are in force, pilots are advised via ATIS. CAT III approaches are available on request.

### ILS Equipment
- RWY 07R, 07C, and 25L have CAT II ILS.
- RWY 07L and 25C have CAT III ILS.

### Holding Positions
- **RWY 07R/25L**: TWY J1, J2, J10, J11 are combined CAT I/II; TWY K for 25L is combined CAT I/II; separate CAT I and II on K1 and K for 07R.
- **RWY 07C/25C**: TWY A1, A2 combined CAT I/II; A11, A12 combined CAT I/II/III; separate CAT I and II on F1/E and F2/F for 07C; separate CAT I and II/III on F7/F and F8/E for 25C.
- **RWY 07L/25R**: TWY C1, C2 combined CAT I/II/III; C11, C12 CAT I.

Pilots must delay 'runway vacated' call until completely vacated ILS sensitive area. ATC may use A-SMGCS to confirm runway vacated. Practice CAT II/III approaches are allowed but without full safeguarding.

## Runway Utilisation and Reduced Runway Separation Minima (RRSM)

To maximise runway utilisation, pilots should enter runway without delay, commence take-off roll as soon as clearance issued, and vacate runway quickly via first available Rapid Exit Taxiway (RET). RETILs are installed at TWYs C4, C5, C6, C7, C8, C9, A3, A5, A6, A7, A8, A10, F3, F4, F5, F6, K3, K5, displaying distance information at 300m, 200m, and 100m.

RRSM may be applied between a departing and succeeding landing aircraft or between two successive landings, provided:
- Visibility at least 5 km
- Cloud ceiling at 3000 ft or more
- Daylight hours (30 min after sunrise to 30 min before sunset)
- Second aircraft can see first clearly
- No unfavourable wind, wet runway, or poor braking

Separation distances when landing aircraft crosses threshold:
- **RWY 07R/25L**: Departing aircraft airborne and passed point at least 2900 m from threshold (abeam TWY K6 for 07R or K2 for 25L); preceding landing aircraft passed point at least 2900 m and vacating without backtracking.
- **RWY 07C/25C**: 2400 m (abeam TWY F6/A8 for 07C or F3 for 25C).
- **RWY 07L/25R**: 2400 m (abeam TWY C8 for 07L or C5 for 25R).

ATC will provide warning phraseology.

## Local Traffic Regulations

### General
- IFR flights: file CAD Flight Plan form DCA6a at least 60 minutes before EOBT.
- VFR flights: file CAD VFR Local Flight Notification Form DCA6b at least 20 minutes before ETD.
- Aircraft must maintain two-way communication with ATS.
- Helicopters departing/landing outside HKIA must contact ATC immediately after lift-off or prior to descent.
- Prior approval needed for deviations from published procedures.
- Foreign military helicopters require advance approval from Director-General of Civil Aviation, flight plan approved by ATC Watch Manager, and area restricted to sanctioned area.

### At HKIA
- Training/test/demonstration flights require prior approval from DGCA; training flights approved only in exceptional circumstances.
- Non-commercial fixed wing and helicopter flights require prior approval; helicopters also need AAHK approval.
- Recreational flights not normally permitted.
- Final approval on day of flight from Aerodrome Control Supervisor (Tel: 2910 6822).

### Helicopter Operations
- Categories: Technical Flight (Maintenance Apron), Flights handled by Hong Kong Business Aviation Centre (Stands X1, X2, X456, X458), CASEVAC (as instructed by ATC).

### ATZ/CTR
- Class C airspace. ATC clearance required for local flying.
- Fixed wing flights in ATZ restricted to joining/leaving traffic circuit.
- VFR weather minima: visibility 5 km, ceiling 1,500 ft.

### UCARAs
- Class G with two-way radio requirement.
- Flight notification to Hong Kong Police for North Border (Tel 2860 2400).
- Squawk discrete SSR codes; if FIS unavailable, squawk 5200.
- Unlicensed pilots restricted to Port Shelter and parts of Tolo and New Town south of 'line a'.

## Helicopter Procedures

### Departure from GFS/BAC
- Kilo East Departure: lift off from TWY K, turn south between TXL Q3 and Fire Station, follow coastline to abeam western roundabout on Chun Wan Road.
- From abeam western roundabout, keep 1,000 m south of runway extended centreline, track to Silvermine or Pak Mong at ≤1,500 ft AMSL.
- Report passing AFFC during Special VFR.

### Arrival
- Follow reciprocal to AFFC at ≤1,500 ft, then ATC instructions.

### Helicopter Landing Locations
- Junction of TWY H and H2: for Maintenance Apron, Stands X456, X458.
- Junction of TWY K and TXL Q3: for BAC Apron, Stands X1, X2.
- Direct approaches not permitted; do not overfly parked aircraft or buildings.

### Holding Procedures
- Mount Rooster: ≤500 ft; Tai O: ≤1,000 ft; Sham Shek: ≤1,000 ft (VFR only); Pak Mong: ≤1,000 ft; Tung Chung Bay: 800-1,000 ft (VFR only).
- For runway crossing: WHISKEY, ECHO (max 1,000 ft, remain 1,000 m north of north runway); West Sea Berth, Expo (800-1,000 ft, 600m x 1,800m area, remain 1,000 m from extended centrelines); Sha Lo Wan (≤1,000 ft, remain 1,000 m south of south runway); Cathay City (≤800 ft VFR, ≤500 ft SVFR, remain 1,000 m south of south runway).
- Holding patterns: right hand orbits, speed ≤70 KIAS.

### Runway Crossing
- Two corridors: Runway 25 Crossing Corridor (between Holding Point ECHO and abeam Cathay City) and Runway 07 Crossing Corridor (between Holding Point WHISKEY and Sha Lo Wan).
- Recommended altitude 800-1,000 ft AMSL.
- Conditional crossing clearances issued; adjust speed to cross after landing traffic.
