## Content
The GEN 2.7 section provides sunrise/sunset tables for Hong Kong International Airport (VHHH) at coordinates 22°18'32"N 113°54'53"E. The tables are prepared by the Hong Kong Observatory and give times in Hong Kong Time (UTC+8) for the beginning of civil morning twilight (TWIL FROM), sunrise (SR), sunset (SS), and end of civil evening twilight (TWIL TO) for each day of the year 2025. This year is used as an 'average year' for the period 2024-2026, with deviations of less than 2 minutes. The times are calculated for a Sun altitude of 6° below the horizon for twilight. Example times: On January 1, twilight begins at 6:40, sunrise at 7:04, sunset at 17:52, and twilight ends at 18:16. On June 21, twilight begins at 5:15, sunrise at 5:40, sunset at 19:11, and twilight ends at 19:36. These tables are essential for planning flights, especially for VFR operations and airport lighting schedules.
