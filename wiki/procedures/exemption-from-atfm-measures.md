## Content
Flights requesting exemption for reasons other than automatic (SAR, HEAD, STATE, HUM, HOSP, MEDEVAC, emergency) must obtain prior approval and file STS/ATFMX in item 18. Single designator is sufficient (e.g., STS/SAR not STS/SAR ATFMX). Include RMK/ for applicable FIRs. Without authorization, ATFMX cannot be used; use RMK/ instead. See [[ATFM Measures]].
