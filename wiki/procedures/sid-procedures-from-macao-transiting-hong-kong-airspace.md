## Content
SID procedures for Macao International Airport that transit Hong Kong airspace are published in AIP Hong Kong. Hong Kong ATC provides ATS surveillance service to departing aircraft while they are in Hong Kong airspace. Pilots must comply with speed restrictions published in the SIDs; if unable, they should inform ATC as soon as possible. In case of loss of communication, aircraft shall comply with the last acknowledged clearance up to the next reporting point in the SID/Terminal Transition Route, then climb to the flight planned cruising level and follow the SID/Terminal Transition Route track to the Hong Kong TMA exit point. SID navigation aids include MCU DVOR/DME (116.4 MHz, CH 111X), TD DVOR/DME (116.1 MHz, CH 108X), U NDB (272 kHz), and ZAO DVOR/DME (117.2 MHz, CH 119X). Significant points include ALLEY, CONGA, DAKIG, DALOL, DUMEP, LARIT, LEKEN, LUBMO, LUKBU, MC311-MC315, MC411, MC501, MEBKI, MULET, NUDPI, PAPA, PECAN, SEGPO, SKATE, SOSLU, SOUSA, SUDVA, VEDVO, and XEMEK, with coordinates and cross-references to navaids.
