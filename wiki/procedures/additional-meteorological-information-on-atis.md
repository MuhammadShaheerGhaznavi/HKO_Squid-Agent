## Content
ATIS broadcasts include: Hilltop winds when 2-minute mean wind at ~2,500 ft AMSL on Yi Tung Shan (primary) or Nei Lak Shan (backup) exceeds 35 kt, e.g., '2500 FT ESTIMATED WIND 160 DEG 40 KT'. Prevailing visibility is included when any RVR is <1,500 m or prevailing visibility <3,000 m. Thunderstorm activity affecting approach, departure, or missed approach areas within one hour is broadcast, with distance, bearing, and movement if possible, e.g., 'Thunderstorm 20 NM southwest moving northeast'. Significant tailwind on base leg for runway 07 operations is included when reported tailwind ≥20 kt and not anticipated from surface wind, e.g., 'Expect significant tailwind on base leg'.
