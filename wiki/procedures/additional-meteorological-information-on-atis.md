## Content
ATIS broadcasts at HKIA include additional meteorological information: Hilltop winds are given when the 2-minute mean wind at approximately 2,500 ft AMSL on Yi Tung Shan (primary) or Nei Lak Shan (backup) exceeds 35 kt, e.g., '2500 FT ESTIMATED WIND 160 DEG 40 KT'. Prevailing visibility is included when any RVR is below 1,500 m or prevailing visibility is below 3,000 m. Thunderstorm activity affecting approach, departure, or missed approach areas within one hour is included, with distance, bearing, and movement if possible, e.g., 'Thunderstorm 20 NM southwest moving northeast'. Significant tailwind on base leg for runway 07 operations is included when reported tailwind is ≥20 kt but not anticipated from surface wind, e.g., 'Expect significant tailwind on base leg'. These are provided on Arrival and Departure ATIS as appropriate.
