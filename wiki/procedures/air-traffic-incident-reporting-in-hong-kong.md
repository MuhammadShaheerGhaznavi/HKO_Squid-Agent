## Content
Air traffic incidents in Hong Kong are defined as occurrences, other than accidents, associated with aircraft operation that affect or could affect safety. They include non-adherence to ATC instructions, radio communication errors, loss of separation, runway incursions, TCAS RA activations, and GPWS/TAWS activations. Pilots must file initial reports immediately by radio to ATC, or after landing by phone if unable. Formal reports use specific forms: DCA 201 (Occurrence Report), DCA 232 (AIRPROX for loss of separation), DCA 235 (Runway Incursion), and DCA 237 (Wake Turbulence). Forms are available on the CAD website. Investigations aim to enhance safety, not apportion blame, and reports are handled confidentially. Voluntary reporting of non-mandatory incidents is encouraged via DCA 234. Bird strikes are reported to the Airport Authority Hong Kong (Tel: +852 2183 6107) or via HKIA Extranet. Windshear and turbulence reports follow GEN 3.5 procedures and are submitted to the Airport Meteorological Office or via HKO website. Wake vortex reports can be submitted via DCA 237 or online directly to ICAO. Contact for ATC safety office: atmd_safety@cad.gov.hk.
