## Content
Air traffic incidents in Hong Kong are defined as occurrences, other than accidents, associated with aircraft operation that affect or could affect safety. They include non-adherence to ATC instructions, radio communication errors, loss of separation, runway incursions, TCAS RA activations, and GPWS/TAWS activations. Pilots must file reports using specific forms: DCA 201 (Occurrence Report Form) for most incidents, DCA 232 (AIRPROX Report - Pilots) for loss of separation, DCA 235 (Runway Incursion Reporting) for runway incursions, and DCA 237 (Wake Turbulence Reporting Form) for wake turbulence encounters. Initial reports should be made immediately by radio to ATC, or after landing by telephone if not possible. Completed forms are sent to contacts listed on the forms, and reporters may contact the ATC safety office at atmd_safety@cad.gov.hk. Investigations aim to enhance safety, not to apportion blame, and all reports are handled in strict confidence. Voluntary reporting of incidents not required to be reported is encouraged via DCA 234. Bird strikes are reported to the Airfield Operations Department at Hong Kong International Airport (Tel: +852 2183 6107) or via HKIA Extranet. Windshear and turbulence encounters are reported per GEN 3.5, with forms from the HKO website. Wake vortex encounters can be reported via DCA 237 or online directly to ICAO.
