## Air Services Operating Permits in Hong Kong

Operating permits are required for both scheduled and non-scheduled air services in Hong Kong, issued by the Director-General of Civil Aviation under the Air Transport (Licensing of Air Services) Regulations (Cap. 448 sub. leg. A).

### Scheduled Air Services
- **Applicability**: Non-Hong Kong registered aircraft operating scheduled air services.
- **Application**: Must be submitted via the online E-filing system at least **one month** before commencement.
- **Required Documents**: General Declaration and Cargo Manifest, with copies for Customs (3) and Immigration (1).
- **Checklist**: Available at [http://www.cad.gov.hk/application/checklist_scheduled_service.zip](http://www.cad.gov.hk/application/checklist_scheduled_service.zip).

### Non-Scheduled Air Services
- **Applicability**: Non-scheduled air services for hire or reward.
- **Application**: Must be submitted at least **3 working days** before arrival/departure; new operators should apply **2 weeks** in advance.
- **Note**: Applications for both passengers and cargo are not normally considered.
- **Grant Criteria**: Permits are granted if scheduled services cannot meet genuine demand, and reciprocity is considered for foreign airlines.
- **Conditions**: No advertising to the general public (except domestic). Certain passengers/cargo may be carried free: infants under 2, directors/employees of permit-holder, tour conductors (1 per 15 persons), cargo for permit-holder's use, authorized aeronautical representatives, and security guards on cargo flights.
