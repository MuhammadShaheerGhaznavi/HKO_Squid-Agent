## Local Flight Regulations

- **Compliance**: Local flights in Hong Kong must comply with ICAO Annex 2 and the Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995.
- **Flight Notification**:
  - IFR flights: file CAD Flight Plan form DCA6a at least 60 minutes before EOBT.
  - VFR flights: file DCA6b at least 20 minutes before ETD.
- **Communication**: Aircraft must maintain two-way communication with ATS.
- **Helicopters Outside HKIA**: Helicopters departing/landing outside HKIA must contact ATC immediately after lift-off or prior to descent.
- **Foreign Military Helicopters**: Require advance approval from the Director-General of Civil Aviation.
- **Approvals at HKIA**: Training, test, or demonstration flights require prior approval; non-commercial fixed-wing and helicopter flights also require approval.
- **Helicopter Categories**:
  - Technical Flight (Maintenance Apron)
  - Flights handled by Hong Kong Business Aviation Centre (Stands X1, X2, X456, X458)
  - CASEVAC (as instructed by ATC)
- **VFR Weather Minima**: Visibility at least 5 km and cloud ceiling not less than 1,500 ft within ATZ and CTR.
- **UCARAs (Category G)**: Pilots must have completed a border check, and flight notification to Hong Kong Police for North Border.
- **Unlicensed Pilots**: Restricted to Port Shelter and parts of Tolo and New Town south of 'line a'.

## Helicopter Operations at HKIA

- **Departure/Arrival Routes**:
  - From GFS/BAC to Pak Mong or Silvermine: Kilo East Departure via TWY K, then turn south between TXL Q3 and Fire Station, follow coastline to abeam western roundabout on Chun Wan Road, then track direct to Silvermine or Pak Mong at not above 1,500 ft AMSL, keeping 1,000 m south of runway extended centreline. Arrivals follow reciprocal.
- **Landing Locations**:
  - Junction of TWY H and H2 (for Maintenance Apron, Stands X456, X458)
  - Junction of TWY K and TXL Q3 (for BAC Apron, Stands X1, X2)
  - Direct approaches to parking locations are not permitted.
- **Runway Prohibition**: Helicopters must not land or lift off when there is movement on RWY 07R/25L.
- **Holding Procedures**:
  - Mount Rooster (not above 500 ft)
  - Tai O (not above 1,000 ft)
  - Sham Shek (not above 1,000 ft)
  - Pak Mong (not above 1,000 ft)
  - Tung Chung Bay (800-1,000 ft)
- **Runway Crossings**:
  - Via Runway 25 Crossing Corridor (between Holding Point ECHO and abeam Cathay City) or Runway 07 Crossing Corridor (between Holding Point WHISKEY and Sha Lo Wan).
  - Recommended altitude 800-1,000 ft AMSL.
  - Holding areas for crossing: WHISKEY (max 1,000 ft), ECHO (max 1,000 ft), West Sea Berth (800-1,000 ft, dimensions 600 m x 1,800 m), Expo (800-1,000 ft, dimensions 600 m x 1,800 m), Sha Lo Wan (not above 1,000 ft), Cathay City (not above 800 ft).
  - Holding patterns are right-hand orbits, speed not more than 70 KIAS.
