## Content
All published geographical coordinates in Hong Kong airspace are expressed in World Geodetic System - 1984 (WGS-84) geodetic reference datum. The area of application coincides with the Hong Kong FIR.

The vertical reference system is the Hong Kong Principal Datum (HKPD), which is about 1.30 metres below Mean Sea Level (MSL) as of 2016. The geoid model used is HKGEOID2016_SMO, corresponding to HKPD for GNSS heighting. Altitudes and elevations in the AIP are measured from MSL per ICAO definition. Differences between orthometric heights referenced to EGM-96 and HKGEOID2016_SMO vary by location; a map in the AIP shows these undulation differences.
