## Content
Hong Kong defines the following terms (from Annex 2 and Annex 11):
- **Acrobatic manoeuvres**: Includes loops, spins, rolls, bunts, stall turns, inverted flying, and any other similar manoeuvre.
- **Aeronautical radio station**: A radio station on the surface that transmits or receives signals to assist aircraft.
- **Air Traffic Control Unit/Service**: A person appointed by the Chief Executive or by any other person maintaining an aerodrome or place to give instructions or advice by radio signals to aircraft in the interests of safety.
- **Apron**: The part of an aerodrome for stationing aircraft for embarkation/disembarkation of passengers, loading/unloading of cargo, and parking.
- **Cloud ceiling**: The vertical distance from the aerodrome elevation to the lowest part of any cloud visible from the aerodrome that obscures more than one-half of the sky.
- **Flight crew**: Members of the crew who act as pilot, flight navigator, flight engineer, and flight radio operator.
- **Ground visibility**: The horizontal visibility at ground level.
- **Special VFR flight**: A flight made in a control zone in meteorological conditions below VMC or at night, with ATC permission, complying with special instructions, remaining clear of cloud and in sight of the surface.
- **Taxiing**: Manoeuvring under the following conditions: (a) aircraft moving under own power within airport boundaries, excluding take-off/landing; (b) aircraft being moved with ancillary power (e.g., tractor, jeep); (c) aircraft being manoeuvred by hand.
