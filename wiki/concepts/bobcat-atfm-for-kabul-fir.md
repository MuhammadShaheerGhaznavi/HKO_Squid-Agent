## Content
The Bay of Bengal Cooperative ATFM (BOBCAT) is implemented by States in the ICAO Asia Pacific Region (Bay of Bengal, South Asia, Pakistan). Bangkok ATFMU provides ATFM service for westbound flights intending to transit Kabul FIR between **2000 UTC and 2359 UTC daily**. The service includes calculation, promulgation, and management of mandatory CTOT, flight level, ATS route, and Calculated Time-Over (CTO) at the entry waypoint. Hong Kong ATC retains tactical management and may delay non-compliant flights via delayed pushback/startup. Exempted flights: emergencies (including unlawful interference), SAR/RFF missions, urgent medical evacuations (declared by medical authorities), Head of State, and other flights identified by State authorities. Exempted flights must indicate STS/ATFMX in item 18. Affected flights must submit slot requests via https://www.bobcat.aero between 0000-1159 UTC on the day of flight. See [[ATFM Measures]] and [[BOBCAT Operating Procedures]].
