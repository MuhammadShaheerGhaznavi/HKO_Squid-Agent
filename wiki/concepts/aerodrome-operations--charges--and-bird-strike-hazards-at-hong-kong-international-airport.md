## Hong Kong International Airport (VHHH) – Operational Overview

### Aerodrome Charges and Departure Tax

Charges at Hong Kong International Airport (HKIA) and Sky Shuttle Heliport include:
- Landing charge
- Parking charge
- Airbridge charge
- Terminal building charge
- Air passenger departure tax
- Other charges (customs, immigration, security)

Items a) to d) are collected by the Airport Authority Hong Kong under the Airport Authority Ordinance (Cap 483), with details gazetted in G.N. 3341 dated 17 June 2016 (available at http://www.gld.gov.hk/egazette). Charges at Sky Shuttle Heliport are referred to the heliport operator (see VHSS AD 3.2).

Under the Air Passenger Departure Tax Ordinance (Cap.140), every passenger departing from Hong Kong by aircraft at HKIA or Sky Shuttle Heliport must pay departure tax at rates approved by the Hong Kong Government, unless exempted. Operators must collect the tax from passengers prior to departure and pay it to the Government; unless satisfactory arrangements are made, payment in cash is required before aircraft clearance. 'Operator' means the person having control over the management of the aircraft, including a properly appointed agent. Further details are at https://www.hongkongairport.com/en/passenger-guide/departures/air-passenger-departure-tax.page.

Other charges for customs, immigration, or security may apply depending on flight operations; enquiries should be directed to authorities listed in GEN 1.1 paras 3, 4, and 6.

Landing and parking charges must be paid before departure unless prior arrangements with AAHK.

### Aerodrome Availability and Operating Minima

Hong Kong International Airport (VHHH) is notified for public transport and flying instruction under the Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995. Permission to land/take-off is not refused solely due to adverse weather, but pilots must comply with company operating minima. Engine-out ferry flights are normally not permitted; exceptions require approval from the Director-General of Civil Aviation.

Aerodrome Operating Minima (AOM) are based on ICAO Doc 9365.

**Take-off minima:**
- 125 m RVR for RWY 07L/25R and 07C/25C
- 150 m RVR for RWY 07R/25L

**Landing minima:**
- CAT I: DH 200 ft, RVR 550 m
- CAT II: DH 100 ft, RVR 300 m for RWY 07L and 25C/07C, 350 m for RWY 25L/07R
- CAT III: DH less than 100 ft, RVR less than 300 m but not less than 75 m for Cat. A/B/C aircraft, and not less than 100 m for Cat. D aircraft

Foreign operators must submit AOM Form DCA 236 for acceptance.

### Bird Migration and Bird Strike Hazards

Bird concentrations near Hong Kong International Airport pose a hazard to aircraft. Several bird species are present year-round:
- **Black-eared kite**: up to 1 kg, usually 10 but up to 45 recorded, most numerous October to January, soaring on thermals up to 2,000 ft over runway and approach areas
- **Black-headed gull**: approximately 300 g, up to 3,000 recorded from early November to early April, usually below 200 ft
- **Crested Myna**: up to 120 g, up to 200 birds around southern and western perimeter
- **Raptors**: numbers increase late October to April, including Common Kestrel >100 g and Common Buzzard >500 g, with buzzards soaring on thermals similar to Black Kite and kestrels foraging near runway up to 200 ft
- **Egret and Herons** (ardeids): up to 250 g, roost on artificial seawall, may fly to lawns when flooded

During spring (April-May) and autumn (August-October), migratory species include snipe, plover, sandpiper, and starling (30-300 g), with maximum concentrations of approximately 500 during heavy rain, low cloud, strong winds, and cold surges, usually below 100 ft.

Mitigation measures include a long grass policy at the airport to deter breeding and roosting, and regular bird control patrols on movement areas. Pilots are advised to operate landing lights at all times during take-off, approach-to-land, climb, and descent phases where design limitations permit. See [[ENR 5.6 Bird Migration]].
