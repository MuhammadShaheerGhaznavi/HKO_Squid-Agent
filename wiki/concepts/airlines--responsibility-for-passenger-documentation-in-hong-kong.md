## Content
Airlines are liable for fines (level 6) if they bring passengers without valid travel documents, except those with right of abode or right to land. Airlines must remove inadmissible passengers. The Immigration (Advance Passenger Information) Regulation sets offences for non-compliance with API. See [[Hong Kong Immigration Requirements]].
