## Hong Kong Immigration Requirements

Immigration is governed by the Immigration Ordinance (Cap. 115). The Advance Passenger Information (API) system is mandatory. Unacceptable travel documents include those from the Imamate of Oman, Venda, Maori Kingdom of Tetiti, Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus, Yemen (Royalist), Khmer Rouge in Pailin, and Taiwan.

**Visa-free entry:**
- British citizens: up to 180 days
- Nationals of many countries (e.g., Andorra, Australia, Canada, Japan, USA): up to 90 days
- Others (e.g., Armenia, Bahrain, Indonesia): up to 30 days
- Some (e.g., Albania, India): up to 14 days with conditions

**Visa always required** for nationals of Afghanistan, Angola, Bangladesh, etc. (list includes 40+ countries). Seamen may have exemptions under ILO Convention 108. See [[Hong Kong Visa Policy]].

## Agricultural Quarantine Requirements for Aircraft

Live animals, birds, or reptiles brought into Hong Kong by air require a special permit from the Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department. They must be humanely crated in IATA-standard containers and consigned as manifest cargo. The permit must be shown to the Import Control Officer before removal from the aircraft.

## Flight Plan Submission Requirements for Hong Kong FIR

All aircraft conducting IFR flights within the Hong Kong FIR must file a flight plan, except authorized operators may submit a CAD approved flight notification form for local VFR flights. Flight plans can be filed up to 5 days (120 hours) prior to EOBT, and must be submitted at least 3 hours prior to EOBT unless operational or technical reasons dictate otherwise. Submissions with less than 3 hours may experience ATFM delay when measures are in force. For delays exceeding 15 minutes beyond the last transmitted EOBT, a DLA message must be sent or a new flight plan filed. For flights departing Hong Kong, operators must inform AIMC of any EOBT delay. See [[Flight Plan Submission Methods]] and [[Flight Plan Changes and Delays]].
