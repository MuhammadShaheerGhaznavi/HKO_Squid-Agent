## Content
**Approach Lighting**
All runways have 900m approach lighting with sequenced strobe lights (2 flashes/sec) on centreline from 900m to 300m from THR. THR lights are green. PAPI provided on both sides (except 07C left side only) with MEHT values: 07L/25R: 65.3 ft (3°); 07C/25C: 72.3 ft (3°); 07R/25L: 71.2 ft (3°) and 77.4 ft (3°) respectively.

**Runway Lighting**
- **Centreline lights**: 3800m, spacing 15m (30m for 07R/25L). Colour: white from 0-2891m (or 2900m for 07C/25C/07R/25L), red/white from 2891-3491m (or 2900-3500m), red from 3491m (or 3500m) to end. Intensity: LIH.
- **Edge lights**: 3800m, spacing 58-60m (60m for 07R/25L). Colour: red from 0-176m (or 180m for 07C/25C, 160m for 07R), white from 176m (or 180m/160m) to 3146m (or 3200m), yellow from 3146m (or 3200m) to end. Intensity: LIH.
- **Runway end lights**: Red.
- **RETIL** (Runway End Identifier Lights) provided for specific taxiways: 07L: C7, C8, C9; 25R: C4, C5, C6; 07C: A7, A8, A10, F5, F6; 25C: A3, A5, A6, F3, F4; 07R: K5; 25L: K3.

**Other Lighting**
- Taxiway centreline lights on all taxiways; edge lights on some sections.
- Secondary power: standby generator, switch-over within 1 sec for CAT II/III, others within 15 sec.

**Approach Categories**
- 07L: CAT III, 25R: CAT I, 07C: CAT II, 25C: CAT III, 07R: CAT II, 25L: CAT II.
