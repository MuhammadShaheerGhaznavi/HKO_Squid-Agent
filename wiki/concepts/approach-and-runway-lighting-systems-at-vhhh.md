## Content
Each runway at VHHH has specific approach and runway lighting. Runway 07L: CAT III approach lighting, 900 m LIH with sequenced strobe lights (2 flashes/sec) from 900 m to 300 m from THR. Threshold lights green, wing bars green. PAPI on both sides, 3° glide path, MEHT 65.3 ft. TDZ lighting 900 m. Runway centreline lights: 3800 m, 15 m spacing, white from 0-2891 m, red/white from 2891-3491 m, red from 3491 m. Edge lights: 3800 m, 58-60 m spacing, red from 0-176 m, white from 176-3146 m, yellow from 3146-3800 m. End lights red. RETIL for TWY C7, C8, C9. Runway 25R: CAT I, similar lighting but PAPI 3.1° and RETIL for TWY C4, C5, C6. Runway 07C: CAT II, PAPI left side only, 3°, MEHT 72.3 ft, RETIL for TWY A7, A8, A10, F5, F6. Runway 25C: CAT III, PAPI both sides, 3°, MEHT 72.3 ft, RETIL for TWY A3, A5, A6, F3, F4. Runway 07R: CAT II, PAPI both sides, 3°, MEHT 71.2 ft, centreline lights 30 m spacing, RETIL for TWY K5. Runway 25L: CAT II, PAPI both sides, 3°, MEHT 77.4 ft, centreline lights 30 m spacing, RETIL for TWY K3. Secondary power supply: stand-by generator with switch-over time within 1 sec for CAT II/III facilities, others within 15 sec. Taxiway centreline lights on all taxiways, edge lights on some sections.
