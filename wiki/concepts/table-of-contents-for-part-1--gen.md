## Content
The Table of Contents for Part 1 (GEN) outlines the sections and subsections. GEN 0 includes preface, records, checklist, hand amendments, and table of contents. GEN 1 covers national regulations: designated authorities (GEN 1.1), entry/transit/departure of aircraft (1.2), passengers and crew (1.3), cargo (1.4), aircraft instruments/equipment/flight documents (1.5), summary of regulations and international agreements (1.6), and differences from ICAO (1.7). GEN 2 includes measuring system, aircraft markings, holidays (2.1), abbreviations (2.2), chart symbols (2.3), location indicators (2.4), radio navigation aids (2.5), conversion tables (2.6), and sunrise/sunset tables (2.7). GEN 3 covers services: aeronautical information (3.1), charts (3.2), air traffic services (3.3), communication (3.4), meteorological (3.5), and search and rescue (3.6). GEN 4 covers charges for aerodromes/heliports (4.1) and air navigation services (4.2).
