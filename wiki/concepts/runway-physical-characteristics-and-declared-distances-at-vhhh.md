## Content
Hong Kong International Airport has three parallel runways: 07L/25R, 07C/25C, and 07R/25L. All runways are 3800 m x 60 m, asphalt, with PCR 720/F/B/W/T. Runway 07L/25R has true bearing 070.90°T, magnetic 073.90°M. Threshold coordinates: 07L at 221917.72N 1135256.26E (elev 23.3 ft), 25R at 221954.45N 1135450.24E (elev 23.1 ft). Runway 07C/25C has thresholds at 221840.58N 1135356.64E (elev 22.3 ft) and 221912.85N 1135536.78E (elev 22.0 ft). Runway 07R/25L has thresholds at 221748.03N 1135357.99E (elev 27.0 ft) and 221826.75N 1135558.15E (elev 26.9 ft). All runways have full-length grooving and RESA of 240 x 150 m. Declared distances: For 07L: TORA 3800 m, TODA 3860 m, ASDA 3800 m, LDA 3626 m (THR displaced 174 m). For 25R: TORA 3800 m, TODA 3860 m, ASDA 3800 m, LDA 3626 m (THR displaced 174 m). For 07C: TORA 3800 m, TODA 3978 m, ASDA 3800 m, LDA 3629 m (THR displaced 171 m). For 25C: TORA 3800 m, TODA 3978 m, ASDA 3800 m, LDA 3629 m (THR displaced 171 m). For 07R: TORA 3800 m, TODA 4100 m, ASDA 3800 m, LDA 3640 m (THR displaced 160 m). For 25L: TORA 3800 m, TODA 4100 m, ASDA 3800 m, LDA 3800 m. When entering from certain taxiways, TORA/ASDA are reduced (e.g., from TWY C3 on 07L: 3317 m; from TWY C10 on 25R: 3228 m; from TWY A4 on 07C: 3014 m; from TWY A9 on 25C: 3249 m; from TWY J3 on 07R: 3130 m; from TWY K2 on 07R: 2720 m; from TWY J9 on 25L: 3200 m; from TWY K6 on 25L: 2880 m).
