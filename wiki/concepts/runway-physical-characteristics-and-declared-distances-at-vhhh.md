## Content
**Runway Physical Characteristics**
All runways are 3800m x 60m, asphalt, PCR 720/F/B/W/T, with full-length grooving and RESA 240m x 150m. Magnetic bearing 073.90°M (True 070.90°T) for all.
- **RWY 07L/25R**: THR 07L at 221917.72N 1135256.26E, elev 23.3 ft; THR 25R at 221954.45N 1135450.24E, elev 23.1 ft. CWY 60m x 150m. Strip 3920m x 300m. OFZ: Yes.
- **RWY 07C/25C**: THR 07C at 221840.58N 1135356.64E, elev 22.3 ft; THR 25C at 221912.85N 1135536.78E, elev 22.0 ft. CWY 178m x 150m. Strip 3920m x 300m. OFZ: Yes.
- **RWY 07R/25L**: THR 07R at 221748.03N 1135357.99E, elev 27.0 ft; THR 25L at 221826.75N 1135558.15E, elev 26.9 ft. CWY 300m x 150m. Strip 3920m x 300m. OFZ: Yes.

**Key Notes**
- Displaced thresholds: 07L/25R by 174m, 07C/25C by 171m, 07R by 160m.
- Intersection take-offs reduce TORA/ASDA: e.g., from TWY C3 on 07L: 3317m; from TWY C10 on 25R: 3228m; from TWY A4 on 07C: 3014m; from TWY A9 on 25C: 3249m; from TWY J3 on 07R: 3130m; from TWY K2 on 07R: 2720m; from TWY J9 on 25L: 3200m; from TWY K6 on 25L: 2880m.
- Nosewheel guideline intersection distances from runway start: J1/J11/K1/K7: 100m; C1: 121m; C2: 241m; C11: 295m; C12: 61m; J10: 199m; J2: 180m; A1/F1: 121m; A2/F2: 219m; A11/F7: 219m; A12/F8: 121m.
- For 07C/25C, sections with arrow markings between specified taxiways are not to be used for landing/take-off.
