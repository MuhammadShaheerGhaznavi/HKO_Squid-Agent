## Content
The Hong Kong Control Zone (CTR) is bounded by a series of coordinates (e.g., 22 28 34N 114 00 54E, 22 26 59N 114 00 25E, etc.) and extends from SFC to 9,000 ft, Class C. Service by HONG KONG TWR (HONG KONG TOWER) on frequencies 118.2, 118.4, 118.7, 119.1, 119.35, 119.5, 120.6, 122.0, 122.075, 122.65, 123.225, 123.8, 124.05, 124.65 MHz, plus emergency 121.5 MHz. H24. Airspace south of the TMA: Class A from UNL to 8,000 ft, Class G from 8,000 ft to SFC, with HONG KONG FIS on 122.4 and 122.075 MHz. Airspace beneath TMA: Class G from SFC to 2,000 ft (within 50 NM of BIGEX) or 8,000 ft (beyond 50 NM), requiring two-way communication capability. UCARAs are Class G areas with specific boundaries, vertical limits up to 2,000 ft or 3,000 ft, served by HONG KONG INFORMATION on 121.0 and 122.075 MHz, hours 01:00 to SS. No UIR exists.
