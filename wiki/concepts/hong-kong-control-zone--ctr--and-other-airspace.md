## Content
The Hong Kong Control Zone (CTR) is a Class C airspace from SFC to 9000 ft, bounded by coordinates including 22 28 34N 114 00 54E, 22 26 59N 114 00 25E, 22 25 51N 114 03 03E, 22 24 20N 114 04 10E, 22 25 15N 114 07 08E, 22 24 40N 114 10 40E, 22 22 40N 114 13 20E, 22 20 30N 114 13 25E, 22 15 15N 114 21 00E, 22 08 50N 114 21 00E, 22 08 50N 114 17 05E, 22 08 20N 114 15 20E, 22 08 50N 114 14 10E, 22 08 50N 114 11 45E, 22 06 40N 114 11 45E, 21 56 25N 113 48 00E, 22 13 44N 113 39 57E, 22 24 34N 113 49 44E. Service is provided by Hong Kong TWR (call sign HONG KONG TOWER) on frequencies 118.2, 118.4, 118.7, 119.1, 119.35, 119.5, 120.6, 122.0, 122.075, 122.65, 123.225, 123.8, 124.05, 124.65 MHz, with emergency 121.5 MHz. The airspace south of the TMA is Class A from UNL to 8000 ft (provided by HONG KONG ACC on frequencies 122.95, 123.7, 125.325, 125.8, 127.1, 128.125, 128.75, 129.9, 132.15, 132.525, 132.775, 135.6 MHz) and Class G from 8000 ft to SFC (provided by HONG KONG FIS on 122.4 and 122.075 MHz). Airspace beneath the TMA is Class G, with a requirement for aircraft to maintain direct two-way communication with ATC. UCARAs are Class G airspace with specific boundaries, hours of service (01:00 to SS for one area), and frequencies 121.0 and 122.075 MHz. There is no UIR in Hong Kong.
