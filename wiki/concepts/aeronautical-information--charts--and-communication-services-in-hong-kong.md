## Aeronautical Information Services (AIS)

The Aeronautical Information Service in Hong Kong is provided by the **Aeronautical Information Management Centre (AIMC)**, a section of the Air Traffic Management Division of the Civil Aviation Department. The International NOTAM office is an integral part of the AIMC. The service follows ICAO Annex 15, Doc 8126, and Doc 10066. The AIMC is responsible for collecting and disseminating information for the Hong Kong Flight Information Region (FIR).

Key publications include:
- **AIP Hong Kong** (English only)
- **AIP Amendments (AIP AMDT)** issued on AIRAC dates
- **AIP Supplements (AIP SUP)** for temporary changes
- **NOTAM** (Series A for international, Series C for local)
- **Aeronautical Information Circulars (AIC)**

All products are available at [http://www.ais.gov.hk](http://www.ais.gov.hk).

**Contact:** AIMC, Civil Aviation Department Headquarters, 1 Tung Fai Road, Hong Kong International Airport, Lantau; Tel +852 2910 6174; Fax +852 2910 1180; AFS VHHHYNYX; email aic@cad.gov.hk.

## Aeronautical Charts

Aeronautical charts in Hong Kong are produced by the **Assistant Director-General of Civil Aviation (Air Traffic Management)** under the Director-General. They comply with ICAO Annex 4 and Doc 8697. Charts are kept up to date via AIP amendments; new charts are notified by AIC. All charts are available for download at [www.ais.gov.hk](http://www.ais.gov.hk).

The chart series include:
- Aerodrome Chart - ICAO
- Aerodrome Chart - Visual Markings
- Aerodrome Chart - Lighting Plan
- Aircraft Parking/Docking Chart - ICAO
- Aerodrome Obstacle Chart - ICAO Type A (for each runway) and Type B
- Precision Approach Terrain Chart - ICAO
- En-route Chart - ICAO
- Area Chart (for departure, arrival, transiting)
- Instrument Approach Chart - ICAO
- SID and STAR charts - ICAO
- ATC Surveillance Minimum Altitude Chart - ICAO

Topographical charts are published by the **Lands Department**, including:
- Hong Kong Helicopter Flying Chart (1:50,000)
- Hong Kong Local Flying Chart (1:100,000)

**Contact:** Map Publications Centre, 6/F North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong; Tel +852 2231 3187; Fax +852 2116 0774; email smosale1@landsd.gov.hk.

## Communication and Navigation Services

Communication and navigation services are administered by the **Assistant Director-General of Civil Aviation (Air Traffic Management)**. Services follow ICAO Annex 10, Doc 8400, Doc 8585, Doc 7030, Doc 7910, and Doc 9880.

Radio navigation aids include:
- ILS
- VOR
- DME
- Various surveillance radars (RSR, ASR, TSR, RSSR, ASSR, TSSR)

The **Aeronautical Network Centre (ANC)** provides automatic message switching via AFTN and ATN on H24 basis. Hong Kong is a Regional OPMET Centre under the ROBEX scheme.

Broadcasting services include:
- HF VOLMET
- VHF ATIS (arrival 128.20 MHz, departure 127.05 MHz)
- Continuous VHF VOLMET of Shenzhen, Macau, Taibei, and Gaoxiong on 128.875 MHz

Data link services include:
- D-ATIS (codes VHHHA for arrival, VHHHD for departure)
- D-VOLMET
- A 2-way pre-departure clearance (PDC) service for aircraft with ACARS compliant with AEEC Specification 623

ATIS hotlines: Arrival +852 3141 2820, Departure +852 3141 2705.

English is the only language used in aeronautical communication.
