## Content
The AIP Hong Kong is published by the Director-General of Civil Aviation, Civil Aviation Department. It is prepared in accordance with ICAO Annex 15, ICAO Doc 8126, and ICAO Doc 10066. Charts follow Annex 4 and ICAO Doc 8697. Differences from ICAO standards are in GEN 1.7. The AIP is part of the Integrated Aeronautical Information Package and has three parts: General (GEN), En-route (ENR), and Aerodromes (AD). Regular amendments are issued every 28 days on AIRAC effective dates. Contact for errors: Assistant Director-General of Civil Aviation (Air Traffic Management), email aic@cad.gov.hk.

Structure:
- Part 1 - General (GEN) with 5 sections (GEN 0-4), covering preface, regulations, tables, services, and charges. GEN 1 covers national regulations, GEN 2 tables and codes, GEN 3 services, GEN 4 charges.
- Part 2 - En-route (ENR) with 7 sections (ENR 0-6), covering rules, airspace, routes, navigation aids, warnings, and charts. ENR 1 covers rules and procedures, ENR 2 airspace, ENR 3 routes, ENR 4 navigation aids, ENR 5 warnings, ENR 6 charts.
- Part 3 - Aerodromes (AD) with 4 sections (AD 0-3), covering introductions, aerodrome details, and heliports. AD 2 provides detailed aerodrome information under 24 subsections.
