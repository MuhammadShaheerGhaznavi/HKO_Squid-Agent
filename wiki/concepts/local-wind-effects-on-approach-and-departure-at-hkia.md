## Content
Significant low-level wind shear and moderate to severe turbulence can occur on approaches/departures when winds blow off Lantau Island (east through southwest at ~15 kt or more) or from northern hills (northwest through northeast at ~20 kt or more). Wind disturbances are small-scale, sporadic, and transient, affecting successive aircraft differently. Surface winds are not good indicators; winds at ~2,000 ft are better. Easterly through southwesterly winds >15 kt cause significant shear, more so >30 kt, especially over south runway (RWY 07R/25L) and from 130°-210°. Northwesterly through northeasterly winds >20 kt affect RWY 25 approaches and RWY 07 departures, with larger effects >30 kt, especially last 15 NM to RWY 25. Land-sea breeze can cause complex wind fields and significant shear. Low-level jets up to 50 kt during winter monsoon surges affect RWY 07 departures. Building-induced turbulence is possible on specific runways: RWY 25L in strong NW/N winds, RWY 25C in strong SW/S/SE winds, RWY 25C in NW/N/NE winds ~15 kt, RWY 25R in strong S winds, RWY 07R TDZ in NW/N winds ~15 kt, and takeoff beyond mid-point of RWY 07C in NW/N/NE winds ~15 kt.
