## Content
The hilly terrain of Lantau Island to the south and east of HKIA causes significant low-level wind shear and moderate to severe turbulence on approaches and departures when winds blow off these hills from east through southwest at about 15 kt or more. Hills to the north are less significant but can cause effects when winds are from northwest through northeast at about 20 kt or more. Wind disturbances can be small-scale, sporadic, and transient, affecting successive aircraft differently. Surface winds are not good indicators; winds at ~2,000 ft are better. Wind shear and turbulence may increase towards final approach. For easterly through southwesterly winds >15 kt, significant shear and moderate turbulence expected, more significant over south runway (RWY 07R/25L), especially from 130°-210°. For northwesterly through northeasterly winds >20 kt, effects are more significant for approaches to RWY 25 and departure from RWY 07, with larger magnitude if >30 kt, especially within last 15 NM on final to RWY 25. Land-sea breeze can cause significant wind direction changes and shear. Low-level jets up to 50 kt during winter monsoon surges cause shear on RWY 07 departures. Building-induced turbulence is possible on specific runways: RWY 25L in strong NW/N winds; RWY 25C in strong SW/S/SE winds or NW/N/NE winds ≥15 kt; RWY 25R in strong S winds; RWY 07R TDZ in NW/N winds ≥15 kt; and take-off beyond mid-point of RWY 07C in NW/N/NE winds ≥15 kt.
