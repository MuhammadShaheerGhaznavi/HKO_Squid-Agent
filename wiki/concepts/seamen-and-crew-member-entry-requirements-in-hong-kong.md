## Content
Seamen who are visa-free nationals do not need visas. Visa nationals with ILO Convention 108 seafarer documents may transit without visa if joining a vessel within 48 hours, repatriating, or joining a vessel in Hong Kong registered in a ratifying territory. Exceptions: nationals of Angola, Cameroon, Ghana, Guinea-Bissau, Iran, Iraq, Liberia, Sri Lanka. Mainland seamen with PRC passports/seamen books need letters of indemnity. Flight crew in uniform with valid Crew Member Certificate or airline ID may get temporary admission, but not for private aircraft crew. See [[Hong Kong Immigration Requirements]].
