## Content
All aircraft (except gliders) in the Hong Kong FIR must carry specified radio and navigation equipment. For IFR flights in controlled airspace, VHF radio, Mode 3/A and Mode C transponder, VOR, and DME are required. Within the Hong Kong Control Zone, VHF radio and transponder are required. Aircraft with maximum total weight not exceeding 2,730 kg engaged in local flying are exempt from VOR and DME. ADF is not required as NDB procedures are not used. All aircraft in controlled airspace must have Mode 3/A (4096 codes) and Mode C transponders complying with ICAO Annex 10 Volume IV. Aeroplanes in commercial air transport with MCTOW >5,700 kg or carrying >19 passengers must have TCAS II (ACAS II standards, Version 7.1 from 1 Jan 2017). All aircraft in RVSM airspace also require TCAS II. For RNAV routes M772 and P901, RNP 10 approval is required; for L642 and M771, RNP 4 approval. RNP 1 is required for SID, STAR, and ILS/LOC transitions at HKIA, with mandatory GNSS receiver, unless exempted (humanitarian/SAR, state aircraft, flight check, maintenance/delivery, air tests, or prior DGCA approval). In case of 'unable RNP' or loss of GNSS, revert to RNAV 1 (DME/DME/IRU) and inform ATC. See [[GEN 1.5 Notification Requirements]] for related notification details.
