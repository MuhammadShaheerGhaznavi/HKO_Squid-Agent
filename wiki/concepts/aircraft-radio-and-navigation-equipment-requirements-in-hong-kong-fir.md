## Content
Aircraft operating in the Hong Kong FIR must comply with the Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995. Key requirements include:
- **VHF radio and transponder**: All IFR flights in controlled airspace require VHF radio on published frequencies, Mode 3/A (4096 codes) and Mode C transponder. Flights within the Hong Kong Control Zone also require these.
- **VOR and DME**: Required for IFR flights in controlled airspace, but aircraft with max total weight ≤ 2,730 kg engaged in local flying within Hong Kong are exempt.
- **ADF**: Not required as NDB procedures are not used.
- **ILS**: All aircraft approaching HKIA must carry ILS-capable radio, unless exempted (e.g., aircraft ≤ 2,730 kg in local flying).
- **ACAS/TCAS**: Required for commercial air transport aeroplanes > 5,700 kg MCTOW or > 19 passengers, and all aeroplanes in RVSM airspace. TCAS II must be Version 7.1 since 1 Jan 2017.
- **RNAV**: RNP 10 approval needed for routes M772 and P901; RNP 4 for L642 and M771; RNP 1 for SID/STAR/ILS transitions at HKIA, with mandatory GNSS receiver. Exemptions for humanitarian/SAR, state aircraft, flight check, maintenance/delivery, air tests, and with DGCA approval.
- **RVSM**: Requires airworthiness and operational approval, participation in monitoring programme (MAAR).
- **ADS-B**: Mandatory at or above F290 in Hong Kong FIR, must meet DO-260/260A/260B standards (ES versions 0, 1, 2). Non-compliant equipment must be deactivated or transmit zero values for NUCp/NIC/NAC/SIL. Flight plan must include ADS-B designator and correct Aircraft Identification (≤7 characters).
