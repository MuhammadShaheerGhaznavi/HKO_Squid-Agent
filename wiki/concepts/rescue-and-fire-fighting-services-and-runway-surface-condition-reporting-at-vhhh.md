## Content
Rescue and fire fighting vehicles are provided at VHHH per Annex 14 Attachment A. Additionally, 2 rescue launches with foam/water capability and 8 high speed rescue boats are available H24. Runway surface condition is assessed and reported using the ICAO Global Reporting Format (GRF) when water or contaminants are present. Runway Condition Report (RCR) includes Runway Condition Code (RWYCC) and details on contaminants (type, depth, coverage per third). RWYCC values applicable in Hong Kong: 6 (Dry), 5 (Wet up to 3 mm depth, braking normal, pilot report GOOD), 3 (Slippery wet, braking noticeably reduced, pilot report MEDIUM), 2 (Standing water >3 mm, braking between medium and poor, pilot report MEDIUM TO POOR). Communication channels: RWYCC 6 – air-ground voice on request, ATIS no, SNOWTAM no; RWYCC 5 – air-ground yes, ATIS yes, SNOWTAM no; RWYCC 3 – air-ground yes, ATIS yes, SNOWTAM no; RWYCC 2 – air-ground yes, ATIS yes, SNOWTAM yes. Pilots must make special air report (AIREP) if worse braking action is experienced. GRF applies to VHHH only. Snow plan: Nil.
