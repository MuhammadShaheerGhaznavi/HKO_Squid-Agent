## Content
Hong Kong ATFMU may implement measures defined in ICAO Doc 9971: Airborne Holding (holding at waypoint), MINutes-In-Trail (MINIT) and Miles-In-Trail (MIT) at FIR boundaries, Reroute (mandatory or advisory scenarios), Arrival/Departure Fix Balancing (stack-swap), Minimum Departure Interval (MDI), Level Capping, Ground Stop (GS), Ground Delay Programme (GDP) for airport constraints (using Airport Acceptance Rate AAR), and GDP for waypoint/airspace constraints (Airspace Flow Programme AFP). GDP assigns Calculated Take Off Times (CTOT). See [[ATFM Overview]] and [[GDP CTOT Compliance]].
