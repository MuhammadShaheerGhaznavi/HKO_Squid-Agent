## Content
Hong Kong ATFMU may implement various ATFM measures to resolve capacity-demand imbalances or facilitate ATC flow restrictions. These include:
- **Airborne Holding**: Aircraft hold at a waypoint in a pre-defined pattern.
- **Minutes-In-Trail (MINIT)**: Tactical measure expressed as minutes between successive aircraft at FIR entry/exit fixes.
- **Miles-In-Trail (MIT)**: Tactical measure expressed in nautical miles between successive aircraft at boundary points.
- **Reroute**: Route-based measure to reroute flights to constrained resources, often in scenarios (mandatory or advisory).
- **Arrival/Departure Fix Balancing**: Re-routing to different fixes or holding patterns (stack-swap) to redistribute demand.
- **Minimum Departure Interval (MDI)**: Time-based departure flow rate restricting departures at an airport.
- **Level Capping**: Restricting aircraft to specified flight levels to balance airspace demand.
- **Ground Stop (GS)**: Tactical measure for unpredicted adverse situations, keeping selected aircraft on the ground.
- **Ground Delay Programme (GDP) for Airport Constraint**: Holds aircraft on ground to manage capacity/demand at an aerodrome, assigning Calculated Take-Off Times (CTOT). Triggered when Airport Acceptance Rate (AAR) is reduced.
- **GDP for Waypoint or Airspace Constraint (Airspace Flow Programme, AFP)**: Holds flights on ground to manage demand in specific airspace or over a waypoint.
These measures are detailed in ICAO Doc 9971. See [[ATFM Overview]] and [[GDP CTOT Compliance Window]].
