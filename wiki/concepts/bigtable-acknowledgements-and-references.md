## Content
The Bigtable paper acknowledges feedback from anonymous reviewers, David Nagle, and shepherd Brad Calder, as well as contributions from many Google users and specific individuals. The references include foundational works on distributed systems, databases, and storage, such as the Google File System, MapReduce, Chubby, and various database systems. These references provide context for Bigtable's design and comparisons. See also [[Bigtable Conclusions and Production Experience]].
