## Content
The Flight Data Processing System (FDPS) in Hong Kong is fully automated, so flight plan data must be submitted in standard format. Operators must strictly comply with route syntax specified in ENR 1.10 paragraphs 6 to 9; any discrepancy will be rejected, causing delay. For flights inbound to HKIA or transiting Hong Kong FIR, Item 18 must include: DOF/yymmdd (Date of Flight), national registration letters if different from Item 7, and EET/VHHK followed by a four-figure group for accumulated estimated elapsed time to Hong Kong FIR. Only specific indicators shall be used in Item 18, and free text is not allowed for 'STS/'—only ICAO Doc 4444 Appendix 2 indicators. Operators must also refer to ENR 1.8 para 8 for Flight Level Assignment Scheme. See [[Flight Plan Submission Requirements]].
