## AIP Hong Kong

The AIP Hong Kong is published by the Director-General of Civil Aviation, Civil Aviation Department. It is prepared in accordance with ICAO Annex 15, ICAO Doc 8126, and ICAO Doc 10066. Charts follow Annex 4 and ICAO Doc 8697. Differences from ICAO standards are in GEN 1.7. The AIP is part of the Integrated Aeronautical Information Package and consists of three parts: General (GEN), En-route (ENR), and Aerodromes (AD).

- **Part 1 (GEN)** has 5 sections: GEN 0 (preface, records, checklist, table of contents), GEN 1 (national regulations), GEN 2 (tables and codes), GEN 3 (services), and GEN 4 (charges).
- **Part 2 (ENR)** has 7 sections covering rules, airspace, routes, navigation aids, warnings, and charts.
- **Part 3 (AD)** has 4 sections covering aerodrome/heliport introduction, detailed aerodrome info, and heliports.

Regular amendments are issued every 28 days on AIRAC effective dates. Errors or omissions should be reported to the Assistant Director-General of Civil Aviation (Air Traffic Management) via email aic@cad.gov.hk.

## Hong Kong FIR and TMA Airspace Structure

The Hong Kong FIR is bounded by coordinates: 23 40 00N 117 30 00E, 21 00 00N 117 30 00E, 16 40 00N 114 00 00E, 19 30 00N 111 30 00E, 21 25 00N 111 30 00E, then along the territorial waters of the PRC (3 NM offshore) and northern boundary of Macao and HKSAR back to start. Vertical limits: UNL to SFC. Airspace classes: A, C, and G. Service provided by Hong Kong ACC (HONG KONG RADAR) on frequencies including 118.925, 121.3, 122.95, 123.475, 123.7, 123.95, 125.175, 125.325, 125.8, 126.3, 126.5, 127.1, 127.55, 128.125, 128.75, 129.9, 132.15, 132.225, 132.525, 132.6, 132.775, 132.8, 133.7, 133.825, 134.3, 135.6 MHz, plus emergency 121.5 MHz and HF 5655, 8942, 13309 kHz. H24.

The Hong Kong TMA has similar boundaries but with vertical limits: Class A from UNL to 8,000 ft (or 9,000 ft above CTR and UCARAs), Class C from 8,000 ft to 2,000 ft (or 9,000 ft to upper limit of UCARA). Service by HONG KONG ACC/APP on frequencies including 118.925, 119.1, 119.35, 119.5, 121.3, 122.0, 122.65, 123.225, 123.475, 123.7, 123.8, 123.95, 124.05, 125.175, 125.325, 125.8, 126.3, 126.5, 127.1, 127.55, 128.125, 128.75, 129.9, 132.15, 132.225, 132.525, 132.6, 132.775, 132.8, 133.7, 133.825, 134.3 MHz, plus emergency 121.5 MHz. H24.
