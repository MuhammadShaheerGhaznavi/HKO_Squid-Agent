## Entry, Transit and Departure of Aircraft, Passengers, Crew and Cargo in Hong Kong

### Aircraft Entry, Transit and Departure

All flights to or from Hong Kong must comply with relevant laws. Fixed-wing aircraft must land at or depart from Hong Kong International Airport. Subsonic jet aircraft must have a noise certificate per the Civil Aviation (Aircraft Noise) Ordinance (Cap. 312) or documentary proof of compliance with Chapter 3 standards of Annex 16. The certificate must be carried on board and produced on request.

Air services (for hire or reward) are regulated by the Air Transport (Licensing of Air Services) Regulations (Cap. 448 sub. leg. A). Operators should direct applications to the Air Services and Safety Management Division (tel: 2910 6648, fax: 2877 8542).

**Insurance:** All civil aircraft must have Combined Single Limit (CSL) insurance covering third party, passenger, baggage, cargo, and mail liabilities (unless declared not carried). Minimum CSL amounts are based on maximum ramp or taxi weight:
- Up to 5,700 kg – US$15M
- 5,700–10,000 kg – US$25M
- 10,000–28,000 kg – US$60M
- 28,000–100,000 kg – US$200M
- 100,000–170,000 kg – US$500M
- Over 170,000 kg – US$1B

Insurance must be per occurrence, and proof must be current. Non-compliant aircraft are not allowed to land or take off except in emergencies.

**Permits:** Scheduled air services require an operating permit from the Director-General of Civil Aviation, applied at least one month in advance. Non-scheduled flights require a permit at least 3 working days before arrival/departure (2 weeks for new operators). Flights not for hire or reward require prior clearance with specific documents (e.g., DCA 41 form). Overflights generally do not require prior permission except for state aircraft, munitions, dangerous goods, or as notified; requests for munitions/dangerous goods must be made at least 11 clear working days in advance.

### Passengers and Crew

**Customs:** Hong Kong is a free port; excise duties apply only to liquors, tobacco, hydrocarbon oils, and methyl alcohol. Duty-free concessions: passengers aged 18+ may bring 1 litre of alcoholic liquor >30% ABV (if HKID holder, must have been outside HK for 24+ hours) and either 19 cigarettes, 1 cigar or 25g of cigars, or 25g of other manufactured tobacco.

**Immigration:** Governed by the Immigration Ordinance (Cap. 115). Advance Passenger Information (API) system is in place. Passengers must have valid travel documents and visas where required. Visas are not required for holders of HK passports, HK Document of Identity, HK Re-entry Permit, HK Seaman's Identity Book, HK Permanent Identity Card, HKID with 'R' code, travel documents with specific endorsements, UN Laissez-Passer, British citizens (up to 180 days), and nationals of many countries (up to 90 days, 30 days, or 14 days depending on nationality). A full list of visa-exempt countries is provided in the AIP. Visas are always required for nationals of certain countries (e.g., Afghanistan, Angola, Bangladesh, etc.) and holders of specific passports (e.g., Albanian non-biometric, Costa Rican provisional, etc.).

**Seamen:** Visa-free nationals do not need visas; visa nationals with ILO Convention 108 seafarer IDs may transit without visa under conditions. Crew members with valid Crew Member Certificates or airline IDs may be granted temporary admission.

**Transit passengers:** Those with stateless documents or from visa-required countries need transit visas to land; those remaining airside do not need visas except for a list of countries. Airlines are responsible for passengers without valid documents and may be fined.

**Health:** Compliance with International Health Regulations and Prevention and Control of Disease Ordinance (Cap. 599). Illness/death on board must be reported. Air Passenger Departure Tax applies under Cap. 140. General aviation and private flights have specific procedures for CIQ clearance.

### Cargo

Customs formalities follow Annex 9 of the Chicago Convention. Certain goods require import licences: narcotic drugs, firearms and ammunitions, dangerous goods, endangered species, strategic commodities, reserved commodities, pesticides, radioactive substances, pharmaceutical products, textiles, and ozone-depleting substances. Further details from Trade Department or Customs & Excise Department.

**Agricultural quarantine:** Animals, birds, or reptiles brought into Hong Kong require a special permit from the Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department; they must be humanely crated in IATA-standard containers and consigned as manifest cargo.

**Health:** Fish, shellfish, vegetables, or beverages from cholera-infected places must be declared to Health Authorities before unloading. Under Cap. 599A, importing human corpses, infectious agents, or suspected infected tissues/fluids without a permit is prohibited.
