## Hong Kong Aircraft Noise Certification

Under Section 3 of the Civil Aviation (Aircraft Noise) Ordinance (Cap. 312), subsonic jet aircraft must have a noise certificate issued by a state party to the Chicago Convention, or documentary proof of compliance with Chapter 3 noise standards (Volume I, Part II, Chapter 3 of Annex 16). The certificate must be carried on board and produced to authorized officers upon request. This applies to all landings and takeoffs in Hong Kong.

## Hong Kong Aircraft Insurance Requirements

The Civil Aviation (Insurance) Order (Cap. 448 sub. leg. F) requires all civil aircraft to have Combined Single Limit (CSL) insurance covering third-party, passenger, baggage, cargo, and mail liabilities. Operators may exclude items if declared in writing. Minimum CSL amounts are based on maximum ramp or taxi weight (or MTOW):

- ≤5,700 kg – US$15M
- ≤10,000 kg – US$25M
- ≤28,000 kg – US$60M
- ≤100,000 kg – US$200M
- ≤170,000 kg – US$500M
- >170,000 kg – US$1B

Coverage must be per occurrence. Proof of insurance must be current, and continued coverage must be evidenced before policy expiry. Non-compliance prohibits landing/takeoff except in emergencies.
