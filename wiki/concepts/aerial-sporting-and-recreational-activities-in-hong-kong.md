## Content
Hong Kong permits various aerial sporting and recreational activities with specific regulations. Paragliding is frequently carried out in several areas during daylight hours, with operating altitudes and boundary coordinates as follows: a) Long Ke Wan (Sai Kung East Country Park, UCARA Port Shelter) SFC-2000 ft AMSL, coordinates 222236N 1142209E, 222300N 1142247E, 222148N 1142259E, 222145N 1142241E; b) Pak Tam Au (Sai Kung East Country Park, UCARA Tolo) SFC-2000 ft AMSL, coordinates 222610N 1141821E, 222729N 1141851E, 222552N 1142233E, 222506N 1142220E, 222517N 1141957E, 222541N 1141836E; c) Pat Sin (Pat Sin Leng Country Park, UCARA New Town) SFC-2500 ft AMSL, coordinates 222759N 1140959E, 222927N 1141029E, 222923N 1141228E, 222908N 1141403E, 222803N 1141404E, 222753N 1141034E; d) Ma On Shan (Ma On Shan Country Park, UCARA New Town) SFC-2500 ft AMSL, with boundary via landmarks (Sai O Raw Water Pumping Station, Ma On Shan Water Treatment Works, Tai Tung Tsuen, Nga Yiu Tau, then along Sai Sha Road to Wong Chuk Wan, Shek Lung Tsai San Tsuen, 222214N 1141453E, Tsim Mei Fung); e) Sai Wan (Sai Kung East Country Park, UCARA Port Shelter) SFC-1000 ft AMSL, coordinates 222331N 1142147E, 222417N 1142150E, 222408N 1142225E, 222316N 1142222E; f) Shek O (Hong Kong Island, CTR Island Zone) SFC-1500 ft AMSL, coordinates 221221N 1141434E, 221442N 1141325E, 221459N 1141507E, 221347N 1141537E, 221228N 1141536E; g) South Lantau East (Lantau South Country Park, CTR Lantau Zone) SFC-2000 ft AMSL, coordinates 221356N 1135731E, 221417N 1135849E, 221542N 1135816E, 221530N 1135723E, then footpath south of Sunset Peak, along ridge to 350m, then direct to Cheung Sha Beach; h) South Lantau West (Lantau South Country Park, CTR Lantau Zone) SFC-2000 ft AMSL, coordinates 221325N 1135543E, 221310N 1135432E, 221438N 1135434E, 221456N 1135541E, then along ridge from Lantau Peak to Tong Fuk; i) South Lantau (Lantau South Country Park, CTR Lantau Zone) SFC-500 ft AMSL (for landing only), coordinates 221340N 1135608E, 221356N 1135731E, 221354N 1135609E, 221427N 1135707E. Paragliders shall avoid landing on bathing beaches or private premises without permission. Captive balloon flights (with or without persons) up to 60 m (200 ft) AGL may be granted permission, with applications to CAD at least 14 days prior. Mass release of small balloons (not larger than 30 cm diameter) up to 500 in number requires notification to CAD at least 7 working days prior. Kites are permitted up to 60 m (200 ft) AGL but prohibited within 5 km of an aerodrome. Model aircraft flying is allowed at suitable locations, usually on weekends and public holidays up to 300 ft AGL between 0900-1900 local time; organised sites include Tai Tong, Yuen Long (UTM Grid JK955820) up to 350 ft AGL, and Tseung Kwan O (UTM Grid KK198670) up to 200 ft AGL; other popular locations include Nam Sang Wai, Tate's Cairn, and Clearwater Bay Peninsula. Aerobatic flights are designated in the NE part of Hong Kong territory up to 3,500 ft AMSL. See [[ENR 5.5 Aerial Sporting and Recreational Activities]].
