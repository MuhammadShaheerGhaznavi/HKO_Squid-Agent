## Content
Three-runway operations (normally 0000-1459 UTC daily): RWY 07L arrivals only; 07C departures only (to North/Northeast/East via BEKOL, LEKEN - V601/V611, or DALOL - V621/V631); 07R mixed (to Southeast/South/West via DALOL - V642/V652 or PECAN - V10/V11/V12). For RWY 25: 25R arrivals only; 25C departures only (to East/Southeast/South/West via DALOL - V621/V631/V642/V652 or PECAN - V10/V11/V12); 25L mixed (to North/Northeast via BEKOL or LEKEN - V601/V611). Arrivals use dependent parallel approaches to 07L/07R or 25R/25L. Aircraft parking at South Cargo Apron or Business Aviation Centre preferentially assigned South Runway (07R/25L). Independent parallel departures on 07C/07R or 25C/25L. Dual/single runway operations: 1500-1759 UTC, Centre (07C/25C) arrivals, South (07R/25L) departures in segregated mode; 1800-2259 UTC, one runway (North or Centre) in mixed mode; 2300-2359 UTC, dual runway operations resume. Runway utilisation: departures should enter runway without delay and commence take-off roll as soon as clearance issued. Each runway has a pair of taxiways at the beginning (e.g., 07L: C1, C2; 25R: C11, C12; 07C: A1/A2 and F1/F2; 25C: A11/A12 and F7/F8; 07R: J1/J2; 25L: J10/J11). Intersection departures may be requested with reduced TORA. Arrivals should vacate via first available RET; RETILs on TWYs C4, C5, C6, C7, C8, C9, A3, A5, A6, A7, A8, A10, F3, F4, F5, F6, K3, K5 provide distance info at 300m, 200m, 100m. Reduced Runway Separation Minima (RRSM) may be applied under conditions: visibility ≥5 km, ceiling ≥3000 ft, daylight hours, second aircraft can see first, no adverse wind/braking. Separation distances: for RWY 07R/25L, 2900 m from threshold (abeam TWY K6 for 07R or K2 for 25L); for RWY 07C/25C, 2400 m (abeam TWY F6/A8 for 07C or F3 for 25C); for RWY 07L/25R, 2400 m (abeam TWY C8 for 07L or C5 for 25R).
