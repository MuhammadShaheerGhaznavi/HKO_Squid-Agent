## Content
Cargo flights must include 'RMK/CARGO' in Item 18. General aviation flights parking at Business Aviation Centre must include 'RMK/BAC PARKING'. For RNAV approvals: RNP10 requires 'R' in Item 10a and 'A1' after PBN/ in Item 18; RNP4 requires 'RG' in Item 10a and 'L1' after PBN/; RNP1 requires 'RGDI' in Item 10a and 'O1' or 'O2' after PBN/; RNAV5 requires 'RGODI' or 'RGSDI' in Item 10a and 'B1' to 'B5' after PBN/; RNAV2 requires 'R' in Item 10a and 'C1' or 'C2' after PBN/; RNAV1 requires 'RDGI' in Item 10a and 'D1' or 'D4' after PBN/; RNP-AR APCH requires 'T1' or 'T2' after PBN/ in Item 18. RVSM approved aircraft must insert 'W' in Item 10; non-RVSM aircraft capable of FL280 or above must insert 'STS/NONRVSM' in Item 18. ADS-B approved aircraft must indicate 'B1' (out) or 'B2' (out and in) in Item 10. See [[Flight Plan Content and Format Requirements]].
