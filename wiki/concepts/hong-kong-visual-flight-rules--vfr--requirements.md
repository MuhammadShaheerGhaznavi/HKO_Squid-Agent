## Hong Kong Visual Flight Rules (VFR) Requirements

### VFR Minima (Annex 2, para 3.9 and 4.1)

- **Class B airspace**:
  - At or above FL100: clear of cloud, flight visibility ≥8 km.
  - Below FL100: clear of cloud, flight visibility ≥5 km.

- **Class C, D, or E airspace**:
  - At or above FL100: 1,500 m horizontally and 1,000 ft vertically from cloud, flight visibility ≥8 km.
  - Below FL100: same cloud clearance, flight visibility ≥5 km.

- **Outside controlled airspace**:
  - At or above FL100: 1,500 m horizontally and 1,000 ft vertically from cloud, flight visibility ≥8 km.
  - Below FL100: same cloud clearance, flight visibility ≥5 km.

### Provisions (deemed compliance)
The above minima are deemed complied with if:
- (i) Aircraft at or below 3,000 ft AMSL, clear of cloud, in sight of surface, flight visibility ≥5 km.
- (ii) Aircraft (other than helicopter) at or below 3,000 ft AMSL, speed ≤140 kt IAS, clear of cloud, flight visibility ≥1,500 m.
- (iii) Helicopter at or below 3,000 ft AMSL, speed reasonable for visibility, clear of cloud, in sight of surface.

### VFR Application (Annex 2, para 4.4)
VFR flights may take place with ATC clearance in the following Class C airspace:
- Hong Kong CTR.
- Hong Kong TMA above UCARAs up to 9,000 ft AMSL.
- Hong Kong TMA within 50 NM of CH DME between 2,000 ft and 8,000 ft AMSL (excluding the above).

VFR may also take place in Class G airspace:
- UCARAs from surface to notified upper limits.
- Hong Kong TMA within 50 NM of CH DME from surface to 2,000 ft AMSL (excluding the above).
- Airspace south of Hong Kong TMA from surface to 8,000 ft.

### Special VFR
- Special VFR may take place with ATC clearance within Hong Kong CTR.
- Special VFR is a flight in IMC or at night in a control zone, with permission from ATC, conducted clear of cloud and in sight of surface.
- VFR does not apply at night, but ATC may authorize Special VFR in a control zone.
- The Hong Kong CTR is notified for Rule 21 of Schedule 14 to the Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995.

### Exemptions
Calibration aircraft are exempt from these restrictions and may operate VFR for calibrating navigational aids.
