## Content
The Hong Kong AIP lists several activities of dangerous nature and potential hazards. In case of disasters, the Civil Aviation Department may establish temporary Danger Areas or impose statutory Restriction of Flying under Article 69 of The Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995, promulgated by NOTAM. Radio-sonde MET balloons are released by the Hong Kong Observatory twice a day at 1130 and 2330 UTC, plus once a week every Wednesday at 0530 UTC, from King's Park Meteorological Station (Hong Kong International Airport ARP 090º/27 km). The balloon is red, 1.5 m diameter at ground level, with a parachute and instrument box tethered 1.5 m and 60 m below, rising at about 1,200 ft per minute up to 90,000 ft. Penny's Bay Gas Turbine Power Station may produce high temperature plumes within a radius of 0.27 NM (0.5 km) of UTM Grid Ref JK952713, up to 1,500 ft AMSL (within VHR12). A laser and searchlight display takes place every evening from buildings along Central, Wanchai, Tsim Sha Tsui, and Kowloon Bay waterfronts from 1200 to 1213 UTC daily, with beams confined to 600 ft to 1,500 ft AMSL. Aircraft and helicopters must remain clear of the area bounded by UTM Grid references KK072671, KK079681, KK119713, KK130691, KK091667 then along the coastline to KK072671, up to 1,500 ft AMSL during the display, unless prior approval from the Director-General of Civil Aviation is obtained (except emergencies). Fireworks displays: at Hong Kong Disneyland, a 20-minute display every evening from 1100-1430 UTC, within a radius of 0.54 NM (1 km) of UTM Grid Ref JK956706, up to 1,100 ft AMSL (inside VHR12); at Victoria Harbour, displays lasting about 25 minutes on special occasions, with date, time, and affected airspace promulgated by NOTAM, and aircraft in CTR Island Zone must keep clear of the NOTAM area, and flights to/from HKIA shall not deviate over Victoria Harbour. The Tung Chung - Ngong Ping Cable Car operates at the southern perimeter of the airport, with the ropeway spanning Tung Chung Bay up to 694 ft AMSL between Tower 2B (190 ft AMSL) and Tower 3 (1,023 ft AMSL); helicopters shall maintain safe clearance. Local rock blasting is carried out at various locations, refer to AIP Supplement. A wind turbine at Lamma Island (22 13 32.16N 114 07 15.10E) has a height of 535 ft AGL. The Hong Kong Offshore Liquefied Natural Gas (HKOLNG) Terminal is located east of Soko Islands, near helicopter routes A2 and B1, with maximum height 83.9 m (275 ft AMSL); during operations, the closest distance between LNG carrier and Route B1 is about 374 m; aviation obstacle lights are installed; local VFR/SVFR traffic should avoid flying over the Terminal, and pilots should adhere to procedures in ENR 3.4. See [[ENR 5.3 Other Activities of Dangerous Nature]].
