## Transit Passenger Rules
Passengers holding stateless documents or nationals of countries listed in GEN 1.3 para 2.4.2 require transit visas to land in Hong Kong. Those remaining airside do not need visas, except nationals of Afghanistan, Angola, Bangladesh, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Congo (DRC), Congo (Republic), Cote d'Ivoire, Eritrea, Eswatini, Ethiopia, Gambia, Ghana, Guinea-Bissau, Iran, Iraq, Korea (DPRK), Liberia, Libya, Nepal, Nigeria, Pakistan, Rwanda, Sierra Leone, Somalia, South Sudan, Sri Lanka, Sudan, Syria, Togo, Yemen. Airlines must prevent such passengers from presenting to immigration. See [[Hong Kong Visa Policy]].

## Air Passenger Departure Tax
Under the Air Passenger Departure Tax Ordinance (Cap. 140), every passenger departing from Hong Kong International Airport must pay departure tax at rates approved by the Hong Kong Government, unless exempted. Details are in GEN 4.1 para 4.

## Air Traffic Services
Air traffic services in Hong Kong are administered by the Assistant Director-General of Civil Aviation (Air Traffic Management) under the Director-General. Services follow ICAO Annex 2, Annex 11, Doc 4444 (PANS-ATM), Doc 8168 (PANS-OPS), and Doc 7030. ATS is provided for the entire Hong Kong territory, including territorial waters and airspace over the South China Sea as listed in ENR 2.1. The airspace comprises a single area with no upper/lower distinction; all services are provided by one unit (Hong Kong ATC). A control zone is established at HKIA, with approach/zone/aerodrome control. Minimum flight altitude on ATS routes ensures at least 1,000 ft vertical clearance above the highest known obstacle within lateral limits.
