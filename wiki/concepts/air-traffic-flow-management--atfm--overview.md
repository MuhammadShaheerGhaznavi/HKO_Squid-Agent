## Content
ATFM aims to ensure an optimum flow of air traffic to/from Hong Kong International Airport (HKIA), Macao International Airport (MIA), and traffic overflying Hong Kong FIR through all sectors. It supports ATC objectives by ensuring traffic volume is compatible with airspace and airport capacity. Early anticipation of constraints and dissemination of ATFM measures assists upstream ACCs and Towers. Tactical measures handle adverse weather or disruptions, facilitating delay notification and diversion planning. The Hong Kong ATFM Unit (ATFMU) manages ATFM services, referencing ICAO Doc 9971, Ground Delay Programmes (GDP), and other tools. See [[ATFM Measures]] and [[Hong Kong ATFMU]].
