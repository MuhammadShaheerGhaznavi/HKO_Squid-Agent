## Content
Air Traffic Flow Management (ATFM) aims to ensure an optimum flow of air traffic to/from Hong Kong International Airport (HKIA), Macao International Airport (MIA), and traffic overflying Hong Kong FIR. It supports ATC objectives by ensuring traffic volume is compatible with airspace and airport capacity. Early anticipation of capacity constraints and dissemination of ATFM measures assists upstream ACCs and Towers. Tactical measures handle adverse weather or major disruptions, facilitating delay notification and diversion planning. ATFM measures are defined in ICAO's Manual on Collaborative ATFM (Doc 9971). The Hong Kong ATFM Unit (ATFMU) manages ATFM services in Hong Kong FIR, including Ground Delay Programmes (GDP), coordination, and notices. See [[ATFM Measures]] and [[Hong Kong ATFMU]].
