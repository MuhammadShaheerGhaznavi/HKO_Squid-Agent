## Content
The AIP Hong Kong GEN 2.2 section provides a comprehensive list of abbreviations used in aeronautical information publications, including both ICAO standard abbreviations and local non-ICAO ones (marked with an asterisk). Examples include: AAHK (Airport Authority Hong Kong), AIP (Aeronautical Information Publication), ATC (Air Traffic Control), ATIS (Automatic Terminal Information Service), CAD (Civil Aviation Department), GFS (Government Flying Service), HKIA (Hong Kong International Airport), and WTWS (Windshear and Turbulence Warning System). The list covers all aspects of aviation operations, from meteorological codes (e.g., METAR, SPECI, TAF) to communication and navigation terms (e.g., VHF, DME, ILS). It also includes time-related abbreviations (e.g., UTC, H24, HJ, HN) and operational terms (e.g., LVP, RVSM, PBN). This list is essential for interpreting AIP content and aeronautical messages.
