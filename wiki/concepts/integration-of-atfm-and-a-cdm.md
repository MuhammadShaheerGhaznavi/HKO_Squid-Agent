## Content
Airport Collaborative Decision Making (A-CDM) aims to improve operational efficiency by reducing delays, enhancing predictability, sharing timely data, and optimizing resources. A-CDM has been implemented at HKIA since **1 November 2018**. It uses an A-CDM system to track milestones and calculate accurate times for flight phases. Details are available at https://extranet.hongkongairport.com (select 'PROCEDURE MANUAL' then 'A-CDM Operations Guidelines'). ATFM and A-CDM are collaborative processes; their integration is essential for an accurate demand picture and effective decision making. AUs must maintain an updated Target Off Block Time (TOBT) to generate a Target Start-up Approval Time (TSAT), which helps ATC issue accurate CTOTs. See [[ATFM Overview]] and [[GDP CTOT Compliance Window]].
