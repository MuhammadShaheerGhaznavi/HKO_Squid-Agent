## Content
Airport Collaborative Decision Making (A-CDM) at HKIA since 1 Nov 2018 tracks milestones and calculates accurate times. ATFM and A-CDM are collaborative processes; integration is essential for accurate demand picture. AUs must maintain updated Target Off Block Time (TOBT) to generate Target Start-up Approval Time (TSAT) and for ATC to issue accurate CTOT. Details in A-CDM Operations Guidelines at extranet.hongkongairport.com. See [[ATFM Overview]].
