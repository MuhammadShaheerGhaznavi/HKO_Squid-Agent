## AIP Hong Kong GEN 2: Abbreviations, Chart Symbols, and Conversion Tables

This note consolidates information from three sections of the AIP Hong Kong GEN 2: abbreviations (GEN 2.2), chart symbols (GEN 2.3), and conversion tables (GEN 2.6).

### GEN 2.2 Abbreviations

The AIP Hong Kong GEN 2.2 section provides a comprehensive list of abbreviations used in aeronautical information publications, covering both ICAO standard abbreviations and local non-ICAO abbreviations (marked with an asterisk). Examples include:

- **AAHK** (Airport Authority Hong Kong)
- **AIMC** (Aeronautical Information Management Centre)
- **AOR** (Area of Responsibility)
- **CDC** (Clearance Delivery Control)
- **GFS** (Government Flying Service)
- **HKIA** (Hong Kong International Airport)
- **PARA** (Paragraph)
- **RAD** (Radar)
- **SKARA** (Shek Kong Aerodrome Reporting Area)
- **TTR** (Terminal Transition Route)
- **WTWS** (Windshear and Turbulence Warning System)
- **ZNC** (Zone Control)

The list is organized alphabetically and includes abbreviations for meteorological terms, communication types, and operational procedures. This list is essential for interpreting AIP content and NOTAMs. See also [[AIP Hong Kong Location Indicators]] and [[AIP Hong Kong Radio Navigation Aids]].

### GEN 2.3 Chart Symbols

The GEN 2.3 section of the AIP Hong Kong, titled 'Chart Symbols', currently contains the entry 'Nil' with an amendment date of 23 MAY 19 (Amendment 2/19). This indicates that there are no additional chart symbols specific to Hong Kong beyond those already defined in ICAO standards. The section is intentionally left blank, and users should refer to ICAO Annex 4 and Doc 8697 for standard chart symbols. This is a placeholder for future additions if needed.

### GEN 2.6 Conversion Tables

The GEN 2.6 section provides conversion tables for aviation use. Key conversion factors:

- 1 NM = 1.852 KM
- 1 KM = 0.54 NM
- 1 FT = 0.3048 M
- 1 M = 3.281 FT

The tables list conversions for values from 0.1 to 500 (for NM-KM and KM-NM) and 1 to 10,000 (for FT-M and M-FT). For example:

- 10 NM = 18.520 KM
- 100 KM = 54.00 NM
- 1000 FT = 304.800 M
- 1000 M = 3280.84 FT

Additionally, there are tables for converting decimal minutes of arc to seconds of arc (e.g., 0.01 min = 0.6 sec, 0.50 min = 30.0 sec) and seconds of arc to decimal minutes (e.g., 1 sec = 0.02 min, 30 sec = 0.50 min). These tables are essential for flight planning and navigation calculations.
