## Content
Hong Kong International Airport provides rescue and fire fighting vehicles per Annex 14 Attachment A, plus 2 rescue launches with foam/water capability and 8 high speed rescue boats, available H24. Runway surface condition is assessed and reported using the ICAO Global Reporting Format (GRF) whenever water or contaminants are present. Runway Condition Report (RCR) includes Runway Condition Code (RWYCC) and surface description per runway third. RWYCC values applicable in Hong Kong: 6 (DRY), 5 (WET up to 3 mm depth, braking GOOD), 3 (WET 'slippery wet', braking MEDIUM), 2 (STANDING WATER >3 mm, braking MEDIUM TO POOR). Communication channels: RWYCC 6 via air-ground voice on request, not on ATIS or SNOWTAM; RWYCC 5 via air-ground voice and ATIS, not SNOWTAM; RWYCC 3 via air-ground voice and ATIS, not SNOWTAM; RWYCC 2 via air-ground voice, ATIS, and SNOWTAM. Pilots must report worse braking action via special AIREP. GRF applies to VHHH only. Snow plan: Nil. See [[Aerodrome Availability and Operating Minima at Hong Kong International Airport]].
