## Content
The AIP includes a wide range of charts for aerodromes and heliports. For VHHH, these include Aerodrome Charts (ADC), Aircraft Parking/Docking Charts, Pavement Types and PCR Value, Taxiways/Taxilanes for Code F Aircraft, Aerodrome Obstacle Charts (AOC), Precision Approach Terrain Charts (PATC), Aerodrome Terrain and Obstacle Chart (Electronic), Area Charts for Departure and Arrival, SID and STAR charts, Instrument Approach Charts (IAC), Pushback Procedures, Control Zone (CTR) charts, VFR holding points, Helicopter landing locations, and lighting charts. For VMMC, there are SID and STAR charts. For VHSS, there are SID and IAC charts. These charts are essential for flight operations and are updated regularly.
