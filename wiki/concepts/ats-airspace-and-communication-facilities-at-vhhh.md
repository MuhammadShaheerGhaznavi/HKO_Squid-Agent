## Content
**Hong Kong ATZ**
- Lateral limits: straight lines joining 22 19 58N 113 51 11E, 22 21 45N 113 56 58E, 22 17 05N 113 58 41E, 22 15 24N 113 53 00E, back to start.
- Vertical: SFC to 2,000 ft AAL. Class C. Call sign: Hong Kong Tower. Transition altitude: 9,000 ft.

**Hong Kong CTR**
- Lateral limits: polygon with points including 22 28 34N 114 00 54E, 22 26 59N 114 00 25E, 22 25 51N 114 03 03E, 22 24 20N 114 04 10E, 22 25 15N 114 07 08E, 22 24 40N 114 10 40E, 22 22 40N 114 13 20E, 22 20 30N 114 13 25E, 22 15 15N 114 21 00E, 22 08 50N 114 21 00E, 22 08 50N 114 17 05E, 22 08 20N 114 15 20E, 22 08 50N 114 14 10E, 22 08 50N 114 11 45E, 22 06 40N 114 11 45E, 21 56 25N 113 48 00E, 22 13 44N 113 39 57E, 22 24 34N 113 49 44E.
- Vertical: SFC to 9,000 ft AMSL. Class C. Call signs: Hong Kong Tower/Zone/Approach. Transition altitude: 9,000 ft.
- CTR subdivided into 7 zones: DELTA, ISLAND, LANTAU, MA WAN, SOUTH OUTER, TUEN MUN, WAGLAN.

**ATS Communication Frequencies**
- **APP**: Hong Kong Approach 119.1 MHz (H24), 119.35 MHz (secondary); Departure 123.8 MHz (H24), 122.0 MHz (H24), 122.65 MHz (as advised), 124.05 MHz (secondary); Director 119.5 MHz (as advised), 123.225 MHz (secondary); Emergency 121.5 MHz (H24).
- **TWR**: Hong Kong Tower 118.2, 118.4, 118.7 MHz (all H24), 124.65 MHz (secondary), 121.5 MHz (emergency).
- **GMC**: Hong Kong Ground 121.6, 121.875, 122.125, 122.55, 122.6 MHz (all H24), 121.925 MHz (secondary).
- **ZNC**: Hong Kong Zone 120.6 MHz (H24), 122.075 MHz (secondary).
- **FIS**: Hong Kong Information 121.0 MHz (01:00-SS), 122.075 MHz (secondary).
- **CDC**: Hong Kong Delivery 122.15 MHz (H24), 121.925 MHz (secondary). 2-way PDC via data-link available.
- **ATIS**: Arrival 128.2 MHz (H24), Departure 127.05 MHz (H24). D-ATIS available.
