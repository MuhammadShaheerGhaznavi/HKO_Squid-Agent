## Content
The Hong Kong Aerodrome Traffic Zone (ATZ) is defined by straight lines joining points: 22 19 58N 113 51 11E, 22 21 45N 113 56 58E, 22 17 05N 113 58 41E, 22 15 24N 113 53 00E, back to start. Vertical limits: SFC to 2,000 ft AAL. Airspace classification C. ATS unit: Hong Kong Tower (English). Transition altitude: 9,000 ft. The Hong Kong Control Zone (CTR) is defined by a larger polygon, with vertical limits SFC to 9,000 ft AMSL, classification C. The CTR is subdivided into 7 zones: DELTA, ISLAND, LANTAU, MA WAN, SOUTH OUTER, TUEN MUN, and WAGLAN. ATS communication facilities include: Hong Kong Approach on 119.1 MHz (primary) and 119.35 MHz (secondary); Hong Kong Departure on 123.8 MHz, 122.0 MHz, 122.65 MHz (as advised), and 124.05 MHz (secondary); Hong Kong Director on 119.5 MHz and 123.225 MHz (secondary); Hong Kong Tower on 118.2, 118.4, 118.7 MHz, with 124.65 MHz secondary; Hong Kong Ground on 121.6, 121.875, 122.125, 122.55, 122.6 MHz, with 121.925 MHz secondary; Hong Kong Zone on 120.6 MHz (primary) and 122.075 MHz (secondary); Hong Kong Information on 121.0 MHz (01:00-SS) and 122.075 MHz (secondary); Hong Kong Delivery on 122.15 MHz (H24) with 2-way PDC data-link; ATIS on 128.2 MHz (arrival) and 127.05 MHz (departure), both H24 with D-ATIS available. Emergency frequency 121.5 MHz is available H24.
