## Content
ATC applies lateral separation minima for RNP 10 approved aircraft on segments of routes within Hong Kong FIR: P901 (BIGEX to IKELA) 50 NM, M772 (ASOBA to DULOP) 50 NM, Q1 (DULOP to CARSO) 50 NM. For RNP 4: L642 (BIGEX to EPKAL) 50 NM, M771 (DOSUT to BIGEX) 50 NM. Pilots must advise ATC of any deterioration or failure of navigation systems. For RNP 10, indicate 'R' in Item 10a and 'PBN/A1' in Item 18. For RNP 4, indicate 'RG' in Item 10a and 'PBN/L1' in Item 18. ADS-C and CPDLC are NOT required for RNP 4 within Hong Kong FIR. Aircraft not meeting RNP 10/4 must flight plan at FL280 or below; operations above FL280 require ATC approval, with coordination considering traffic density, communications, weather, and other factors.
