## Content
ATC applies lateral separation minima of 50 NM for RNP 10 approved aircraft on routes: P901 (BIGEX to IKELA), M772 (ASOBA to DULOP), and Q1 (DULOP to CARSO). For RNP 4 approved aircraft, 50 NM separation applies on L642 (BIGEX to EPKAL) and M771 (DOSUT to BIGEX). Pilots must advise ATC of any deterioration or failure of navigation systems. For RNP 10, pilots indicate 'R' in Item 10a and 'PBN/A1' in Item 18. For RNP 4, indicate 'RG' in Item 10a and 'PBN/L1' in Item 18. ADS-C and CPDLC are not required for RNP 4 within Hong Kong FIR.
