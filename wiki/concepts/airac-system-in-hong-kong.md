## Content
Operationally significant changes are published as AIP AMDT or AIP SUP on predetermined AIRAC dates. AIRAC information must be available at least 28 days in advance, and for major changes at least 56 days in advance. After publication, a Trigger NOTAM is issued stating the subject of changes and remains in force for 14 days after the AIRAC date. The AIRAC dates for 2026 are: 22 Jan, 19 Feb, 19 Mar, 16 Apr, 14 May, 11 Jun, 9 Jul, 6 Aug, 3 Sep, 1 Oct, 29 Oct, 26 Nov, 24 Dec. For 2027: 21 Jan, 18 Feb, 18 Mar, 15 Apr, 13 May, 10 Jun, 8 Jul, 5 Aug, 2 Sep, 30 Sep, 28 Oct, 25 Nov, 23 Dec. For 2028: 20 Jan, 17 Feb, 16 Mar, 13 Apr, 11 May, 8 Jun, 6 Jul, 3 Aug, 31 Aug, 28 Sep, 26 Oct, 23 Nov, 21 Dec. For 2029: 18 Jan, 15 Feb, 15 Mar, 12 Apr, 10 May, 7 Jun, 5 Jul, 2 Aug, 30 Aug, 27 Sep, 25 Oct, 22 Nov, 20 Dec.
