## Content
ENR 3.2 of the Hong Kong AIP states 'Nil' for upper ATS routes. This means there are no routes published in this category. The page is marked as intentionally left blank. Upper ATS routes are typically used for high-altitude traffic, but in Hong Kong FIR, such routes are not defined. See [[ATS Routes Overview]].
