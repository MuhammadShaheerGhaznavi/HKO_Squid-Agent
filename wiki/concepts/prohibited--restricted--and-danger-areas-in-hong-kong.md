## Content
Hong Kong has one prohibited area, three restricted areas, and several danger areas. Prohibited Area VHP8 (STANLEY FORT) covers sectors 090°-120° and 230°-270° at 221154N 1141304E within a radius of 3,000 ft (914.4 m), from GND to 1,000 ft ALT, active H24, due to a satellite earth station. Flights within prohibited areas are prohibited to civil aircraft, and deliberate infringement is an offence under the Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995. Restricted Area VHR7 (TUNGSHA (PRATAS) ISLAND) is a circle of 10 NM (18.5 km) radius centred at 2042N 11643E, from SFC to 5,000 ft ALT, active H24, for military firing, controlled by Taipei Civil Aeronautics Administration. Restricted Area VHR12 (PENNY'S BAY) is a circle of 1.35 NM (2.5 km) radius centred at 221855.56N 1140244.0E, from SFC to 4,000 ft ALT, active H24, for an entertainment park. No aircraft shall operate within VHR12 except for: aircraft flying in accordance with ATC instructions or published procedures for weather avoidance or technical deviation; aircraft of the Government Flying Service for fire fighting, fire prevention, life saving, casualty evacuation, or police operations; and aircraft granted specific exemption by the Director-General of Civil Aviation. Restricted Area VHR13 (KAU SHAT WAN) is a circle of 0.19 NM (350 m) radius centred at 221619.20N 1140113.80E, from SFC to 3,000 ft ALT, active H24, for an explosive depot. No aircraft shall operate within VHR13 except for emergency. Danger Areas include VHD5 (TSING SHAN FIRING RANGE) with coordinates 222653N 1135742E, 222448N 1135609E, 222454N 1135522E, 222337N 1135527E, 222243N 1135539E, 222233N 1135557E, 222228N 1135623E, 222358N 1135732E, 222613N 1135844E, 222653N 1135742E, from GND to 12,000 ft ALT, activity notified by NOTAM, for artillery firing; VHD11 (NINEPINS RANGE) with coordinates 222000N 1142600E, 222000N 1143000E, 221600N 1143000E, 221600N 1142600E, 222000N 1142600E, from SFC to 2,000 ft ALT, activity notified by NOTAM, for police firing; and ZJ(D) 208 with coordinates 201116N 1110004E, 202054N 1114452E, 194210N 1115408E, 193233N 1110928E, 201116N 1110004E, from 6,000 m to 15,000 m, daily 0030-1200, for ground to air firing. Nomenclature: areas are named with 'VH' for Hong Kong, followed by 'P' for Prohibited, 'R' for Restricted, or 'D' for Danger, and a numeric sequence. Numbers are not reused. See [[ENR 5.1 Prohibited, Restricted and Danger Areas]].
