## Content
All flights in controlled airspace in the Hong Kong FIR must be flown in accordance with Instrument Flight Rules (IFR), except local flights as specified in ENR 1.2. A flight plan must be submitted for every IFR flight, except as notified in ENR 1.10. These procedures align with ICAO Regional Supplementary Procedures (DOC 7030).
