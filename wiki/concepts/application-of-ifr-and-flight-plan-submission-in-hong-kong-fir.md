## Content
In the Hong Kong FIR, all flights in controlled airspace must be conducted in accordance with Instrument Flight Rules (IFR), except for local flights as specified in ENR 1.2. A flight plan must be submitted for every IFR flight, except as notified in ENR 1.10. This ensures standardized procedures for air traffic control and safety.
