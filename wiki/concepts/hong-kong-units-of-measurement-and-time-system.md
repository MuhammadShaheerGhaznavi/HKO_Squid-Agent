## Content
Hong Kong uses units of measurement in accordance with ICAO Annex 5. Commonly used units include:
- Distance in navigation: Nautical Miles (NM) (1 NM = 1,852 m)
- Relatively short distances (e.g., runway length): Metres (m)
- Altitudes, elevations, heights: Feet (ft) or Metres (m)
- Horizontal speed including wind: Knots (kt)
- Vertical speed: Feet per minute (ft/min)
- Wind direction for take-off/landing: Degrees Magnetic (°M)
- Wind direction except take-off/landing: Degrees True (°T)
- Visibility (including RVR): Metres or Kilometres (m, km)
- Barometric pressure: Hectopascals (hPa)
- Temperature: Degrees Celsius (°C)
- Weight: Tonnes or Kilogrammes (t, kg)
- Time: Hours and minutes, day of 24 hours beginning at midnight UTC (hhmm)

Time system: UTC is used in air traffic and communication services and in documents published for international distribution. Hong Kong Standard Time is 8 hours ahead of UTC. In NOTAM and aeronautical documents, all-numeric date/time is presented as yymmddtttt (year-month-day-time). Year digits may be omitted if no confusion arises.
