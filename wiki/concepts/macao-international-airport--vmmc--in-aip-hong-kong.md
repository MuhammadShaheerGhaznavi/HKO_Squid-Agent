## Content
Macao International Airport (VMMC) is located in the Macao Special Administrative Region, People’s Republic of China. Its aeronautical information is included in AIP Hong Kong to comply with ICAO Annex 15 and Doc 10066 PANS-AIM requirements, specifically to publish SID, STAR, and associated flight procedures that transit Hong Kong airspace. Hong Kong ATC provides ATS surveillance service to aircraft on departure, approach, and missed approach to/from Macao International Airport while they are transiting Hong Kong airspace. For other aeronautical information and flight procedures outside Hong Kong airspace, refer to AIP Macao and/or AIP China. Most administrative data (AD 2.3 to AD 2.17) is referenced to AIP Macao, with only flight procedures and communication facilities detailed in AIP Hong Kong.
