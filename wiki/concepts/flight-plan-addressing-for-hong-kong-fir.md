## Content
Flight movement messages (including flight plan, amendment, and cancellation messages) for traffic into or via the Hong Kong FIR must be addressed correctly for proper relay. For all flights (IFR/VFR) inbound to Hong Kong International Airport or transiting the Hong Kong FIR, the message address is VHHKZQZX. For all flights outbound from Hong Kong International Airport, the address is VHHHZPZA. This is per ICAO PANS-ATM DOC 4444, Chapter 11, para 11.2.1.1.3. See [[Flight Plan Submission Requirements]].
