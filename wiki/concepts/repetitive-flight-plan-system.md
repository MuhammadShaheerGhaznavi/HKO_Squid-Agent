## Content
A repetitive flight plan system following ICAO PANS-ATM DOC 4444 is available for flights between: (a) Taibei/Gaoxiong and Hong Kong, (b) Jakarta and Hong Kong, and (c) Kuala Lumpur and Hong Kong. When filing a repetitive flight plan, operators must include RVSM approval status: 'EQPT/W' for RVSM approved, or 'STS/NONRVSM' for non-RVSM capable of FL280 or above, regardless of requested flight level. See [[Special Flight Plan Markings for Cargo, General Aviation, and RNAV/RVSM/ADS-B]].
