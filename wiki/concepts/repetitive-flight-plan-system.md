## Content
A repetitive flight plan system following ICAO PANS-ATM DOC 4444 is available for flights between Taibei/Gaoxiong and Hong Kong, Jakarta and Hong Kong, and Kuala Lumpur and Hong Kong. When filing a repetitive flight plan, operators must include RVSM approval status: 'EQPT/W' in Item Q for flights with RVSM approval, or 'STS/NONRVSM' for flights without RVSM approval capable of operating at FL280 or above, regardless of requested flight level. See [[Flight Plan Equipment and Performance-Based Navigation (PBN) Requirements]].
