## Content
BOBCAT is an automated ATFM service under ICAO Bay of Bengal ATS Coordination Group. Bangkok ATFMU provides service for westbound flights transiting Kabul FIR between 2000-2359 UTC daily, including CTOT, flight level, route, and CTO at entry waypoint. Exemptions: emergencies, SAR/RFF, urgent medical evacuation, Head of State, or other State-identified. Exempted flights file STS/ATFMX. Slot requests via https://www.bobcat.aero between 0000-1159 UTC on day of flight. See [[AMNAC]] and [[BOBCAT Operating Procedures]].
