# Airspace Structure and Procedures in Hong Kong FIR

## PBN Routes

PBN routes are listed in ENR 3.3 and include L642 (RNP 4), M750 (RNAV 5), M771 (RNP 4), M772 (RNP 10), P901 (RNP 10), and Q1 (RNP 10). These routes have specific navigation performance requirements and are one-way routes.

- **L642**: Runs from EPKAL (175130.00N 1125718.00E) via ENBOK, EPDOS to BIGEX. Track 219°M initially, upper limit UNL, lower limit 8000 ft AMSL, Class A, FL series Even.
- **M750**: Runs from KILOG to ENVAR. Track 090°M, upper limit UNL, lower limit FL270, Class A, FL series Odd. Applicable only under radar environment with ATC monitoring navigation performance.
- **M771**: Runs from DOSUT via DULOP, DUMOL to BIGEX. Track 037°M, upper limit UNL, lower limit 8000 ft AMSL, Class A, FL series Odd.
- **M772**: Runs from ASOBA to DULOP. Track 001°M, upper limit FL460, lower limit FL285, Class A.
- **P901**: Runs from IKELA via IDOSI to BIGEX. Track 039°M, upper limit UNL, lower limit FL285, Class A, FL series Odd/Even.
- **Q1**: Runs from DULOP to CARSO. Track 015°M, upper limit UNL, lower limit 8000 ft AMSL, Class A, FL series Odd.

Compulsory reporting points for all aircraft include EPKAL, DOSUT, DULOP, ASOBA, IKELA, and ENVAR; other waypoints are compulsory for non-jet aircraft only. For RVSM airspace, refer to FLAS in ENR 1.8 para 8. See [[ATS Routes Overview]].

## Helicopter Routes

The Hong Kong FIR has two types of helicopter routes: Oil Rig Support Helicopter Routes and Hong Kong - Macau Helicopter Routes. Only helicopter operators authorized by the Director-General of Civil Aviation may use these routes. The routes are used for IFR and VFR operations, with specific tracks and controlling units.

### Oil Rig Support Helicopter Routes

- **Track H (IFR/VFR)**: From helicopter base via HENGA, AOTOU to SESAN. Controlled by Hong Kong Information (122.4 MHz primary, 122.075 MHz secondary).
- **Track D (Secondary IFR)**: From ZUH VOR via ROMEO to DELTA. Controlled by Hong Kong Radar (123.95 MHz primary, 132.225 MHz secondary).
- **Track VW (VFR)**: From helicopter base via ZAO DVOR, QUBEC, HVC02, HVW01 to VICTOR WHISKEY. Controlled by Hong Kong Zone (120.6 MHz primary, 122.075 MHz secondary).
- **Track VH (Secondary VFR)**: From helicopter base via HENGA, PINGSHAN, along Eastern Coast of DAPENG WAN to DAPENG. Controlled by Hong Kong Information (122.4 MHz primary, 122.075 MHz secondary).

**Vertical limits** vary by track and distance from BIGEX:
- For VFR, less than 50 NM from BIGEX:
  - Track H: 2,000 ft AMSL or less
  - Track VH (base to rig): 2,000 ft AMSL or less
  - Track VW (ZAO-QUBEC): 500 ft AMSL or less (RWY 07) or 800 ft AMSL or less (RWY 25)
  - Track VW (QUBEC-VW): 1,000 ft AMSL or less
  - Track VW (VW-rig): 2,000 ft AMSL or less
- For 50 NM or greater from BIGEX: VFR is below 8,000 ft AMSL at appropriate cruising levels, and oil rig support helicopters shall not normally be flown at or above 8,000 ft AMSL in controlled airspace.
- For IFR:
  - Track H (base to rig): 5,000 ft AMSL
  - Track H (rig to base): 4,000 ft AMSL
  - Track D (base to rig): 4,000 ft AMSL (PRI) or 6,000 ft AMSL (SRY subject to coordination)
  - Track D (rig to base): 5,000 ft AMSL

**Reporting points** for oil rig routes include MCU DVOR (116.4 MHz), ZAO DVOR (117.2 MHz), ZUH VOR (116.7 MHz), AOTOU, DAPENG, DELTA, HENGA, HOTEL, HVC02, HVW01, PINGSHAN, QUBEC, ROMEO, SESAN, UNIFORM, VICTOR WHISKEY, with coordinates as listed.

### Hong Kong - Macau Helicopter Routes

There are VFR/SVFR routes A, A2, B1, C1 and IFR routes J and L.

- **Route A** (Sky Shuttle Heliport - GREEN ISLAND - CHEUNG CHAU BUOY - FAN LAU - TANGO - Macao Heliport): VFR/SVFR standard westbound, specifically approved operators only.
- **Route A2** (Sky Shuttle Heliport - HOROT - LEVKE - WAVOS - ZEXEK - CHAKO - HASAN - FATUT - FOVOG - GOGRE - Macao Heliport): SVFR only.
- **Route B1** (Macao Heliport - UNIFORM - HVB01 - HVB02 - HVB03 - HVB04 - CHEUNG CHAU SOUTH - GREEN ISLAND - Sky Shuttle Heliport): VFR only.
- **Route C1** (Macao Heliport - QUBEC - HVC02 - HVC03 - LIGHTHOUSE - WAYPOINT 2 - CHEUNG CHAU SOUTH - GREEN ISLAND - Sky Shuttle Heliport): VFR/SVFR standard eastbound SVFR route.

**Maximum altitudes** for these routes depend on runway in use and operating conditions:
- For Route A, A2, B1, and C1 with RWY 07: VFR/SVFR is 500 ft AMSL.
- For Route C1 with RWY 07: SVFR is 1,000 ft AMSL.
- For Route C1 with RWY 25: VFR/SVFR is 1,200 ft AMSL.

**Communication** for VFR/SVFR is via Hong Kong Zone 120.6 MHz, or Hong Kong Approach 119.1 MHz, or Hong Kong Departure 123.8 MHz as instructed.

**IFR routes J and L** have specific reporting points (KEMTE, PEARL, QUBEC, WALIN) and altitude restrictions:
- Route J: From Macao Heliport to MCU R116/DME 1 NM is visual not above 500 ft; from MCU R116 to WALIN is 1,800 ft.
- Route L: From KEMTE to TD R225 is 1,600 ft; from TD R225 to MCU R116/DME 1.1 NM is 2,000 ft; from MCU R116/DME 1.1 NM to Macao Heliport is visual not above 500 ft.

**Operating procedures** for IFR routes include: only specifically approved operators, authorisation from Hong Kong Control Tower Supervisor and Macao Control Tower by fax or telephone prior to departure, only one helicopter at a time on both routes, use of published instrument departure or approach procedures, serviceable SSR transponder, not normally used during peak periods of Macao Airport traffic, and no deviation from published procedures due to low altitudes. See [[ENR 3.4 Helicopter Routes]] for details.
