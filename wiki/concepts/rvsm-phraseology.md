## Content
Recommended phraseology for RVSM operations includes: 'CONFIRM RVSM APPROVED' (controller to ascertain approval status), 'Negative RVSM' (pilot reports non-approval on initial call, in requests for level changes, and in read-backs; also for vertical transit through F290/F410 except State aircraft), 'AFFIRM RVSM' (pilot reports approval), 'NEGATIVE RVSM STATE AIRCRAFT' (pilot of non-approved State aircraft), 'UNABLE CLEARANCE INTO RVSM AIRSPACE, MAINTAIN [or DESCEND TO, or CLIMB TO] FLIGHT LEVEL (number)' (controller denies clearance), 'UNABLE RVSM DUE TURBULENCE' (pilot reports severe turbulence), 'UNABLE RVSM DUE EQUIPMENT' (pilot reports equipment degradation), 'READY TO RESUME RVSM' (pilot reports ability to resume), and 'REPORT ABLE TO RESUME RVSM' (controller confirms readiness).
