## Content
Part 3 (AD) of the AIP Hong Kong provides comprehensive information on aerodromes and heliports. It is divided into three main sections: AD 1 (General), AD 2 (Aerodromes), and AD 3 (Heliports).

- **AD 1** covers general aerodrome information including availability, rescue and fire fighting services, runway surface condition assessment, snow plan, index to aerodromes, grouping, and certification status.
- **AD 2** details specific aerodromes, with the primary focus on Hong Kong International Airport (VHHH) and Macau International Airport (VMMC). Each aerodrome section includes subsections such as location, geographical data, operational hours, handling services, passenger facilities, rescue and fire fighting, aprons, taxiways, markings, obstacles, meteorological info, runway characteristics, declared distances, lighting, ATS airspace, communication facilities, radio navigation aids, local traffic regulations, noise abatement, flight procedures, additional info, and related charts.
- **AD 3** covers heliports, specifically Sky Shuttle Heliport (VHSS), with similar subsections tailored for heliports.

The table of contents lists numerous charts, including aerodrome charts, obstacle charts, approach charts, SIDs, STARs, and more. For VHHH, there are detailed SIDs for various runways (07R, 25L, 07C, 25C, 07L, 25R) and STARs, as well as instrument approach charts for ILS, LOC, and RNP approaches. The document is regularly amended, with the latest amendment noted as 7/26 (effective 9 JUL 26).
