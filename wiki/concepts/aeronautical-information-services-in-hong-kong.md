## Aeronautical Information Services (AIS)

The Aeronautical Information Service in Hong Kong is provided by the Aeronautical Information Management Centre (AIMC), a section of the Air Traffic Management Division of the Civil Aviation Department. The International NOTAM office is an integral part of the AIMC. The service complies with ICAO Annex 15, Doc 8126, and Doc 10066. The AIMC is responsible for collecting and disseminating information for the Hong Kong Flight Information Region (FIR).

Key publications include:
- AIP Hong Kong (English only)
- AIP Amendments (AIP AMDT) issued on AIRAC dates
- AIP Supplements (AIP SUP) for temporary changes
- NOTAM (Series A for international, Series C for local)
- Aeronautical Information Circulars (AIC)

All products are available at http://www.ais.gov.hk.

Contact details: AIMC, Civil Aviation Department Headquarters, 1 Tung Fai Road, Hong Kong International Airport, Lantau; Tel +852 2910 6174; Fax +852 2910 1180; AFS VHHHYNYX; email aic@cad.gov.hk.

## Aeronautical Charts

Aeronautical charts in Hong Kong are produced by the Assistant Director-General of Civil Aviation (Air Traffic Management) under the Director-General. They comply with ICAO Annex 4 and Doc 8697. Charts are updated via AIP amendments, and new charts are notified by AIC. All charts are available at www.ais.gov.hk.

The chart series include:
- Aerodrome Chart - ICAO (1:15,000)
- Aerodrome Chart - Visual Markings (1:15,000)
- Aerodrome Chart - Lighting Plan (1:15,000)
- Aircraft Parking/Docking Chart - ICAO (1:10,000)
- Aerodrome Obstacle Chart - ICAO Type A (1:15,000 horizontal, 1:1,500 vertical), Type B (1:20,000)
- Precision Approach Terrain Chart (1:2,500)
- En-route Chart (1:3,500,000)
- Area Charts (1:2,500,000)
- Instrument Approach Charts (1:500,000 to 1:250,000)
- SID/STAR charts (various scales)
- ATC Surveillance Minimum Altitude Chart (1:800,000)

Topographical charts are available from the Lands Department, including Hong Kong Helicopter Flying Chart (1:50,000) and Hong Kong Local Flying Chart (1:100,000). Contact: Map Publications Centre, 6/F North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong; Tel +852 2231 3187; Fax +852 2116 0774; email smosale1@landsd.gov.hk.

## Air Traffic Services (ATS)

Air traffic services in Hong Kong are administered by the Assistant Director-General of Civil Aviation (Air Traffic Management) under the Director-General. Services comply with ICAO Annex 2, Annex 11, Doc 4444 (PANS-ATM), Doc 8168 (PANS-OPS), and Doc 7030. ATS is provided for the entire Hong Kong territory, including territorial waters and airspace over the South China Sea as listed in ENR 2.1. The airspace comprises a single area with no upper/lower distinction; all services are provided by Hong Kong ATC. A control zone is established at Hong Kong International Airport, with approach/zone/aerodrome control. Minimum flight altitudes on ATS routes ensure at least 1,000 ft vertical clearance above obstacles.

Contact: Air Traffic Management Division, Civil Aviation Department Headquarters, 1 Tung Fai Road, Hong Kong International Airport; Tel 2910 6501; Fax 2910 0186; AFS VHHKZQZX; email enquiry@cad.gov.hk.

## Communication Services

Communication services in Hong Kong are provided by the Assistant Director-General of Civil Aviation (Air Traffic Management) in accordance with ICAO Annex 10, Doc 8400, Doc 8585, Doc 7030, Doc 7910, and Doc 9880. Radio navigation aids include ILS, VOR, DME, and various surveillance radars (RSR, ASR, TSR, RSSR, ASSR, TSSR). The Aeronautical Network Centre (ANC) provides automatic message switching via AFTN/ATN on H24 basis. Hong Kong is a Regional OPMET Centre under the ROBEX scheme.

Broadcasting services include:
- HF VOLMET
- VHF ATIS (arrival 128.20 MHz, departure 127.05 MHz)
- Continuous VHF VOLMET for Shenzhen, Macau, Taibei, and Gaoxiong on 128.875 MHz

Data link services include:
- D-ATIS (VHHHA for arrival, VHHHD for departure)
- D-VOLMET
- 2-way PDC via ACARS (AEEC Spec 623)

ATIS hotlines: Arrival +852 3141 2820, Departure +852 3141 2705.

Private Communication Network service allows authorized operators to access AMHS web server for message exchange, flight plan submission, and PIB download; contact PCCW for subscription (Tel +852 2888 3492) and support (Hotline +852 2888 7180). English is the only language used in aeronautical communication.

## Terminal Transition Routes

Terminal transition routes in Hong Kong are designated with 'J' (e.g., J101, J103, J104) and 'V' (e.g., V10, V11, V12, V31, V32, V512, V522, V525, V533, V534, V542, V551, V561, V571, V591, V601, V611, V621, V631, V642, V652, W29) prefixes. These routes connect en-route waypoints to terminal areas, with tracks in degrees magnetic and distances in NM.

Examples:
- J101 runs from ELATO to SIU MO TO (SMT) DVOR/DME via TUBBY, SAMMI, NEDLE, PONTI, BUMDI, with tracks around 269-286°M and distances varying from 8.3 to 70.3 NM.
- J103 connects DUMOL to BEKOL via ALDOM, ISBAN, ROBIN, TAPPO, SAPAX.
- V10 connects SIKOU to PECAN via DAGBU and ALLEY.

Controlling units are Hong Kong Radar with specific PRI and SRY frequencies per segment. Some routes are for ATC tactical use only (e.g., V525, V534, W29) and shall not be used for flight planning. Compulsory reporting points are noted for all aircraft (e.g., ELATO, BEKOL, SIKOU, DOTMI, NOMAN, SABNO, LELIM, ENVAR) or non-jet aircraft only.
