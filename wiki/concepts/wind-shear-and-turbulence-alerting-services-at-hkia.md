## Content
The Wind Shear and Turbulence Warning System (WTWS) integrates data from TDWR, LIDARs, anemometers, and weather buoys to detect wind shear and turbulence within 3 NM of each runway threshold and over the runway. Alerts are updated at least once per minute. Alert types: Microburst Alert (RWY orientated wind speed loss ≥30 kt with precipitation), Wind Shear Alert (wind speed gain ≥15 kt, or loss 15-29 kt, or loss ≥30 kt without precipitation), and Turbulence Alert (moderate or severe intensity for HEAVY aircraft). Severe turbulence is alerted when the cubic root of eddy dissipation rate (EDR) ≥0.5; moderate when ≥0.3. Alerts are passed by ATC preceded by 'caution', e.g., '(call sign) caution microburst minus 30 kt on final approach'. When multiple alerts occur, a priority system consolidates them, e.g., microburst takes precedence over wind shear. Wind shear loss alerts have higher priority than gain alerts. Pilots are advised to avoid alert areas, especially with gains/losses ≥30 kt. Warnings on ATIS are given as 'forecast' or 'forecast and reported' (if confirmed by pilot reports in last 30 minutes). Microburst warnings are given without such qualifiers. Examples: 'Microburst and significant wind shear 07L and 07R'.
