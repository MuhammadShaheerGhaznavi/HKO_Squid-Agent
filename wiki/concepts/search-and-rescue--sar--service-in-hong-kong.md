## Content
The search and rescue (SAR) service in Hong Kong is provided by the Civil Aviation Department (CAD) for all civil and military aircraft within the Hong Kong FIR. It operates in accordance with ICAO Annex 12 (Search and Rescue), Annex 13 (Aircraft Accident Investigation), and Doc 7030 (Regional Supplementary Procedures). A Rescue Co-ordination Centre (RCC) provides H24 service. Initial communication on SAR matters, including Cospas-Sarsat data, should be addressed to the ATC Watch Manager at the Hong Kong Air Traffic Control Centre (tel: +852 2910 6821, fax: +852 2910 1177, AFTN: VHHHYCYX or VHHHZQZX). The RCC's contact details are: tel +852 2910 6813, fax +852 2910 1188, AFS VHHHYCYX, email rcc@cad.gov.hk. SAR services are provided within the Hong Kong FIR. Locally based helicopters and light aircraft are deployed in cooperation with other government departments; for long-range SAR over high seas, assistance comes from adjacent SAR centres. SAR facilities include helicopters (equipped with floats, searchlights, winches), fixed-wing MRG aircraft (with multimode surveillance radar, electro-optical infrared system, droppable dinghies), fire/rescue launches (operating within 5 km of airport), and mountain rescue units. No formal facilitation of entry agreement exists, but coordination is done via inter-area speech circuits. Specific SAR aircraft are not immediately available, but other organisations provide assistance on request. Procedures for pilots observing accidents or intercepting distress calls are in Annex 12 Chapter 5; meteorological info provision is in Annex 3 Chapter 10; distress message handling per Annex 10 Volume II Chapter 5 para 5.3; abbreviations per Doc 8400. ATS units guard 121.5 MHz continuously; Coast Station (VPS) guards maritime distress frequencies H24. SAR aircraft call sign is normally pilot's call sign or registration preceded by 'Rescue'; Hong Kong RCC call sign is 'Hong Kong Rescue'. SAR signals are per Annex 12 Appendix.
