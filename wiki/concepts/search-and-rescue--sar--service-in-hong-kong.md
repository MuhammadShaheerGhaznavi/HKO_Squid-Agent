## Content
The search and rescue (SAR) service in Hong Kong is provided by the Civil Aviation Department (CAD) and is responsible for all civil and military aircraft within the area where Hong Kong provides Air Traffic Services. The service is in accordance with ICAO Annex 12 (Search and Rescue), Annex 13 (Aircraft Accident Investigation), and Doc 7030 (Regional Supplementary Procedures). A Rescue Co-ordination Centre (RCC) provides a H24 SAR service. For SAR operations, qualified personnel are deployed through the Air Traffic Control Centre. Enquiries and initial communication on SAR matters, including Cospas-Sarsat data, should be addressed to the ATC Watch Manager at the Hong Kong Air Traffic Control Centre (telephone +852 2910 6821, fax +852 2910 1177, AFTN VHHHYCYX or VHHHZQZX). The RCC contact details are: postal address Rescue Co-ordination Centre, Air Traffic Management Division, CAD Headquarters, 1 Tung Fai Road, Hong Kong International Airport, Lantau; telephone +852 2910 6813; fax +852 2910 1188; AFS VHHHYCYX; email rcc@cad.gov.hk; website http://www.hkatc.gov.hk. The area of responsibility is the Hong Kong FIR. Within territorial limits, locally based helicopters and light aircraft are deployed with other government departments; for long-range SAR over high seas, assistance comes from adjacent SAR centres. No formal Facilitation of Entry Agreement exists with adjacent states, but coordination is done via inter-area speech circuits. Specific SAR aircraft and surface vessels are not immediately available, but other organisations provide assistance on request. Procedures for pilots-in-command observing an accident or intercepting a distress call are in Annex 12 Chapter 5; meteorological information provision is in Annex 3 Chapter 10; distress message handling is per Annex 10 Volume II Chapter 5 paragraph 5.3. Abbreviations and codes from ICAO Doc 8400 are used. ATS units guard 121.5 MHz continuously; Coast Station (VPS) operates H24 and guards maritime distress frequencies. SAR aircraft call sign is normally pilot's personal call sign or registration preceded by 'Rescue'; Hong Kong RCC call sign is 'Hong Kong Rescue'. SAR signals are those in the Appendix of Annex 12. SAR facilities include: helicopters (HEL-M) at Hong Kong Airport equipped with floats, searchlights, winches; fixed-wing MRG aircraft at Hong Kong Airport with multimode surveillance radar, electro-optical infrared system, droppable dinghies; fire/rescue launch at Hong Kong Airport (within 5 km); mountain rescue units in Hong Kong; additional craft and personnel available on request from other government departments.
