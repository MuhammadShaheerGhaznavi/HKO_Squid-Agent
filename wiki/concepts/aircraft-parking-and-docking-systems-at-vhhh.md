## Content
Frontal parking stands have continuous yellow nosewheel guidance lines for wide-body aircraft. Some stands also accommodate narrow-body aircraft at a location displaced 9 m to the right, indicated by dashed yellow lines, with 'R' suffix (e.g., S25R). These include South Apron stands S1R, S2R, S3R, S25R, S27R, S29R, S31R, S33R, S35R, S41R, S43R, S45R, S47R, S49R; North Apron N6R, N7R, N24R, N26R, N28R, N30R, N32R, N34R, N60R, N62R, N64R, N66R, N68R, N70R; West Apron W40R, W42R, W44R, W46R, W48R, W61R, W63R, W65R, W67R, W69R, W71R. Remote parking stands on South and North aprons, except S109, N145, R13-R21, can accommodate wide or narrow-body. West apron remote stands can accommodate up to 5 wide-body or 7 narrow-body. Midfield apron stands D301-D310 can accommodate up to 10 wide-body or 18 narrow-body. Narrow-body locations on West apron have 'L' or 'R' suffixes (e.g., W121L, W123R). Stand W126 is self-manoeuvring, only up to Code C. All frontal parking stands are equipped with Safegate Docking System for wide-body aircraft, allowing parking without marshaller.
