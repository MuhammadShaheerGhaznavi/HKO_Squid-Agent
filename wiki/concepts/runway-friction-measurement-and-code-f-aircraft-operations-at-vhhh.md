## Content
Runway surface friction at Hong Kong is measured using a Surface Friction Tester Vehicle at 95 km/h with a self-watering device providing 1 mm water depth. If friction value falls to 0.34 or less, the runway is notified as liable to be slippery when wet. In such conditions, take-off or landing should only be considered if distances available equal or exceed those required for slippery conditions per the Aeroplane Flight Manual. Pilots experiencing significant braking degradation should report to ATC. All runways at VHHH can support Code F aircraft operations, but not all taxiways/taxilanes are available; refer to chart AD 2-VHHH-ADC-6.
