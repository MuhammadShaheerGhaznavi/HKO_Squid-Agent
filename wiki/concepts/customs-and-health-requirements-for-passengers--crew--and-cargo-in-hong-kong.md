## Content
## Customs Requirements

Hong Kong is a free port with no customs tariff on imports/exports, but excise duties apply to liquors, tobacco, hydrocarbon oils, and methyl alcohol.

### Passengers and Crew
- Duty-free concessions: passengers aged 18+ may bring 1 litre of alcoholic liquor above 30% ABV (if HKID holder, must have been outside HK for 24+ hours).
- Tobacco: 19 cigarettes, or 1 cigar or 25g of cigars, or 25g of other manufactured tobacco.

### Cargo
- Customs formalities follow Annex 9 of the Chicago Convention.
- Goods requiring import licences include: narcotic drugs, firearms and ammunitions, dangerous goods, endangered species, strategic commodities, reserved commodities, pesticides, radioactive substances, pharmaceutical products, textiles, and ozone-depleting substances.
- Contact Trade Department or Customs & Excise Department for details.

## Health Requirements

### Aircraft
- Strict compliance with WHO International Health Regulations and Prevention and Control of Disease Ordinance (Cap. 599).
- Aircraft disinfection must use WHO-approved methods.
- Pilots must report seriously ill or dead persons on board by radio, and for HK-registered aircraft, comply with Civil Aviation (Births, Deaths and Missing Persons) Regulations 1984.

### Cargo
- Fish, shellfish, vegetables, or beverages from cholera-infected areas must be declared to health authorities before unloading.
- Under Section 14(1) of the Prevention and Control of Disease Regulation (Cap. 599A), importing human corpses, infectious agents, or suspected contaminated tissues/fluids requires a permit from the Director.
