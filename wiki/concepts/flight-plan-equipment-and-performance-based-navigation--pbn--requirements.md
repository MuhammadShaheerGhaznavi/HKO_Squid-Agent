## Content
Operators must include specific information in flight plan fields for various approvals. For RNP10: include 'R' in Item 10a and 'A1' after 'PBN/' in Item 18, plus true Mach number and flight level at entry/exit points. For RNP4: include 'RG' in Item 10a and 'L1' after 'PBN/'. For RNP1: include 'RGDI' in Item 10a and 'O1' or 'O2' after 'PBN/'; exempted categories must specify STS/ (e.g., STS/HUM, STS/SAR, STS/STATE, STS/HEAD, STS/FLTCK) and reasons in RMK for categories d) to f). For RNAV5: include 'RGODI' or 'RGSDI' in Item 10a and 'B1', 'B2', 'B3', 'B4', or 'B5' after 'PBN/' (S indicates standard equipment including VOR). For RNAV2: include 'R' in Item 10a and 'C1' or 'C2' after 'PBN/'. For RNAV1: include 'RDGI' in Item 10a and 'D1' or 'D4' after 'PBN/'. For RNP-AR APCH: include 'T1' or 'T2' after 'PBN/'. RVSM: Hong Kong airspace between FL290 and FL410 inclusive is RVSM; insert 'W' in Item 10 for RVSM approval; non-RVSM aircraft capable of FL280+ must insert 'STS/NONRVSM' in Item 18. ADS-B: insert 'B1' for ADS-B out, 'B2' for ADS-B out and in. Cargo flights must include 'RMK/CARGO' in Item 18; general aviation parking at Business Aviation Centre must include 'RMK/BAC PARKING'.
