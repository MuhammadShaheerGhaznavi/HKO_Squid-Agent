## Content
All flights to or from Hong Kong must comply with relevant laws. Fixed-wing aircraft must use Hong Kong International Airport. Subsonic jet aircraft require a noise certificate per the Civil Aviation (Aircraft Noise) Ordinance (Cap. 312), meeting Chapter 3 standards of Annex 16. The certificate must be carried and produced on request. Insurance under the Civil Aviation (Insurance) Order (Cap. 448 sub. leg. F) requires a Combined Single Limit (CSL) covering third-party, passenger, baggage, cargo, and mail liabilities, with minimum amounts based on aircraft weight: up to 5,700 kg – US$15M; 5,700–10,000 kg – US$25M; 10,000–28,000 kg – US$60M; 28,000–100,000 kg – US$200M; 100,000–170,000 kg – US$500M; over 170,000 kg – US$1B. Insurance must be per occurrence, and proof must be current. Non-compliance prevents landing/takeoff except in emergencies.
