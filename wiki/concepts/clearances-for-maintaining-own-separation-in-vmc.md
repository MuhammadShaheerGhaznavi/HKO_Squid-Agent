## Content
An ATCU may grant an IFR flight a clearance to maintain own separation and remain in visual meteorological conditions (VMC) only when requested by the pilot, and only for climb, descent, or approach portions of the flight by day. In Hong Kong, such clearances are granted only if the aircraft is at or below FL150.
