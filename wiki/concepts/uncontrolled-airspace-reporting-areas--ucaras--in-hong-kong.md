## Content
Six Uncontrolled Airspace Reporting Areas (UCARAs) are established: Mirs Bay, North Border, New Town, Ninepins, Port Shelter, and Tolo. Airspace classification is Category G with additional requirement for two-way radio communication. Vertical limits: New Town, Port Shelter, Ninepins: surface to 2,000 ft AMSL; Mirs Bay and Tolo: surface to 3,000 ft AMSL; North Border: surface to 2,000 ft AMSL (normally restricted to 1,000 ft or below, prior permission for above). Flight notification must be submitted to Hong Kong Aerodrome Control. Pilots must report position and altitude when entering/leaving each UCARA and every 15 minutes. When Hong Kong Information is not available, broadcast on 121.0 MHz. Flights in North Border require coordination with Zhuhai ATC, with at least 2 minutes notice at or below 1,000 ft, 3 minutes above. Radio communication failure: fixed-wing proceed to Shek Kong Aerodrome, helicopters land at suitable site and contact ATC by phone. An Aerobatic Area is established in northern portions of Mirs Bay and Tolo, available daily 0900-1700 local time, vertical limits SFC-3,500 ft AMSL, aircraft must remain below 3,000 ft and request clearance to 3,500 ft. Flight Information Service (Hong Kong Information) on 121.0 MHz primary, 122.075 MHz secondary, provides information and alerting service between 0900 local time and sunset; alerting only between sunset and 0859.
