## Content
The safety criteria for reduced lateral separation minima of 60 NM require RNP10 navigation performance, with standard deviation of lateral track errors less than 8.7 km (4.7 NM). Monitoring of aircraft navigation performance is a joint responsibility of operators, States, regulatory authorities, and ATS providers. Non-conformance is detected via radar monitoring: lateral deviations of 15 NM or more from track centreline; longitudinal deviations of 3 minutes or more when time separation is applied, or 10 NM or more when distance-based standards are used. ATC will advise pilots of deviations and investigate with the operator and State.
