## Content
The nationality mark for aircraft registered in Hong Kong is the letter 'B'. It is followed by a hyphen and a registration mark consisting of a three-letter group. The first letter of the registration mark is 'H', 'K', or 'L', representing Hong Kong. Registration marks are issued within the combinations 'HAA' to 'HZZ', 'KAA' to 'KZZ', and 'LAA' to 'LZZ'.

Public holidays are notified annually in Aeronautical Information Circulars.
