## Content
The Wind Shear and Turbulence Warning System (WTWS) integrates data from TDWR, LIDARs, anemometers, and weather buoys to detect wind shear and turbulence within 3 NM of runway thresholds and over runways, updating alerts at least every minute. Alert types: Microburst Alert (RWY orientated wind speed loss ≥30 kt with precipitation), Wind Shear Alert (gain ≥15 kt, loss 15-29 kt, or loss ≥30 kt without precipitation), Turbulence Alert (moderate or severe turbulence, with severe when cubic root of eddy dissipation rate (EDR) ≥0.5, moderate ≥0.3). Alerts are passed by ATC preceded by 'caution', e.g., 'caution microburst minus 30 kt on final approach'. When multiple alerts occur, a priority system consolidates them, e.g., microburst takes precedence over wind shear. Wind shear and turbulence warnings are also broadcast on ATIS, with 'forecast' or 'forecast and reported' labels. Pilots are advised to avoid alert areas, especially with gains/losses ≥30 kt.
