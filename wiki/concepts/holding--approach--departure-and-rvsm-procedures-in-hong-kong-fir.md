## Holding, Approach, and Departure Procedures in Hong Kong FIR

- Holding procedures are based on ICAO DOC 8168 Fourth Edition 1993.
- Entry into holding patterns uses the three-sector entry procedure.
- Turns are made at a bank angle of 25° or 3° per second, whichever requires lesser bank.
- Pilots must reach assigned level before or at holding fix to avoid step-descents.
- Expected Approach Time (EAT) or Onward Clearance Time (OCT) is given for delays of 15 minutes or more, prior to entering holding pattern.
- Speed control is in force unless cancelled; arriving and departing aircraft fly at 250 KIAS or less below 10,000 ft AMSL.
- Minimum inter-arrival spacing at HKIA is 3.0 NM, subject to wake turbulence separation.
- Pilots unable to comply with speed restrictions must inform Approach Control on first contact.
- See [[ENR 3.6 En-route Holding]] for details.

## RVSM Airspace Procedures in Hong Kong FIR

- Within the Hong Kong FIR, controlled airspace from FL290 to FL410 inclusive is designated as RVSM airspace.
- Operators must obtain airworthiness and operational approval from the State of registry or State of the Operator.
- The FAA maintains a website for RVSM approval information: http://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/separation_standards/parmo/.
- Before entering RVSM airspace, pilots must review equipment status; required equipment includes:
  - Two primary altimetry systems
  - One automatic altitude-keeping device
  - One altitude-alerting device
- Pilots must notify ATC if the aircraft is no longer RVSM compliant due to:
  - Equipment failure
  - Loss of redundancy of altimetry systems
  - Turbulence affecting the ability to maintain flight level
- During climb or descent, aircraft should not exceed the assigned level by more than 150 ft.
- Except in a radar environment, pilots must report reaching any level assigned within RVSM airspace.
