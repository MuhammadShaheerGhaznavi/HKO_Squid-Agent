## Content
The GEN 2.6 section provides conversion tables for common aviation units. Key conversion factors: 1 NM = 1.852 km, 1 km = 0.54 NM, 1 ft = 0.3048 m, 1 m = 3.281 ft. The tables list conversions for values from 0.1 to 500 (for NM/km) and 1 to 10,000 (for ft/m). For example, 10 NM = 18.520 km, 100 km = 53.99 NM, 1000 ft = 304.8 m, and 1000 m = 3280.84 ft. Additionally, there are tables for converting decimal minutes of arc to seconds of arc (e.g., 0.01 min = 0.6 sec, 0.50 min = 30.0 sec) and seconds of arc to decimal minutes (e.g., 1 sec = 0.02 min, 30 sec = 0.50 min). These tables are crucial for flight planning, navigation, and altitude conversions.
