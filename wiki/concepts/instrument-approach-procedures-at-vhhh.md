## Content
VHHH offers a variety of instrument approach procedures for its three runways. For runway 07R, there are ILS, LOC, RNP Z (AR), and RNP Y (AR) approaches. Runway 25L has ILS, LOC, RNP Z (AR), and RNP Y (AR). Runway 07C has ILS or LOC, and RNP (LNAV/VNAV only). Runway 25C has ILS, LOC, RNP Z (LNAV/VNAV only), and RNP Y (AR). Runway 07L has ILS or LOC, and RNP (LNAV/VNAV only). Runway 25R has ILS, LOC, RNP Z (LNAV/VNAV only), and RNP Y (AR). Additionally, there is an approach transition chart for RNAV transition to ILS runway 25R. These procedures are documented in the AIP as instrument approach charts (IACs) with specific identifiers.
