## Content
Hong Kong ATFMU or other APAC ATFMUs target to distribute CTOTs at least 90 minutes before the Estimated Off-Block Time (EOBT) of constrained flights. For GDP for Airport Constraint (HKIA & MIA), the CTOT compliance window is **-5/+10 minutes**. For GDP for Waypoint or Airspace Constraint, the window is **-5/+5 minutes**. ATC will release departures as close as possible to the assigned CTOT. Compliance is essential to realize the ATFM plan and reduce tactical airborne delay. To secure a CTOT, operators must file FPLs at least 3 hours before EOBT, adhering to procedures in ENR 1.10 para 2. Late FPL submission may result in additional delay. See [[ATFM Measures]] and [[Coordination during GDP]].
