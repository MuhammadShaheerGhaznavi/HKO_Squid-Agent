## Content
All published geographical co-ordinates within Hong Kong airspace are expressed in terms of World Geodetic System - 1984 (WGS-84) geodetic reference datum. The area of application coincides with the entire Hong Kong FIR.

Vertical reference system: Hong Kong Principal Datum (HKPD) is used, which is about 1.30 metres below Mean Sea Level (MSL) as at 2016. The geoid model used is HKGEOID2016_SMO, corresponding to HKPD for GNSS heighting. Altitudes and elevations in AIP Hong Kong are measured from MSL as per ICAO definition.

Differences between orthometric heights referenced to EGM-96 and HKGEOID2016_SMO depend on geographic location; a map shows undulation differences in Hong Kong.
