## Content
Mach Number Technique may be applied between turbo-jet aircraft to establish required longitudinal separation minima, whether in level, climbing, or descending flight. When ATC issues a clearance based on this technique, the pilot must maintain the specified Mach Number. If it becomes infeasible to maintain the last assigned Mach number, pilots must advise ATC and request re-clearance. The technique is used on ATS Routes A1, A461, A583, B330, G86, and R473, and on PBN Routes L642, M771, and P901.
