## Content
Mach Number Technique may be applied between turbo-jet aircraft to establish required longitudinal separation minima, whether in level, climbing, or descending flight. When ATC issues a clearance based on this technique, the pilot must maintain the specified Mach Number. If unable to maintain the last assigned Mach number, pilots must advise ATC and request re-clearance. The technique may be used on ATS routes A1, A461, A583, B330, G86, and R473, and on PBN routes L642, M771, and P901.
