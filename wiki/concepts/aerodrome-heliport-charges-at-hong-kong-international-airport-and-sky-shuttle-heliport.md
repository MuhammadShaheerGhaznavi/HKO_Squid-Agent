## Content
The following charges apply to Hong Kong International Airport (HKIA) and, where applicable, to Sky Shuttle Heliport: (a) landing charge, (b) parking charge, (c) airbridge charge, (d) terminal building charge, (e) air passenger departure tax, and (f) other charges (customs, immigration, security). Charges a) to d) are collected by the Airport Authority Hong Kong under the Airport Authority Ordinance (Cap 483), with details gazetted in G.N. 3341 dated 17 June 2016, available at http://www.gld.gov.hk/egazette. Charges collected by Sky Shuttle Heliport should be referred to the heliport operator (see VHSS AD 3.2). Under the Air Passenger Departure Tax Ordinance (Cap.140), every passenger departing from Hong Kong by aircraft at HKIA or Sky Shuttle Heliport must pay a departure tax at rates approved by the Hong Kong Government, unless exempted. Operators must collect the tax prior to departure and pay it to the Government; unless satisfactory arrangements are made, payment in cash is required prior to aircraft clearance. 'Operator' means the person having control over the management of the aircraft, including a properly appointed agent. Further details are available at https://www.hongkongairport.com/en/passenger-guide/departures/air-passenger-departure-tax.page. Other charges for customs, immigration, or security services may apply depending on flight operations; enquiries should be directed to the appropriate authority listed in GEN 1.1 paras 3, 4, and 6.
