## Content
ATS routes in Hong Kong FIR are listed in ENR 3.1, with each route defined by designator, significant points (with coordinates), magnetic track, distance, upper/lower limits, airspace classification, lateral limits, FL series, and controlling unit frequencies. Routes include A1, A202, A461, A470, A583, B330, G581, G86, and several terminal transition routes (J101, J103, J104, V10, V11, V12, V31, V32, V512, V522, V525, V533, V534, V542, V551, V561, V571, V591, V601, V611, V621, V631, V642, V652, W29). Most routes have upper limit UNL and lower limit 8000 ft AMSL, Class A airspace, lateral limits 50 NM (some 12 NM), and are controlled by Hong Kong Radar with primary and secondary frequencies. Compulsory reporting points are designated for all aircraft (e.g., IKELA, ELATO, SIKOU, BEKOL, NOMAN, DOTMI, TAMOT, SABNO, KAPLI, LELIM, ENVAR, etc.), while other waypoints are compulsory for non-jet aircraft only. Some routes are one-way (e.g., A461 northbound only, B330 southbound normally, G86 eastbound only). For RVSM airspace (FL290-410), refer to FLAS in ENR 1.8 para 8.
