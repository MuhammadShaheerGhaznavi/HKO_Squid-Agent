## Content
Under Schedule 6 of the Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995, Hong Kong International Airport is notified for ILS equipment carriage, requiring all aircraft making an approach to landing to carry ILS-capable radio unless exempted (fixed-wing and helicopters ≤2,730 kg in local flying). The Hong Kong Control Zone and Terminal Area are notified for SSR transponder carriage (Mode A and C, 4096 codes, ICAO Annex 10 Vol IV). Other notifications include: radio frequencies in ENR 2.1 (Article 35(2)); licensed aerodromes in AD 1.1 (Articles 71(1)(a) and 73); Class A and C airspace in ENR 2.1 (Article 98); controlled airspace in ENR 2.1; danger areas in ENR 5.1; ILS and SSR requirements in GEN 1.5 (Article 14, Schedule 6); flight in Class A airspace in ENR 1.2 and 1.3 (Rule 21); altimeter setting in ENR 1.7 (Rule 26); holding and approach procedures in ENR 1.5 (Rule 27(3)); position reports in ENR 1.1 (Rule 28); and ATC radio frequencies at aerodromes in VHHH AD 2.18 (Rule 35(2)). See [[Aircraft Radio and Navigation Equipment Requirements in Hong Kong FIR]] for detailed equipment requirements.
