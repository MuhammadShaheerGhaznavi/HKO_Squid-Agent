## Visual Flight Rules (VFR) in Hong Kong

VFR flights may take place subject to ATC clearance within Class C airspace, including:
- The Hong Kong CTR
- The Hong Kong TMA above the UCARAs from their notified upper limits up to 9,000 ft AMSL
- The Hong Kong TMA within 50 NM of BIGEX between 2,000 ft and 8,000 ft AMSL (excluding the above)

VFR may also take place in Class G airspace. Special VFR may take place subject to ATC clearance within the Hong Kong CTR.

### VFR Weather Minimums
VFR flights must be conducted in visibility and distance from cloud equal to or greater than:
- **Class C and G airspace:** flight visibility 5 km, distance from cloud 1,500 m horizontally and 1,000 ft vertically.
- **At or below 3,000 ft:** remain clear of cloud and in sight of surface.
- **Aircraft other than helicopters at or below 3,000 ft at airspeed 140 kt or less:** flight visibility 1,500 m and remain clear of cloud.
- **Helicopters at or below 3,000 ft at a reasonable speed:** remain clear of cloud and in sight of surface.

### Special VFR
VFR does not apply to flights at night, but ATC may authorize Special VFR within a control zone. A Special VFR flight is a flight made in IMC or at night in a control zone, with permission from the appropriate ATC unit to follow special instructions instead of IFR; such flights must be conducted clear of cloud and in sight of the surface.

The Hong Kong CTR is notified for Rule 21 of Schedule 14 to the Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995. Calibration aircraft are exempted from these restrictions and may operate under VFR for calibrating navigational aids.

## Instrument Flight Rules (IFR) in Hong Kong

For flights within controlled airspace, Rules 25, 27, and 28 of the Rules of the Air, Schedule 14 to the Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995 apply. For flights outside controlled airspace, Rules 25 and 26 apply.

All flights within controlled airspace notified for Rule 21 are subject to IFR in all weather conditions. An aircraft flying at night shall be flown in accordance with IFR unless ATC authorizes a Special VFR flight.

The Terminal Control Area (TMA) from 8,000 ft AMSL to unlimited, as defined in ENR 2.1, and all ATS Routes and PBN Routes as defined in ENR 3.1 and ENR 3.3 respectively, are notified for Rule 21.
