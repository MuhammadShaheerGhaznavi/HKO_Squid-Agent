## Content
Rules are contained in Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995. Aircraft may not deviate from current flight plans without prior ATC approval, except in emergency necessitating immediate action. In such emergencies, inform ATC as soon as possible. ATC clearances and instructions may not be deviated from except in emergency. Deviation from standard instrument approach or departure procedures requires prior ATC approval. If visual reference is established before completing an instrument approach, the entire procedure must be flown until clearance to change flight path is granted by ATC.
