## Content
The rules for flight under IFR and VFR are contained in the Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995. Aircraft may not deviate from current flight plans without prior ATC approval, except in an emergency necessitating immediate action. In such emergencies, pilots must inform ATC as soon as possible. Similarly, ATC clearances and instructions may not be deviated from except in an emergency. Deviation from standard instrument approach or departure procedures is also prohibited without prior ATC approval. If visual reference is established before completing an instrument approach, the entire procedure must be flown until clearance to change flight path is requested and granted by ATC.
