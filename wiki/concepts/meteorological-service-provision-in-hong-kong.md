## Content
The Meteorological Authority for Hong Kong is the Hong Kong Observatory (HKO), designated under ICAO Annex 3 para. 2.1.4. The HKO establishes the Airport Meteorological Office (AMO) as the Meteorological Service Provider (para. 2.1.5) for Hong Kong International Airport (HKIA). The AMO comprises three resilient offices: the South AMO (S-AMO) at Level T3 of the South Aerodrome Control Tower (S-TWR), the Ancillary AMO (AAMO) at Level T2 of the North Aerodrome Control Tower (N-TWR), and the North AMO (N-AMO) at Level T2 of the New Air Traffic Control Tower (NAT). The AMO serves as the meteorological watch office for the Hong Kong FIR, issuing SIGMETs, and as the aerodrome meteorological office for HKIA. Services are provided in accordance with ICAO Annex 3, Doc 10157 (PANS-MET), and other regional documents, with differences detailed in GEN 1.7.
