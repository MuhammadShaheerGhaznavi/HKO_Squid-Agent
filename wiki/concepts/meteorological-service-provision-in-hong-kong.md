## Content
The Hong Kong Observatory (HKO) is the Meteorological Authority for Hong Kong, providing meteorological facilities and services for international air navigation as per ICAO Annex 3. The Airport Meteorological Office (AMO) is the designated Meteorological Service Provider for Hong Kong International Airport (HKIA), with three resilient offices: South AMO (S-AMO) at Level T3 of the South Aerodrome Control Tower (S-TWR), Ancillary AMO (AAMO) at Level T2 of the North Aerodrome Control Tower (N-TWR), and North AMO (N-AMO) at Level T2 of the New Air Traffic Control Tower (NAT). The AMO serves as the meteorological watch office for the Hong Kong FIR, issuing SIGMETs, and as the aerodrome meteorological office for HKIA. Services comply with ICAO Annex 3, Doc 10157 (PANS-MET), and other regional documents.
