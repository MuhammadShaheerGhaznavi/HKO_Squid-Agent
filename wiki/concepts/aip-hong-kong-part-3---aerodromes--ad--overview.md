## Content
Part 3 of the AIP Hong Kong, published by the Civil Aviation Department, contains detailed information about aerodromes and heliports. It is divided into three main sections: AD 1 (General), AD 2 (Aerodromes), and AD 3 (Heliports). AD 1 covers general topics such as aerodrome availability, rescue and fire fighting services, and certification status. AD 2 provides detailed data for each aerodrome, including Hong Kong International Airport (VHHH) and Macau International Airport (VMMC). AD 3 covers heliports, such as Sky Shuttle Heliport (VHSS). The document includes a comprehensive table of contents listing all subsections and charts. Amendments are issued regularly, with the latest being Amendment 7/26 effective 9 JUL 26.
