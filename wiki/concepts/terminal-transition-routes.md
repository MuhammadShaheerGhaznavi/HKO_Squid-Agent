## Content
Terminal transition routes are listed in ENR 3.1 (2) and include J101, J103, J104, V10, V11, V12, V31, V32, V512, V522, V525, V533, V534, V542, V551, V561, V571, V591, V601, V611, V621, V631, V642, V652, and W29. Each route lists significant points with coordinates, magnetic track, and distance in NM. For example, J101 runs from ELATO (222000.00N 1173000.00E) via TUBBY, SAMMI, NEDLE, PONTI, BUMDI to SIU MO TO (SMT) DVOR/DME, with tracks around 269-286°M and distances varying from 8.3 to 70.3 NM. J103 connects DUMOL to BEKOL via ALDOM, ISBAN, ROBIN, TAPPO, SAPAX, with track 358°M. J104 runs from SIKOU to CHALI via RAGSO, DASON, COTON. V10 connects SIKOU to PECAN via DAGBU and ALLEY. V11 and V12 connect IDOSI/EPDOS to PECAN via SURFA/LAXET. V31 and V32 connect IDOSI/EPDOS to ALLEY. V512 and V522 connect ABBEY to DOTMI/ELATO via FISHA, ENPET, MAGOG. V525 and V533 connect BETTY to ELATO via SONNY, DAKTO, EATON, AKEKU (V525) or via UNTUL, OSUMO (V533). V534 connects NOMAN to BETTY via UNTUL, OSUMO, HOCKY, CYBER. V542 connects SABNO to BETTY via TOFEE, SUKER, HOCKY, CYBER. V551 connects CARSO to BETTY via SUKER, HOCKY, CYBER. V561 connects IDOSI to CANTO via MYWAY, GAMBA, MAPLE, COMBI, ROCCA. V571 connects SIKOU to CANTO via GAMBA, MAPLE, COMBI, ROCCA. V591 connects ABBEY to LELIM via FISHA, ENPET, MAGOG, MALKA. V601 and V611 connect LEKEN to DOTMI/LELIM via LARIT, NUDPI, SOUSA (and BESDA, LIMSU for V611). V621 and V631 connect DALOL to ELATO/ENVAR via DUMEP, MEBKI, CONGA. V642 connects NOMAN to DALOL via NOPER, SKATE, XEMEK. V652 connects SABNO to DALOL via UNTUL, RUSBI, POVAM, SKATE, XEMEK. W29 connects BETTY to MEPUT via SONNY, DAKTO, EATON. Routes V525, V534, and W29 are for ATC tactical use only and shall not be used for flight planning. Compulsory reporting points for all aircraft are noted (e.g., ELATO, BEKOL, SIKOU, DOTMI, NOMAN, SABNO, LELIM, ENVAR), while other waypoints are compulsory for non-jet aircraft only.
