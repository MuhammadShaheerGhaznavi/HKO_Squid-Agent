## Content
The units of measurement used in all air and ground operations in Hong Kong comply with ICAO Annex 5. Commonly used units include: distance in nautical miles (NM) for navigation, metres for short distances like runway length; altitudes, elevations, and heights in feet or metres; horizontal speed in knots; vertical speed in feet per minute; wind direction for take-off and landing in degrees magnetic, otherwise degrees true; visibility in metres or kilometres; barometric pressure in hectopascals; temperature in degrees Celsius; weight in tonnes or kilogrammes; time in hours and minutes UTC. One international nautical mile equals 1,852 metres.

Time system: Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) is used in air traffic services and international documents. Hong Kong Standard Time is UTC+8. In NOTAM and messages, date/time is presented as yymmddtttt (year-month-day-time), with year digits often omitted when no confusion arises.
