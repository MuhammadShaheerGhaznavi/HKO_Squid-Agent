## Content
**ILS/DME per Runway**
- **RWY 07L**: LOC IZSL 111.55 MHz (CAT III), GP IZSL 332.75 MHz (CAT III), DME IZSL CH 52Y (co-located with GP). GP unusable beyond 6° left of course.
- **RWY 25R**: LOC ITFR 108.75 MHz (CAT I), GP ITFR 330.35 MHz (CAT I), DME ITFR CH 24Y. GP unusable below elevation angle 1.8°. LOC unusable beyond 16° right of course.
- **RWY 07C**: LOC IZSC 109.3 MHz (CAT II), GP IZSC 332 MHz (CAT II), DME IZSC CH 30X. LOC unusable beyond 22° left and 26° right of course.
- **RWY 25C**: LOC ITFC 109.3 MHz (CAT III), GP ITFC 332 MHz (CAT III), DME ITFC CH 30X. LOC unusable beyond 19° right of course.
- **RWY 07R**: LOC IZSR 110.9 MHz (CAT II), GP IZSR 330.8 MHz (CAT II), DME IZSR CH 46X. LOC unusable beyond 19° right of course.
- **RWY 25L**: LOC ITFL 110.9 MHz (CAT II), GP ITFL 330.8 MHz (CAT II), DME ITFL CH 46X. LOC unusable beyond 28° left and 22° right of course; GP unusable beyond 7° left of course.

**DVOR/DME**
- SMT: 114.8 MHz, CH 95X, at 222015.43N 1135855.46E. Coverage not less than 25 NM with restrictions: unusable between radials 041-051 beyond 15 NM; 051-071 beyond 15 NM below 7,000 ft; 112-155 beyond 20 NM below 9,000 ft; 155-216 beyond 13 NM below 12,000 ft; 216-301 beyond 15 NM; 301-041 beyond 10 NM.
- TD: 116.1 MHz, CH 108X, at 221452.42N 1141735.30E. Coverage not less than 200 NM at 35,000 ft and above.
