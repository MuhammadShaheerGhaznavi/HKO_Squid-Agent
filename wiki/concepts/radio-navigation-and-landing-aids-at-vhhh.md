## Content
Hong Kong International Airport has ILS and DME for all runways, plus two DVOR/DME aids. For RWY 25R: ILS CAT I (LOC 108.75 MHz, GP 330.35 MHz, DME CH 24Y), ID ITFR, GP 3.1° nominal, unusable below 1.8° elevation. For RWY 25L: ILS CAT II (LOC 110.9 MHz, GP 330.8 MHz, DME CH 46X), ID ITFL, GP 3° nominal, LOC unusable beyond 28° left and 22° right, GP unusable beyond 7° left. For RWY 25C: ILS CAT III (LOC 109.3 MHz, GP 332 MHz, DME CH 30X), ID ITFC, GP 3° nominal, LOC unusable beyond 19° right. For RWY 07R: ILS CAT II (LOC 110.9 MHz, GP 330.8 MHz, DME CH 46X), ID IZSR, GP 3° nominal, LOC unusable beyond 19° right. For RWY 07C: ILS CAT II (LOC 109.3 MHz, GP 332 MHz, DME CH 30X), ID IZSC, GP 3° nominal, LOC unusable beyond 22° left and 26° right. For RWY 07L: ILS CAT III (LOC 111.55 MHz, GP 332.75 MHz, DME CH 52Y), ID IZSL, GP 3° nominal, GP unusable beyond 6° left. DVOR/DME SMT at 114.8 MHz (CH 95X) at 222015.43N 1135855.46E, with coverage restrictions (e.g., unusable between radials 041 and 051 beyond 15 NM). DVOR/DME TD at 116.1 MHz (CH 108X) at 221452.42N 1141735.30E, coverage not less than 200 NM at 35,000 ft and above. TD DME is critical for RNAV operations.
