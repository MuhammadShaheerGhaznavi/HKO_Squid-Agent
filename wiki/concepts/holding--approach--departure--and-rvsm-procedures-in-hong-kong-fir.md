## Holding, Approach, and Departure Procedures

Holding procedures in the Hong Kong FIR are based on ICAO Doc 8168 Fourth Edition 1993. Pilots must cross-check positions to remain within the FIR. An Expected Approach Time (EAT) or Onward Clearance Time (OCT) is given for delays of 15 minutes or more, prior to entering the holding pattern. Terminal holding entry uses the three-sector entry procedure, with turns at a bank angle of 25° or 3° per second, whichever requires lesser bank. Pilots must reach assigned level before or at the holding fix.

SID, STAR, and Terminal Transition Routes (TTR) are established for Hong Kong and Macao. Unless otherwise instructed, aircraft must maintain a minimum rate of 500 feet per minute during level changes within the FIR. Speed control is in force unless cancelled; arriving and departing aircraft fly at 250 KIAS or less below 10,000 ft AMSL. The minimum inter-arrival spacing at HKIA is 3.0 NM, subject to wake turbulence separation. Pilots unable to comply with speed restrictions must inform Approach Control on first contact, especially if final approach speed is below 125 KIAS.

## RVSM Airspace Procedures

Within the Hong Kong FIR, controlled airspace from FL290 to FL410 inclusive is designated as Reduced Vertical Separation Minima (RVSM) airspace. Operators must obtain airworthiness and operational approval from the State of registry or State of the Operator. The FAA maintains a website for RVSM approval information: http://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/separation_standards/parmo/.

Before entering RVSM airspace, pilots must ensure the following equipment is operating normally: two primary altimetry systems, one automatic altitude-keeping device, and one altitude-alerting device. Pilots must notify ATC if the aircraft is no longer RVSM compliant due to equipment failure, experiences loss of redundancy of altimetry systems, or encounters turbulence affecting the capability to maintain flight level.

During climb or descent, aircraft should not exceed the assigned level by more than 150 ft. Except in a radar environment, pilots shall report reaching any level assigned within RVSM airspace. RVSM procedures are suspended when there are pilot reports of greater than moderate turbulence, and vertical separation minima become 2,000 ft.
