## Content
Under the Civil Aviation (Insurance) Order (Cap. 448 sub. leg. F), all civil aircraft (commercial or non-revenue) must have Combined Single Limit (CSL) insurance. The CSL must cover liabilities for third party, passenger, baggage, cargo, and mail, but may exclude items if the operator declares in writing that the aircraft does not carry them. Coverage must be per occurrence. Minimum CSL amounts are tiered by aircraft maximum ramp or taxi weight (or max take-off weight if not applicable): not exceeding 5,700 kg – US$15,000,000; exceeding 5,700 kg but not exceeding 10,000 kg – US$25,000,000; exceeding 10,000 kg but not exceeding 28,000 kg – US$60,000,000; exceeding 28,000 kg but not exceeding 100,000 kg – US$200,000,000; exceeding 100,000 kg but not exceeding 170,000 kg – US$500,000,000; exceeding 170,000 kg – US$1,000,000,000. Aircraft not complying are not allowed to land or take off, except in emergencies. Operators must provide documentary proof from the insurance company and keep it current, providing evidence of continued cover before expiry. The order is available at https://www.elegislation.gov.hk/.
