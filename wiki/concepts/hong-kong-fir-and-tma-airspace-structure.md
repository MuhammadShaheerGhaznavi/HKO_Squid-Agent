## Content
The Hong Kong FIR is bounded by coordinates: 23 40 00N 117 30 00E, 21 00 00N 117 30 00E, 16 40 00N 114 00 00E, 19 30 00N 111 30 00E, 21 25 00N 111 30 00E, then along the limit of territorial waters of the PRC (3 NM off-shore) and the northern boundary of Macao and HKSAR back to start. It extends from SFC to UNL, with airspace classes A, C, and G. The service unit is Hong Kong ACC (call sign HONG KONG RADAR) on frequencies including 118.925, 121.3, 122.95, 123.475, 123.7, 123.95, 125.175, 125.325, 125.8, 126.3, 126.5, 127.1, 127.55, 128.125, 128.75, 129.9, 132.15, 132.225, 132.525, 132.6, 132.775, 132.8, 133.7, 133.825, 134.3, 135.6 MHz, with emergency frequencies 121.5 MHz, 5655, 8942, 13309 kHz. HONG KONG RCC provides SAR on 123.1 MHz and 3023 kHz. The Hong Kong TMA has similar lateral limits but excludes the CTR and UCARAs within 50 NM from BIGEX. It has Class A airspace from UNL to 8000 ft (or 9000 ft above CTR and UCARAs), and Class C from 8000 ft to 2000 ft. Frequencies include 119.1, 119.35, 119.5, 122.0, 122.65, 123.225, 123.475, 123.7, 123.8, 123.95, 124.05, 125.175, 125.325, 125.8, 126.3, 126.5, 127.1, 127.55, 128.125, 128.75, 129.9, 132.15, 132.225, 132.525, 132.6, 132.775, 132.8, 133.7, 133.825, 134.3 MHz. Service is provided by Hong Kong ACC/APP, H24.
