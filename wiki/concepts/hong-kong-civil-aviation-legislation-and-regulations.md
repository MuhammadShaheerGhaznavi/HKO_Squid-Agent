## Content
Hong Kong civil aviation is governed by several key pieces of legislation:
- **Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995 (Cap. 448C)**: Highest operating regulation, supported by CADs, HKARs, and AICs.
- **Civil Aviation Ordinance (Cap. 448)**: Primary legislation implementing Chicago Convention, covering registration, airworthiness, licensing.
- **Air Transport (Licensing of Air Services) Regulations (Cap. 448A)**: Governs scheduled and non-scheduled air services.
- **Air Navigation (Dangerous Goods) Regulations (Cap. 448C Sch. 16)**: Regulates carriage of dangerous goods by air.
- **Air Navigation (Flight Prohibition) Order (Cap. 448E)**: Establishes prohibited airspace.
- **Hong Kong Air Navigation (Fees) Regulations (Cap. 448D)**: Prescribes fees for various certificates and licences.
- **Civil Aviation (Insurance) Order (Cap. 448F)**: Insurance requirements for aircraft landing/taking off in Hong Kong.
- **Dangerous Goods (Consignment by air) (Safety) Regulations (Cap. 384A)**: Controls preparation, packing, marking, labelling of dangerous goods.
- **Civil Aviation (Aircraft Noise) (Certification) Regulations (Cap. 312A)**: Noise certificates for aircraft.
- **Aviation Security Regulations (Cap. 494A)**: Permit system for restricted areas.
- **Air Passenger Departure Tax Ordinance (Cap. 140)**: Imposes departure tax.
- **Prevention and Control of Disease Ordinance (Cap. 599)**: Disease control, implements International Health Regulations.
- **Civil Aviations (Births, Deaths and Missing Persons) Ordinance (Cap. 173)**: Records of births/deaths on Hong Kong registered aircraft.
- **Hong Kong Airport (Control of Obstructions) Ordinance (Cap. 301)**: Controls building heights near airport.
- **Civil Aviation (Aircraft Noise) Ordinance (Cap. 312)**: Controls aircraft noise emission.
- **Airport Authority Ordinance (Cap. 483)**: Establishes Airport Authority Hong Kong for Chek Lap Kok airport.
- **Aviation Security Ordinance (Cap. 494)**: Implements Tokyo, Hague, Montreal Conventions and Montreal Protocol.
- **Carriage by Air Ordinance (Cap. 500)**: Implements Warsaw and Montreal Conventions.
