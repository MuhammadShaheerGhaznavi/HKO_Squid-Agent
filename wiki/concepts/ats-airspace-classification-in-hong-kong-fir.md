## Content
Hong Kong FIR airspace is subdivided into three classes: A, C, and G. Classes B, D, E, and F are not used. Class A: IFR only, separation provided to all aircraft, Air Traffic Control Service, no speed limitation (except note 1), continuous two-way communication required, subject to ATC clearance. Class C: IFR flights receive separation from IFR and VFR, Air Traffic Control Service; VFR flights receive separation from IFR, traffic information for VFR/VFR, and traffic avoidance advice on request; speed limitation 250 KIAS below 10,000 ft AMSL; continuous two-way communication required; subject to ATC clearance. Class G: IFR and VFR, no separation, Flight Information Service, speed limitation 250 KIAS below 10,000 ft AMSL, continuous two-way communication required (except south of Hong Kong TMA where not required), no ATC clearance required. Airspace descriptions: Within 50 NM of BIGEX (excluding airspace within and above Hong Kong CTR and UCARAs): UNL to 8,000 ft Class A; 8,000 ft to 2,000 ft Class C; 2,000 ft to SFC Class G (2-way comm required). Exceeding 50 NM from BIGEX but within Hong Kong TMA: UNL to 8,000 ft Class A. Exceeding 50 NM from BIGEX and beneath Hong Kong TMA: 8,000 ft to SFC Class G (2-way comm required). Above Hong Kong CTR: UNL to 9,000 ft Class A. Hong Kong CTR: 9,000 ft to SFC Class C. Above UCARAs: UNL to 9,000 ft Class A; 9,000 ft to upper limit of UCARAs Class C. UCARAs: upper limit to SFC Class G (2-way comm required). Airspace south of Hong Kong TMA: UNL to 8,000 ft Class A; 8,000 ft to SFC Class G (2-way comm not required). Speed restriction applies to flights departing/arriving HKIA and Macao International Airport (see ENR 1.5, SID, STAR, etc.). Two-way communication is required in Class G within 50 NM of BIGEX, beneath Hong Kong TMA, and in UCARAs; not required south of Hong Kong TMA.
