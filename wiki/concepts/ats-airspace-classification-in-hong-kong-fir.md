## Content
The airspace within Hong Kong FIR is subdivided into three classifications: A, C, and G, according to ICAO specifications. Classes B, D, E, and F are not used. Class A: IFR only, separation provided to all aircraft, Air Traffic Control Service, no speed limitation (but note speed restrictions for HKIA and Macao), continuous two-way communication required, subject to ATC clearance. Class C: IFR flights receive separation from IFR and VFR, Air Traffic Control Service, no speed limitation (note), continuous two-way communication, ATC clearance required; VFR flights receive separation from IFR, traffic information from VFR, speed limitation 250 KIAS below 10,000 ft AMSL, continuous two-way communication, ATC clearance required. Class G: IFR and VFR receive no separation, Flight Information Service, speed limitation 250 KIAS below 10,000 ft AMSL, two-way communication required within 50 NM of BIGEX, beneath Hong Kong TMA, and in UCARAs (not required south of Hong Kong TMA), no ATC clearance required. Airspace description: Within 50 NM of BIGEX (excluding airspace within and above Hong Kong CTR and UCARAs): UNL to 8,000 ft Class A; 8,000 ft to 2,000 ft Class C; 2,000 ft to SFC Class G (2-way comm required). Exceeding 50 NM from BIGEX but within Hong Kong TMA: UNL to 8,000 ft Class A. Exceeding 50 NM from BIGEX and beneath Hong Kong TMA: 8,000 ft to SFC Class G (2-way comm required). Above Hong Kong CTR: UNL to 9,000 ft Class A. Hong Kong CTR: 9,000 ft to SFC Class C. Above UCARAs: UNL to 9,000 ft Class A; 9,000 ft to upper limit of UCARAs Class C. UCARAs: upper limit to SFC Class G (2-way comm required). Airspace south of Hong Kong TMA: UNL to 8,000 ft Class A; 8,000 ft to SFC Class G (2-way comm not required).
