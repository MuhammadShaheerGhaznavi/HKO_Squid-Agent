## Content
**Aircraft Parking**
- Frontal parking stands: continuous yellow nosewheel guidance lines for wide-body; dashed for narrow-body (displaced 9m right, 'R' suffix). Narrow-body stands include S1R, S2R, S3R, S25R, S27R, S29R, S31R, S33R, S35R, S41R, S43R, S45R, S47R, S49R (South); N6R, N7R, N24R, N26R, N28R, N30R, N32R, N34R, N60R, N62R, N64R, N66R, N68R, N70R (North); W40R, W42R, W44R, W46R, W48R, W61R, W63R, W65R, W67R, W69R, W71R (West).
- Remote stands: South/North aprons except S109, N145, R13-R21 can accommodate wide/narrow; S109, N145, R13-R21 narrow only. West apron: up to 5 wide or 7 narrow. Midfield D301-D310: up to 10 wide or 18 narrow. Narrow-body locations with 'L'/'R' suffix (e.g., W121L, W122R). W126 is self-manoeuvring, Code C only.

**Safegate Docking System**
- All frontal stands equipped; enables parking without marshaller.

**RETO Operations**
- Only for Airbus A320 series with specific engine types.
- Requires AAHK approval; thrust ≤40% N1; monthly compliance reports.

**Maritime Facilities**
- Five facilities west of airport: Dayushan Anchorage No.1 & 2, Longgu West Fairway, Tonggu Fairway, Lingding Fairway.
- Tall vessels up to 170m air-draft may enter Anchorage No.1 during contingency.
- Vessel heights affect procedures: e.g., Longgu West Fairway vessels >49m up to 55m: RNP suspended, ILS/LOC available; >55m up to 63m: increase climb gradient to 3.8% until 700 ft; >63m up to 66m: ILS suspended, LOC available, climb gradient 4.3%; >110m up to 170m: ILS suspended, LOC available, climb gradient 5.0% for RWY 25R SIDs, 3.6% for RWY 25C SIDs.
- Air-draft measured from 2.65m AMSL.
