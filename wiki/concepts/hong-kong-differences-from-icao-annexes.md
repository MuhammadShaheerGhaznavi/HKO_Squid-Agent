# Hong Kong Differences from ICAO Annexes

This note summarizes the differences filed by Hong Kong from various ICAO Annexes, as per the latest editions. Items marked with an asterisk (*) are Recommended Practices.

## Annex 1 – Personnel Licensing (13th edition)

- **Flight crew definition**: Same as ICAO, including pilot, flight navigator, flight engineer, and flight radio operator.
- **PPL flight instructor rating (2.3.2.1)**: A PPL holder with a flight instructor rating for aeroplanes may be paid for instruction or flight tests when acting with members of the same flying club.
- **Cross-country solo flight experience (2.3.3.1.2)**: If a cross-country flight of at least 270 km (150 NM) with full-stop landings at two different aerodromes cannot be completed, the licence is endorsed: 'The holder has not met the requirement in respect of the experience of cross-country solo flight time specified in paragraph 2.3.3.1.2 of Annex 1 to the Convention on International Civil Aviation'. The endorsement can be removed upon evidence of meeting the requirement.
- **CPL cross-country solo flight (2.3.4.1.2)**: Similar endorsement for a cross-country flight of at least 180 km (100 NM) with two full-stop landings.
- **Instrument Rating (2.7.1.3.1)**: Applicants for IR(A) or IR(H) must hold a valid Hong Kong Class 1 Medical Certificate.
- **Minimum ages**: PPL (Gliders) 17 years, CPL (Gliders) 18 years (2.9.1.1); PPL (Balloons and Airships) 17 years, CPL (Balloons) 18 years (2.10.1.1); Flight Engineer's Licence 21 years (3.3.1.1).

## Annex 2 – Rules of the Air (10th edition)

- **Balloons (3.1.10)**: Balloons exceeding 2 metres shall not be flown in controlled airspace except with permission and compliance with specific conditions.
- **VFR rules (3.9 and 4.1)**: As detailed in [[Hong Kong Visual Flight Rules (VFR) Requirements]].
- **Change from IFR to VFR (4.8)**: Not permitted in controlled airspace.
- **VMC clearances (5.9)**: Not offered in Hong Kong; VMC clearances requested by pilots are normally granted subject to the VMC portion being at or below FL150.
- **Timed approach procedures (6.5.6.2.1)**: Not authorized.
- **Time checks (7.4.1.2.1 f)**: Given only on request.
- **Light signals (7.6.3.2.3.2)**: Not used.
- **Identification method (8.6.2.4.2)**: Not used.
- **Nominal glide path (8.9.6.1.3 c)**: Hong Kong does not inform aircraft of the nominal glide path.
- **Surveillance radar final approach (8.9.7.1)**: Not authorized.
- **Meteorological info on ATIS/VOLMET (11.4.3.2)**: Surface wind velocity measured at RWY 07C/25C mid-point; visibility representative of minimum all-round surface visibility.
- **SLOP (16.5)**: Strategic Lateral Offset Procedures not implemented in Hong Kong FIR.
- **Item 18 (Appendix 2, 2.2)**: Non-RVSM approved aircraft capable of operating at FL280 or above must insert 'STS/NONRVSM'.
- **Class A airspace (1.2.1)**: All flights in Class A airspace in Hong Kong FIR must conform to IFR except as indicated in ENR 1.2, para 4.
- **Diversion SSR codes (5.1.3.2)**: On diversion into another FIR, original code retained subject to acceptance; otherwise codes from blocks 5101-5107, 5110-5113, 5114-5117, or appropriate codes from 3310-3357, 3530-3577, 5120-5177, 5250-5257, 5710-5757 assigned.

## Annex 4 – Aeronautical Charts (11th edition)

- **Orientation (2.1.7*)**: Some charts are not True North orientated; aerodrome charts are runway orientated.
- **Horizontal scale (3.4.3*)**: 1:10,000 not used; scale is 1:15,000.
- **Elevations (4.3.1)**: Shown to nearest metre or foot in compliance with Doc 10066 Appendix 1.
- **Relief (10.6.2*)**: Only relief within the boundary of Hong Kong SAR is shown.
- **World Aeronautical Chart (16.1)**: Scale 1:1,000,000 not produced.
- **Local Flying Chart (17.1)**: Scale 1:100,000.
- **Relief (17.7.10.1)**: Only relief within Hong Kong SAR boundary shown.
- **Isogonic lines (17.8.1)**: Not shown.
- **Chart scale (21.3.3*)**: Not same as Area Chart; scale is 1:800,000.

## Annex 6 – Operation of Aircraft (Part I 11th, Part II 10th, Part III 9th editions)

- **Aerial work (1)**: Not defined as an aircraft operation for specialized services such as agriculture, construction, photography, surveying, observation and patrol, search and rescue, aerial advertisement, etc.
- **Hand-held microphone (6.20)**: The pilot and flight engineer (if any) shall not use a hand-held microphone (for radio communication within the aircraft) while flying in controlled airspace below FL150 or during take-off/landing.
- **Recency requirements (9.4.3.5 and 9.4.3.6)**: A 13-month, instead of 12-month, recency requirement is stipulated for pilot-in-command on a route or within an area.

## Annex 9 – Facilitation (15th edition)

- **Character width (6.2)**: ICAO requires width of each character (except I and 1) and hyphens to be two-thirds of height. In Hong Kong, width of letters M and W shall be neither less than two-thirds nor more than their height.
- **General Declaration (2.11)**: Normally required on arrival and prior to departure under local legislation.
- **Air cargo reporting (2.14)**: Prescribed by local legislation, no option as proposed.
- **Administration info (2.44*, 2.45)**: Required to meet operational and legal requirements; details in AIP Hong Kong.
- **Visas (3.25)**: Normally valid for not more than 3 months from date of issue.
- **Embarkation/disembarkation card (3.28)**: Format at Appendix 5 not adopted; existing card in prescribed format per legislation.
- **Air crew immigration (3.65, 3.65.1*, 3.65.2*)**: Visa nationals require visa for entry; non-operating aircrew cleared as normal passengers.
- **Civil aviation inspectors (3.67*, 3.68*, 3.69*, 3.70*)**: Entry subject to normal immigration; facilitation may be accorded; Civil Aviation Inspector Certificate not issued.
- **Temporary entry (3.71*, 3.72, 3.74*, 3.75*, 3.76*)**: Subject to normal immigration; facilitation on case-by-case basis.
- **Minor's country of residence (3.84*)**: Not collected by aircraft operators.
- **Dutiable commodities (4.29, 4.37, 4.39*)**: Hong Kong is non-tariff port; duty only on alcoholic liquors, tobacco, hydrocarbon oil, and methyl alcohol. Simplified customs procedures with security bonds for release before duty payment; supporting documentation required for duty assessment.
- **Cargo clearance (4.51)**: Contained cargo can be cleared by Customs within 80 minutes upon request.
- **Inadmissible persons (5.11, 5.11.1*)**: Legal provisions for removal; aircraft operators must comply with directions.
- **Improper documents (5.14)**: Aircraft operators should not carry improperly documented persons without prior consent; fines considered case-by-case.
- **Deportation (5.19, 5.19.2)**: Operators informed before execution; legal requirements observed.
- **Travel document production (5.27)**: Requires application form with signature.
- **Pre-examination (6.44*)**: No arrangement to station representatives in other States.
- **Currency declaration (6.48*, 6.49*)**: No restriction on import/export of funds; passengers arriving with CBNIs > HKD120,000 must make written declaration; departing passengers must disclose upon request.
- **PNR (9.24-9.39*)**: Not yet in place in Hong Kong.

## Annex 10 – Aeronautical Telecommunications (Vol I 7th, Vol II 7th, Vol III 2nd, Vol IV 5th, Vol V 3rd editions)

- **Passenger data (8.15.1*)**: Two additional items to be completed: Passport/Identity Card number and Issuing Country/Organization, in addition to Appendix 13.
- **PNR (9.24-9.39*)**: Not yet in place in Hong Kong.
- **Localizer coverage (Vol I, 3.1.3.3.1)**: The following localizers at Hong Kong International Airport do not meet standard coverage criteria: RWY 07R, RWY 25L, RWY 25R, RWY 07C, RWY 25C.

## Annex 11 – Air Traffic Services (15th edition)

- **Glide-path coverage (3.1.5.3.1)**: The following glide-paths at Hong Kong International Airport do not meet standard coverage criteria: RWY 25L, RWY 25R, RWY 07L.
- **Definitions (1)**: Air Traffic Control Unit/Service, Flight crew, Incident, Operator, Special VFR flight, Taxiing as defined in [[Hong Kong Definitions of Key Aviation Terms]].
- **Class G airspace (2.6.3)**: Requires continuous two-way radio communication equipment.

## Annex 12 – Search and Rescue (8th edition)

- **Legal framework (2.1.1.2)**: The SAR services system in Hong Kong does not include a legal framework.
- **Homing device (2.6.4)**: Some helicopters are not equipped with homing device since they are usually deployed for local SAR tasks.
- **International Code of Signals (2.6.6)**: Carriage of a copy is not considered necessary for helicopters usually deployed for local SAR tasks.

## Annex 14 – Aerodromes (Vol I 8th, Vol II 5th editions)

- **No-entry bar (Vol I, 5.3.29*)**: Not provided.
- **Obstruction lights (Vol I, 6.2.2.6 and Vol II, 5.3.1)**: The colour of low intensity obstruction lights displayed on rescue and fire fighting vehicles is red.

## Annex 15 – Aeronautical Information Services (16th edition)

- **Metadata (4.2.1, 4.2.2)**: No metadata are collected for the complete data chain.
- **Digital data sets (5.3.3.3.5*, 5.3.3.3.6*, 5.3.3.4.7*, 5.3.3.4.8*, 5.4.3.1*, 5.4.3.1.1, 5.4.3.1.2*, 5.4.3.1.3*)**: Not implemented; provision of digital data sets via information services is not available.
- **Operator definition (1)**: As defined in [[Hong Kong Definitions of Key Aviation Terms]].

## Annex 16 – Environmental Protection (Vol I 8th, Vol II 4th, Vol III 1st, Vol IV 1st editions)

- **CORSIA (Vol IV)**: Multiple differences have been filed in Chinese. For further details, contact the Environmental Management Office of Hong Kong Civil Aviation Department.

## Annexes with No Differences Filed

Hong Kong has filed no differences from the following ICAO Annexes:

- **Annex 3** - Meteorological Service for International Air Navigation (20th edition): NIL
- **Annex 5** - Units of Measurement (5th edition): NIL
- **Annex 7** - Aircraft Nationality and Registration Marks (6th edition): NIL
- **Annex 8** - Airworthiness of Aircraft (12th edition): NIL
- **Annex 13** - Aircraft Accident and Incident Investigation (12th edition): NIL
- **Annex 17** - Aviation Security (12th edition): NIL
- **Annex 18** - Safe Transport of Dangerous Goods by Air (4th edition): NIL
- **Annex 19** - Safety Management (2nd edition): NIL
