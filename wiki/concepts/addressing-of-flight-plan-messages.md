## Content
Flight movement messages (including flight plan, amendment, and cancellation messages) for traffic into or via Hong Kong FIR must be addressed as follows: For all flights (IFR/VFR) inbound to Hong Kong International Airport or transiting Hong Kong FIR, address to VHHKZQZX. For all flights outbound from Hong Kong International Airport, address to VHHHZPZA. This ensures correct relay and delivery per ICAO PANS-ATM DOC 4444, Chapter 11, para 11.2.1.1.3. See [[Flight Plan Submission Methods for Departures from HKIA]].
