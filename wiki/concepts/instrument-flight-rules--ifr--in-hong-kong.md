## Content
For flights within controlled airspace, Rules 25, 27, and 28 of Schedule 14 to the Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995 apply. For flights outside controlled airspace, Rules 25 and 26 apply. All flights within controlled airspace notified for Rule 21 are subject to IFR in all weather conditions. Aircraft flying at night must be flown in accordance with IFR unless ATC authorizes Special VFR. The Terminal Control Area (TMA) from 8,000 ft AMSL to unlimited, as defined in ENR 2.1, and all ATS Routes and PBN Routes as defined in ENR 3.1 and ENR 3.3, are notified for Rule 21.
