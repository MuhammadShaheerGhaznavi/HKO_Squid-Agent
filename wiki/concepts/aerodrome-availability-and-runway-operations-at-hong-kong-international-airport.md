## Aerodrome Availability and Operating Minima

Hong Kong International Airport (VHHH) is notified for public transport and flying instruction under the Air Navigation (Hong Kong) Order 1995. Permission to land or take-off will not be refused solely due to adverse weather, but pilots must comply with company aerodrome operating minima (AOM) per Articles 30 and 31. Engine-out ferry flights are not normally permitted; exceptions require approval from the Director-General of Civil Aviation. Landing and parking charges must be paid before departure unless prior arrangements with AAHK.

### Operating Minima (AOM)

- **Take-off minima:**
  - 125 m RVR for RWY 07L/25R and 07C/25C
  - 150 m RVR for RWY 07R/25L
- **Landing minima:**
  - CAT I: DH 200 ft, RVR 550 m
  - CAT II: DH 100 ft, RVR 300 m for RWY 07L and 25C/07C; 350 m for RWY 25L/07R
  - CAT III: DH less than 100 ft, RVR less than 300 m but not less than 75 m for Cat. A/B/C aircraft, and not less than 100 m for Cat. D aircraft.

Foreign operators must submit AOM Form DCA 236 to the Director-General for acceptance.

## Parallel Runway Operations

Hong Kong International Airport operates three runways: North (07L/25R), Centre (07C/25C), and South (07R/25L).

### Three-Runway Operations (normally 0000-1459 UTC daily)

- **Runway 07:**
  - 07L: arrivals only
  - 07C: departures only (to North/Northeast/East via BEKOL, LEKEN - V601/V611, or DALOL - V621/V631)
  - 07R: mixed (to Southeast/South/West via DALOL - V642/V652 or PECAN - V10/V11/V12)
- **Runway 25:**
  - 25R: arrivals only
  - 25C: departures only (to East/Southeast/South/West via DALOL - V621/V631/V642/V652 or PECAN - V10/V11/V12)
  - 25L: mixed (to North/Northeast via BEKOL or LEKEN - V601/V611)

Arrivals use dependent parallel approaches to 07L/07R or 25R/25L. Aircraft parking at South Cargo Apron or Business Aviation Centre preferentially assigned South Runway (07R/25L). Independent parallel departures on 07C/07R or 25C/25L.

### Other Time Periods

- **1500-1759 UTC:** Centre and South runways operate in Segregated Mode (Centre arrivals, South departures).
- **1800-2259 UTC:** Only one runway (North or Centre) operates in Mixed Mode.
- **2300-2359 UTC:** Dual runway operations resume.

Scheduled maintenance rotates across runways between 1800-2359 UTC, published in AIP Supplements and NOTAM.
