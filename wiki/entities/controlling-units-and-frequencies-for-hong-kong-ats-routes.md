## Content
The controlling unit for most ATS and PBN routes in Hong Kong is Hong Kong Radar, with primary (PRI) and secondary (SRY) frequencies that vary by route segment. Common frequencies include: 127.1 MHz (PRI) and 123.7 MHz (SRY) for many segments; 121.3 MHz (PRI) and 128.125 MHz (SRY) for eastern segments; 125.325 MHz (PRI) and 132.775 MHz (SRY) for southern segments; 128.75 MHz (PRI) and 129.9 MHz (SRY) for southeastern segments; 132.15 MHz (PRI) and 129.9 MHz (SRY) for some segments; 118.925 MHz (PRI) and 128.125 MHz (SRY) for some eastern segments; 126.5 MHz (PRI) and 132.8 MHz (SRY) for V512/V522; 126.3 MHz (PRI) and 132.6 MHz (SRY) for V525/V533/V534/V542/V551; 133.7 MHz (PRI) and 129.9 MHz (SRY) for V533/V534/V642/V652/W29; 133.825 MHz (PRI) and 132.8 MHz (SRY) for V601/V611/V621/V631/V642/V652; 127.55 MHz (PRI) and 134.3 MHz (SRY) for V561/V571; 123.475 MHz (PRI) and 132.6 MHz (SRY) for V10/V11/V12/V31/V32; 122.95 MHz (PRI) and 135.6 MHz (SRY) for L642/M771/M772; 125.8 MHz (PRI) and 132.775 MHz (SRY) for L642/M771/Q1. Some segments have 'When instructed' frequencies, e.g., 118.925 MHz for A1/G581. For A461, Hong Kong DEP is used for FL250 and below (123.8 MHz PRI, 124.05 MHz SRY), and Hong Kong Radar above FL250.
