## Observing Systems at Hong Kong International Airport

### Anemometers
Three sets of anemometers are installed near each runway:
- **South runway**: RWY 07R TDZ, 07R/25L MID, 25L TDZ
- **Centre runway**: 07C TDZ, 07C/25C MID, 25C TDZ
- **North runway**: 07L TDZ, 07L/25R MID, 25R TDZ

Official wind reports use the anemometer near RWY 07L/25R MID RWY. If unavailable, the substitution order is: RWY 25R TDZ, 07L TDZ, 25L TDZ, 07R TDZ, 07R/25L MID.

### Ceilometers
Ceilometers are installed near the thresholds of all six runways (07L, 07C, 07R, 25L, 25C, 25R) and at Siu Mo To and Meteorological Garden.

### RVR and Visibility
Nine sets of transmissometers and forward scatterers measure RVR and visibility along runways. RVR is reported when less than 1,500 m, with the following scale:
- 50-400 m in 25 m increments
- 400-800 m in 50 m increments
- 800-1,500 m in 100 m increments
- Rounding down

RVR values are updated every 10 seconds and averaged over 60 seconds.

### Temperature and Pressure
Temperature and pressure are measured by digital thermometers and barometers in the Meteorological Garden adjacent to S-TWR.

## Certification Status
Hong Kong International Airport (VHHH) was certified on 24 November 2025, with a validity of 1 year. No remarks.
