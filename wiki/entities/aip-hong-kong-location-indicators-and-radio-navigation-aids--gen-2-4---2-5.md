## AIP Hong Kong GEN 2.4: Location Indicators

The GEN 2.4 section lists the location indicators for the Hong Kong FIR and associated aerodromes:

- **VHHK** – HONG KONG FIR
- **VHHH** – HONG KONG/ INTERNATIONAL
- **VMMC** – MACAO/ INTL AIRPORT
- **VMMH** – MACAO/ HELIPORT
- **VHSK** – SEK KONG
- **VHSS** – SHEUNG WAN / SKY SHUTTLE HELIPORT

**Important notes:**
- The indicators VHSK and VHSS cannot be used in the address component of AFS messages.
- For the use of VMMC and VMMH in AFS addresses, refer to AIP Macao.
- These indicators are used for flight planning, ATS messages, and navigation.

## AIP Hong Kong GEN 2.5: Radio Navigation Aids

The GEN 2.5 section lists the radio navigation aids in Hong Kong:

- **ITFC** – HONG KONG/ INTERNATIONAL: DME RWY 25C, ILS GP RWY 25C, ILS LOC RWY 25C (Purpose: A – Aerodrome)
- **ITFL** – HONG KONG/ INTERNATIONAL: DME RWY 25L, ILS GP RWY 25L, ILS LOC RWY 25L (Purpose: A)
- **ITFR** – HONG KONG/ INTERNATIONAL: DME RWY 25R, ILS GP RWY 25R, ILS LOC RWY 25R (Purpose: A)
- **IZSC** – HONG KONG/ INTERNATIONAL: DME RWY 07C, ILS GP RWY 07C, ILS LOC RWY 07C (Purpose: A)
- **IZSL** – HONG KONG/ INTERNATIONAL: DME RWY 07L, ILS GP RWY 07L, ILS LOC RWY 07L (Purpose: A)
- **IZSR** – HONG KONG/ INTERNATIONAL: DME RWY 07R, ILS GP RWY 07R, ILS LOC RWY 07R (Purpose: A)
- **SMT** – SIU MO TO: DME, DVOR (Purpose: AE – Dual purpose, Aerodrome and Enroute)
- **TD** – TUNG LUNG: DME, DVOR (Purpose: AE)

**Purpose codes:** A = Aerodrome, E = Enroute, AE = Dual purpose. These aids support approach and en-route navigation.
