## Content
The heliport is an elevated helipad with dimensions 34.4m x 34.4m. FATO dimensions: 25.0m x 25.0m, aluminum helideck. TLOF: 34.2m x 34.2m, aluminum helideck, load bearing 9.0t. Coordinates of geometric centre: Western Helipad midpoint 221720.0N 1140907.1E, Eastern Helipad midpoint 221719.3N 1140909.8E. Elevation 120 ft AMSL, slope <2%. Safety area extends 4.7m from FATO periphery to edges of helipad, with 1.5m safety net. Markings: Western and Eastern helipads have yellow circular TLOF and white 'H' heliport identification marking. Additional markings: Maximum Allowable Mass '9.0t' in white, D-Value 'D17m' in white, TLOF perimeter continuous white line, Heliport Name 'VHSS' in white. Lighting: TLOF has fixed omni-directional green perimeter lights spaced 3m apart; floodlights arranged to avoid glare. WDI and anemometer located on top of Marine Control Tower at outer pier, with lights.
