## Content
Hong Kong has one international aerodrome: HONG KONG/INTERNATIONAL (VHHH), with traffic type INTL, IFR-VFR, S-NS-P, referenced in AD 2. There is one military aerodrome: SEK KONG (VHSK), with traffic type Military - NTL, VFR, referenced in AD 3. There is one heliport: SHEUNG WAN / SKY SHUTTLE HELIPORT (VHSS), with traffic type INTL, VFR, NS, referenced in AD 3. Grouping criteria: Primary/Major International Aerodrome/Heliport is for international traffic with customs, immigration, health, quarantine, and regular ATS. National Aerodrome/Heliport is for domestic traffic, including military aerodromes where civil traffic is allowed under conditions. See [[Aerodrome Certification Status in Hong Kong]].
