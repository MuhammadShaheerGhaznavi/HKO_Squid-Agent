## Content
The aerodromes and heliports in Hong Kong are: HONG KONG/INTERNATIONAL (VHHH) – international, IFR-VFR, scheduled/non-scheduled/private, reference AD 2; SEK KONG (VHSK) – military, VFR, military traffic, reference AD 3; SHEUNG WAN/SKY SHUTTLE HELIPORT (VHSS) – international, VFR, non-scheduled, reference AD 3. Grouping criteria: Primary/Major International Aerodrome/Heliport is an aerodrome of entry/departure for international traffic with customs, immigration, health, quarantine, and regular ATS. National Aerodrome/Heliport is for domestic traffic only, including military aerodromes where civil traffic is allowed under conditions.
