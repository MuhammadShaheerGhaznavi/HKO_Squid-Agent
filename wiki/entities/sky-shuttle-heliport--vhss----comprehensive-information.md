## Overview
Sky Shuttle Heliport (VHSS), also known as Sheung Wan / Sky Shuttle Heliport, is located on the north shore of Hong Kong Island, west of Star Ferry Pier, Central. It is an unlicensed heliport requiring PPR (Prior Permission Required).

## Key Data
- **Heliport Reference Point:** 221719.7N 1140908.4E
- **Elevation:** 120 ft AMSL
- **Magnetic Variation:** 3°W (2020), annual change 4’W
- **Types of Traffic Permitted:** VFR/SVFR
- **Administration:** Sky Shuttle Helicopters Ltd., Room 1603, China Merchants Tower, Shun Tak Centre, 200 Connaught Road Central, Hong Kong; Tel: +852 2108 9873, Fax: +852 2108 9875

## Operational Hours
- **Heliport Administration:** 0100-1000
- **Customs and Immigration:** 0000-1459
- **Handling:** 0000-1459
- **Security:** H24

## Operational Restrictions
- Heliport closes when typhoon signal No. 8 is hoisted.
- Passenger movement on helipad suspended when wind speed >30 kts.

## Fire Fighting and Rescue
- **Heliport category for fire fighting:** H2
- Rescue launches with foam/water and life rafts available from Government Department at short notice.
