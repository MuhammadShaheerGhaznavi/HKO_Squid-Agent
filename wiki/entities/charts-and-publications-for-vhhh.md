## Content
Numerous charts are published for VHHH, including: Aerodrome Chart (AD-2-VHHH-ADC-1), Visual Markings (ADC-2), Lighting Plan (ADC-3), Aircraft Parking/Docking (ADC-4), Pavement Types and PCR Value (ADC-5), Taxiways for Code F (ADC-6). Aerodrome Obstacle Charts Type A for each runway pair (AOC-1 to AOC-3) and Type B (AOC-4). Precision Approach Terrain Charts for all runways (PATC-1 to PATC-6). Area Charts for Departure and Arrival (AC-DEP, AC-ARR). SID charts for various procedures: ADLAD, LARIT, DUMEP, XEMEK, SOSLU for RWY 07C; BEKOL, LEKEN, DALOL, PECAN for all runways with RF variants; RAMEN and RUMSY conventional. STAR charts for ABBEY, BETTY, CANTO, SIERA, and GNSS versions. Instrument Approach Charts for ILS, LOC, RNP Z, and RNP Y for each runway (IAC-01A to IAC-06G). Pushback procedure charts (PBP-1 to PBP-5). CTR Zones and UCARAs chart (CTR-1), Entry/Exit Routes (CTR-2), VFR Holding Points (VFR-1), Helicopter VFR Holding Areas (VFR-2), Helicopter Holding Areas in Tung Chung (VFR-3), Helicopter Landing Locations (HLL), HZMB Lighting (HZMB-LGT), and Aeronautical Ground Lights (AERO-GND-LGT).
