## Content
Table COM-1A lists international AFS circuits: Hong Kong connects via LDD/d channels to Bangkok, Beijing, Fukuoka, Guangzhou, Haikou, Hochiminh City, Macao, Manila, and Taibei (all H24). Direct controller-to-controller voice links (LTF) are established with Guangzhou ACC, Macao TWR, Manila ACC, Nanning ACC, Sanya ACC, Shanghai ACC, Shantou ACC, Taibei ACC, Xiamen ACC, Zhanjiang ATCU, and Zhuhai APP. Hong Kong Radar has a direct link with Taibei ACC (Taibei Radar). Table COM-1B lists local circuits: Hong Kong (VHHH) connects to VHHHYAHQ (CAD ASO), VHHHYLYX (AAIC/AAIA), VHHHYAYC (CAA), VHHHYKYX (MRCC), VHHHZSZX (COSPAS-SARSAT), VHHKZDZX (ATFMU), VHHKZQZX (ATCC), VHHHYNYX (AIMC/NOF), VHHHYMYX (AMO/HKO), VHHHYCYX (RCC), and VHHHYFYX (COM), all via LDD/a or LDD/d, H24.
