## Content
VHSS is an elevated helipad with dimensions 34.4m x 34.4m. FATO dimensions 25.0m x 25.0m, aluminum helideck; TLOF 34.2m x 34.2m, load bearing 9.0t. Coordinates: Western Helipad midpoint 221720.0N 1140907.1E, Eastern Helipad midpoint 221719.3N 1140909.8E. Elevation 120 ft AMSL, slope <2%. Safety area extends 4.7m from FATO periphery, with 1.5m safety net. Markings: both helipads have yellow circular TLOF, white 'H' identification, '9.0t' maximum allowable mass, 'D17m' D-value, continuous white line for TLOF perimeter, and 'VHSS' heliport name. Lighting: TLOF has fixed omni-directional green perimeter lights spaced 3m apart; floodlights arranged to avoid glare. Anemometer and WDI located on top of Marine Control Tower at outer pier. Secondary power supply available.
