## Content
**Location and Administration**
- ARP: 221832N 1135453E (mid-point of aerodrome)
- 25 km west of Central District, Hong Kong Island
- Elevation: 28 ft, Reference temperature: 34°C
- MAG VAR: 3°W (2020), annual change 4’W
- Operator: Airport Authority Hong Kong, HKIA Tower, 1 Sky Plaza Road, Hong Kong International Airport, Lantau, Hong Kong. Tel: +852 2188 7111, Fax: +852 2824 0717, Web: http://www.hkairport.com

**Operational Hours (all H24)**
- Administration, Customs/Immigration, Health/Sanitation, AIS Briefing, ATS Reporting, MET Briefing, ATS, Fuelling, Handling, Security. De-icing: Nil.

**Handling Services**
- Cargo handling up to 4,500 kg
- Fuel: Jet A1 (AVTUR 650) and all oil grades; hydrant refuelling at 500 gpm on all main and cargo stands
- Hangar space: limited, unheated, by prior arrangement
- Repair: major/minor, engine changes, spare parts by prior arrangement
- Fixed ground power (960V 400 Hz) at all main and cargo apron stands

**Passenger Facilities**
- Hotels adjacent and in city; restaurants; transport (rail, buses, taxis, ferries); medical at terminal and city hospital; bank/post office; tourist office; transit lounge; bonded storage.

**Rescue and Fire Fighting**
- Category 10
- Additional: 2 rescue launches with foam/water and life rafts; 8 high speed rescue boats
- Disabled aircraft removal: specialized equipment for Code F aircraft, coordinated by Airport Authority Hong Kong.

**Seasonal Clearing**: Nil.
