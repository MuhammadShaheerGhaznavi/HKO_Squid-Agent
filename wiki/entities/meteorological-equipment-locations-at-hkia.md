## Content
Meteorological equipment locations include: Meteorological Garden adjacent to S-TWR (ceilometer, rain gauge, temperature screen, barometer) and at West Support Area; anemometers, RVR transmissometers, and forward scatterers at each runway TDZ and mid-point (e.g., RWY 07R TDZ, 07C TDZ, 07L TDZ, 07R/25L mid, 07C/25C mid, 07L/25R mid, 25L TDZ, 25C TDZ, 25R TDZ); ceilometers at each runway TDZ and Siu Mo To; additional anemometers at aprons (E9, E4, N70, D201, D219, W50, N12, S31, X458, M6, SSI, D312, D319, D323), near RWY 25L approach lights, and at various locations (eastern end of South Runway, middle of South and Center Runways, Nei Lak Shan, Ngong Ping, Pak Kung Au, Sha Chau, Sha Lo Wan, Sham Wat Hilltop, Tai Fung Au, Tai Mo To, Tai O, Yi Tung Shan); weather buoys (1, 2, 4, 5, 8, 12, 13); TDWRs at Brothers Point and Tai Lam Chung; LIDARs at North Runway (1, 2), Center Runway, South Runway, and short-range LIDARs at various points (near AsiaWorld-Expo, western/eastern end of North Runway, Government Flying Service, Gate 3, Fire Station on HZMB HK Port, South Perimeter Road, Siu Ho Wan); wind profilers at Sha Lo Wan, Siu Ho Wan, and on-airport; Siu Ho Wan X-Band Radar.
