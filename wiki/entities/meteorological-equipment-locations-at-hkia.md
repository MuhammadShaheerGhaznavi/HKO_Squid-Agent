## Content
Equipment locations include: Meteorological Gardens adjacent to S-TWR and West Support Area; anemometers at runway TDZ and mid-points (e.g., RWY 07R TDZ, 07C TDZ, 07L TDZ, 07R/25L MID, 07C/25C MID, 07L/25R MID, 25L TDZ, 25C TDZ, 25R TDZ); apron anemometers at E9, E4, N70, D201, D219, W50, N12, S31, X458, M6, SSI, D312, D319, D323; ceilometers at all runway TDZs and Siu Mo To; hilltop anemometers at Nei Lak Shan, Ngong Ping, Pak Kung Au, Sha Chau, Sha Lo Wan, Sham Wat Hilltop, Tai Fung Au, Tai Mo To, Tai O, Yi Tung Shan; weather buoys 1, 2, 4, 5, 8, 12, 13; TDWR at Brothers Point and Tai Lam Chung; LIDARs at North Runway (2), Center Runway, South Runway, and short-range LIDARs near AsiaWorld-Expo (2), western and eastern ends of North Runway, Government Flying Service, Gate 3, Fire Station on HZMB HK Port, South Perimeter Road, Siu Ho Wan; wind profilers at Sha Lo Wan, Siu Ho Wan, and on-airport; Siu Ho Wan X-Band Radar.
