## Content
**Aerodrome Charts**
- Aerodrome Chart (Layout): AD 2-VHHH-ADC-1
- Visual markings: ADC-2
- Lighting Plan: ADC-3
- Aircraft Parking/Docking: ADC-4
- Pavement Types and PCR: ADC-5
- Taxiways/Taxilanes for Code F: ADC-6

**Obstacle Charts**
- Type A for each runway pair: AOC-1 (07R/25L), AOC-2 (07C/25C), AOC-3 (07L/25R)
- Type B: AOC-4
- Precision Approach Terrain Charts: PATC-1 to PATC-6
- Aerodrome Terrain and Obstacle Chart (Electronic): ATOC-1

**SID Charts**
- RNAV SIDs for various runways: ADLAD, LARIT, DUMEP, XEMEK, SOSLU (RWY 07C); BEKOL, LEKEN, DALOL, PECAN for all runways with suffixes (A-F, X, Y, Z).
- Conventional SIDs: RAMEN (RWY 07R/C/L), RUMSY (RWY 25L/C/R).

**STAR Charts**
- ABBEY, BETTY, CANTO, SIERA, and GNSS STARs.

**Approach Charts**
- ILS, LOC, RNP Z, RNP Y for each runway: e.g., IAC-01A (ILS 07R), IAC-01C (LOC 07R), IAC-01E (RNP Z 07R), IAC-01G (RNP Y 07R).
- RNAV transition to ILS 25R: IAC-06B.

**Other Charts**
- Pushback procedures: PBP-1 to PBP-5
- CTR/UCARA charts: CTR-1, CTR-2
- VFR holding: VFR-1, VFR-2, VFR-3
- Helicopter landing locations: HLL
- HZMB lighting: HZMB-LGT
- Aeronautical ground lights: AERO-GND-LGT
