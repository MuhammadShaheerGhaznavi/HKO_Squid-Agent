## Radio Navigation Aids in Hong Kong (GEN 2.5)

This section lists radio navigation aids in Hong Kong, covering both terminal and en-route aids.

### Airport Aids (Hong Kong International Airport, VHHH)

ILS and DME installations are available for runways 07C, 07L, 07R, 25C, 25L, and 25R. Each has a specific identifier:

- **25C**: ITFC
- **25L**: ITFL
- **25R**: ITFR
- **07C**: IZSC
- **07L**: IZSL
- **07R**: IZSR

These aids are used for approach purposes (category 'A' for Aerodrome).

### En-route Aids

En-route aids include DVOR and DME at Siu Mo To (SMT) and Tung Lung (TD), both dual purpose (category 'AE' for Aerodrome and Enroute).

The only en-route radio navigation aid listed in the Hong Kong FIR is the **TUNG LUNG DVOR/DME**. Its details are:

- **Identification**: TD
- **Frequency**: 116.1 MHz (Channel 108X)
- **Hours of operation**: H24
- **Coordinates**: 221452.42N 1141735.30E
- **Elevation of DME antenna**: 249.87 m
- **Remarks**: Coverage up to 200 NM at 35,000 ft.
- **Magnetic variation**: 3°W

This aid is used for en-route navigation and as a reference for many holding fixes and routes. See [[ENR 4.1 Radio Navigation Aids]].

### Explanation of Categories

- 'A' stands for Aerodrome
- 'E' stands for Enroute
- 'AE' stands for dual purpose

These aids support both terminal and en-route navigation.
