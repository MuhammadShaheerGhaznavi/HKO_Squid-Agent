## Sheung Wan / Sky Shuttle Heliport (VHSS)

**Location:** North shore of Hong Kong Island, west of Star Ferry Pier, Central.

**Reference Point:** 221719.7N 1140908.4E

**Elevation:** 120 ft AMSL

**Magnetic Variation:** 3°W (2020), annual change 4’W

**Administration:** Sky Shuttle Helicopters Ltd., Room 1603, China Merchants Tower, Shun Tak Centre, 200 Connaught Road Central, Hong Kong; Tel: +852 2108 9873, Fax: +852 2108 9875

**Status:** Unlicensed heliport; PPR required from the administration.

**Operations:** VFR/SVFR

**Operational Hours:**
- Heliport Administration: 0100-1000
- Customs and Immigration: 0000-1459
- Handling: 0000-1459
- Security: H24

**Closure:** The heliport closes when typhoon signal No. 8 is hoisted.

**Services:** ATS, MET, and AIS briefing available from Hong Kong International Airport. No fuelling, de-icing, or cargo handling facilities.
