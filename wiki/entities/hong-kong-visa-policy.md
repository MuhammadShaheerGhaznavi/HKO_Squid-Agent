## Content
Visa-free entry for up to 180 days: British citizens. Up to 90 days: nationals of Andorra, Australia, Austria, Belgium, Brazil, Canada, Chile, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Iceland, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Japan, Korea (Republic), Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, Malaysia, Malta, Mexico, Monaco, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Portugal, San Marino, Singapore, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, UK (various categories), USA (except diplomatic passports), and many others. Up to 30 days: Armenia, Bahrain, Belarus, Bolivia, Cape Verde, Costa Rica (except provisional passports), Dominican Republic, El Salvador, Georgia, Guatemala, Honduras, Indonesia, Jordan, Kuwait, Morocco, Oman, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Qatar, Samoa, South Africa, Thailand, Tunisia, Uganda, UAE. Up to 14 days: Albania (biometric only), Algeria, Benin, Bhutan, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Burkina Faso, Chad, Comoros, Djibouti, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Guinea, Haiti, India (with PAR), Kazakhstan, Lesotho, Madagascar, Mali, Marshall Islands, Mauritania, Micronesia, Mongolia, Montenegro, Mozambique, Niger, North Macedonia, Palau, Philippines, Russia, Sao Tome and Principe, Serbia (biometric only), Suriname, Ukraine, Vatican City, US Trust Territory. Visa always required for nationals of Afghanistan, Angola, Azerbaijan, Bangladesh, Burundi, Cambodia, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Congo (DRC), Congo (Republic), Cote d'Ivoire, Cuba, Eritrea, Eswatini, Ethiopia, Gambia, Ghana, Guinea-Bissau, Iran, Iraq, Korea (DPRK), Kyrgyzstan, Laos, Lebanon, Liberia, Libya, Moldova, Myanmar, Nepal, Nicaragua, Nigeria, Pakistan, Palestine, Rwanda, Senegal, Sierra Leone, Solomon Islands, Somalia, South Sudan, Sri Lanka, Sudan, Syria, Tajikistan, Togo, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, Vietnam, Yemen. Also holders of non-biometric Albanian/Serbian passports, Costa Rican provisional passports, Special Peruvian passports, Tongan National/Protected Person passports, I-TUVALU documents, Uruguayan Decree 289/90 passports, US diplomatic passports, Vatican service passports, and stateless documents. Conditions: onward/return tickets and adequate means of support required.
