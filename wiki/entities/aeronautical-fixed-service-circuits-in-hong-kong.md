## Content
Table COM-1A lists international AFTN circuits from Hong Kong to Bangkok, Beijing, Fukuoka, Guangzhou, Haikou, Hochiminh City, Macao, Manila, and Taibei, all LDD/d type, AFS traffic, H24. Direct controller-to-controller voice links (LTF) are established with Guangzhou ACC, Macao TWR, Manila ACC, Nanning ACC, Sanya ACC, Shanghai ACC, Shantou ACC, Taibei ACC, Xiamen ACC, Zhanjiang ATCU, and Zhuhai APP, all H24. Hong Kong Radar has a direct link with Taibei Radar. Table COM-1B lists local circuits: VHHHYAHQ (CAD ASO), VHHHYLYX (AAIC/AAIA), VHHHYAYC (CAA), VHHHYKYX (MRCC), VHHHZSZX (COSPAS-SARSAT), VHHKZDZX (ATFMU), VHHKZQZX (ATCC), VHHHYNYX (AIMC/NOF), VHHHYMYX (AMO/HKO), VHHHYCYX (RCC), and VHHHYFYX (COM), all LDD/a or LDD/d, AFS traffic, H24.
