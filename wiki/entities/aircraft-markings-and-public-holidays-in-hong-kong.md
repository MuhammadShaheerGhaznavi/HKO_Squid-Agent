## Content
The nationality mark for aircraft registered in Hong Kong is the letter 'B'. It is followed by a hyphen and a three-letter registration mark, with the first letter being 'H', 'K', or 'L' representing Hong Kong. Registration marks are issued within the combinations 'HAA' to 'HZZ', 'KAA' to 'KZZ', and 'LAA' to 'LZZ'.

Public holidays in Hong Kong are notified annually in Aeronautical Information Circulars (AICs).
