## Content
The GEN 2.7 section provides sunrise/sunset tables for HONG KONG/ INTERNATIONAL (VHHH), coordinates 221832N 1135453E. The tables are prepared by the Hong Kong Observatory and are in Hong Kong Time (UTC+8). They include times for beginning of civil morning twilight (TWIL FROM), sunrise (SR), sunset (SS), and end of civil evening twilight (TWIL TO) for each day of the year. The times are calculated for an altitude of the Sun 6º below the horizon. The tables for 2025 are used as an 'average year' for 2024-2026, with deviations less than 2 minutes. Example times: On Jan 1, twilight from 6:40, sunrise 7:04, sunset 17:52, twilight to 18:16. On Jun 21, twilight from 5:15, sunrise 5:40, sunset 19:10, twilight to 19:35. On Dec 21, twilight from 6:35, sunrise 6:59, sunset 17:46, twilight to 18:10. These tables are used for planning operations during twilight hours.
