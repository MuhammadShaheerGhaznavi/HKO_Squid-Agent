## Content
The GEN 2.4 section lists the ICAO location indicators for the Hong Kong FIR and associated aerodromes. The indicators are: VHHK for Hong Kong FIR, VHHH for Hong Kong International Airport, VMMC for Macao International Airport, VMMH for Macao Heliport, VHSK for Sek Kong, and VHSS for Sheung Wan / Sky Shuttle Heliport. Note that VHSK and VHSS cannot be used in the address component of AFS messages. For Macao, refer to AIP Macao regarding the use of VMMC and VMMH as part of AFS message addresses. These indicators are used in flight planning, ATS messages, and aeronautical information services.
