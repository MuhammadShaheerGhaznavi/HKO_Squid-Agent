## Content
Primary radar locations: TSR (Hong Kong APP, Tai Mo Shan 222442N 1140724E, 24 kW, coverage 140 NM), ASR (Hong Kong APP, Sha Chau 222044N 1135324E, 23 kW, coverage 80 NM), RSR (Hong Kong ACC, Mount Parker 221605N 1141317E, 24 kW, coverage 200 NM). Secondary surveillance radar: RSSR (Hong Kong ACC, Mount Parker 221611N 1141316E, 2 kW, coverage not less than 256 NM up to FL490), TSSR (Hong Kong APP, Tai Mo Shan, 2.5 kW, coverage not less than 256 NM up to FL490), ASSR (Hong Kong APP, Sha Chau, 2.5 kW, coverage not less than 256 NM up to FL490). Radar control units provide radar service to maximum extent practicable. Radar separation minima range from 3 to 10 NM per ICAO Doc 4444. Wake turbulence separation minima: SUPER leader: 5 NM to HEAVY, 7 NM to MEDIUM, 8 NM to LIGHT; HEAVY leader: 4 NM to MEDIUM, 5 NM to LIGHT; MEDIUM leader: 5 NM to LIGHT. Enhanced wake turbulence separation (eWTS) based on groups A-G defined by mass and wing span. eWTS minima table: Group A leader: 4 NM to Group B, 5 NM to C, 5 NM to D, 6 NM to E, 6 NM to F, 8 NM to G; Group B leader: 3 NM to C, 4 NM to D, 4 NM to E, 5 NM to F, 5 NM to G, 7 NM to G? (see table); Group C leader: 3 NM to D, 3.5 NM to E, 3.5 NM to F, 6 NM to G; Group D leader: 4 NM to G; Group E leader: 4 NM to G. Separation minima shall not be infringed; if catch-up situation, instruct break-off or missed approach. SSR code assignment: departing traffic and diverting traffic: 3301-3377, 3501-3577, 5101-5177, 5301-5377, 5701-5777; arriving traffic if not assigned: 5201-5277; overflying traffic: same as departing. Transponder procedures: outbound per ATC, Mode C; inbound use last assigned code or 2000, Mode C, within 300 NM. Emergency code 7700, communication failure 7600, unlawful interference 7500. Radar failure: provide non-radar separation, may use reduced vertical separation (500 ft) temporarily. Radio communication failure: comply with ENR 1.5 procedures.
