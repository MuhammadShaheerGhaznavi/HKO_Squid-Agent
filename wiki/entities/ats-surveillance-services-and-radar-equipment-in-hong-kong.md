## Content
Hong Kong primary radar equipment includes: TSR (Hong Kong APP) at Tai Mo Shan 222442N 1140724E, 24 kW, coverage 140 NM; ASR (Hong Kong APP) at Sha Chau 222044N 1135324E, 23 kW, coverage 80 NM; RSR (Hong Kong ACC) at Mount Parker 221605N 1141317E, 24 kW, coverage 200 NM. Secondary Surveillance Radar (SSR) includes: RSSR (Hong Kong ACC) at Mount Parker 221611N 1141316E, 2 kW, coverage not less than 256 NM up to FL490; TSSR (Hong Kong APP) at Tai Mo Shan, 2.5 kW, coverage not less than 256 NM up to FL490; ASSR (Hong Kong APP) at Sha Chau, 2.5 kW, coverage not less than 256 NM up to FL490. Radar control units provide radar service to aircraft to the maximum extent practicable. Radar control service includes separation, monitoring, vectoring, emergency assistance, and weather information. Radar separation minima range from 3 to 10 NM depending on airspace, distance from airport/radar head, and number of radar stations tracking. Wake turbulence separation minima are distance-based: SUPER leader to HEAVY 5 NM, MEDIUM 7 NM, LIGHT 8 NM; HEAVY to MEDIUM 5 NM, LIGHT 6 NM; MEDIUM to LIGHT 5 NM. Enhanced Wake Turbulence Separation (eWTS) uses groups A-G based on mass and wing span; minima range from 3 NM to 8 NM. SSR code assignment: departing traffic uses 3301-3377, 3501-3577, 5101-5177, 5301-5377, 5701-5777; arriving/local traffic uses 5201-5277; overflying uses same as departing. Transponder codes: 7700 emergency, 7600 communication failure, 7500 unlawful interference. In radar failure, non-radar separation is provided, with temporary reduced vertical separation (e.g., 500 ft) if needed.
