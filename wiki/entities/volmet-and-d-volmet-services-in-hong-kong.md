## Content
VOLMET broadcasts in English are available on HF and VHF channels. VHF: 128.875 MHz continuous H24, including ZGSZ (Shenzhen) METAR/SPECI, VMMC (Macao) METAR/SPECI & Trend FCST, RCTP (Taibei) METAR/SPECI & Trend FCST, RCKH (Gaoxiong) METAR/SPECI & Trend FCST. D-VOLMET: as requested, H24, includes VHHH HKG SIGMET (if any). HF: 6679 kHz, 8828 kHz, 13282 kHz, broadcast H+15 to H+20 and H+45 to H+50, H24, including VHHH (with note 1 and 2), ZGGG (Guangzhou/Baiyun), ROAH (Naha), RCTP, RCKH, RPLL (Manila International), RPVM (Mactan) METAR/SPECI & Trend FCST, and VHHH FCST/Amend FCST. Note: METAR/SPECI older than two hours at broadcast time are not transmitted. Special air-reports not covered by SIGMET are included as last item in D-VOLMET.
