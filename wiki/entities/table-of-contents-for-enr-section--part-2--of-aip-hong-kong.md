## Content
The ENR section of AIP Hong Kong is organized as follows: ENR 0.6 Table of Contents; ENR 1 General Rules and Procedures (ENR 1.1 General Rules, ENR 1.2 Visual Flight Rules, ENR 1.3 Instrument Flight Rules, ENR 1.4 ATS Airspace Classification, ENR 1.5 Holding, Approach and Departure Procedures, ENR 1.6 ATS Surveillance Services and Procedures, ENR 1.7 Altimeter Setting Procedures, ENR 1.8 Regional Supplementary Procedures (Doc 7030), ENR 1.9 Air Traffic Flow Management (ATFM), ENR 1.10 Flight Planning, ENR 1.11 Addressing of Flight Plan Messages, ENR 1.12 Interception of Civil Aircraft, ENR 1.13 Unlawful Interference, ENR 1.14 Air Traffic Incidents); ENR 2 Air Traffic Services Airspace (ENR 2.1 FIR, UIR, TMA; ENR 2.2 Other Regulated Airspace); ENR 3 ATS Routes (ENR 3.1 ATS Routes, ENR 3.2 Upper ATS Routes, ENR 3.3 Performance Based Navigation (PBN) Routes, ENR 3.4 Helicopter Routes, ENR 3.5 Other Routes, ENR 3.6 En-route Holding); ENR 4 Radio Navigation Aids/Systems (ENR 4.1 Radio Navigation Aids - En-route, ENR 4.2 Special Navigation Systems, ENR 4.3 Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS), ENR 4.4 Name-Code Designators for Significant Points, ENR 4.5 Aeronautical Ground Lights - En-route); ENR 5 Navigational Warnings (ENR 5.1 Prohibited, Restricted and Danger Areas, ENR 5.2 Military Exercise and Training Areas, ENR 5.3 Other Activities of a Dangerous Nature and Other Potential Hazards, ENR 5.4 Air Navigation Obstacles - En-route, ENR 5.5 Aerial Sporting and Recreational Activities, ENR 5.6 Bird Migration in Hong Kong); ENR 6 Charts (ENR 6.1 En-Route Chart Hong Kong, ENR 6.2 Flight Plan Routes Transiting Hong Kong TMA, ENR 6.3 Communication Facilities in Hong Kong FIR, ENR 6.4 Radio Facilities Chart, ENR 6.5 Terminal Transition Routes (Hong Kong Departures/Arrivals), ENR 6.6 Terminal Transition Routes (Macao Departures/Arrivals), ENR 6.7 Hong Kong ATC Sector Boundaries Chart). Specific sub-sections include procedures for ATS routes A202, A1(E), G581, G86, M750, M503, M771, L642, M772, Q1, and others. The document is dated 9 JUL 26 with amendment 7/26.
