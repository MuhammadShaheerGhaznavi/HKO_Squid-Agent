## Content
Associated MET office is Hong Kong Airport MET Office, H24. TAF preparation not applicable. Briefing/consultation via self-briefing using AMIDS and telephone consultation. METAR/TAF for HKIA available from AMIDS in English. Charts available: S, U, P, W, T, satellite and radar pictures from AMIDS. Supplementary equipment: current temperature, wind direction and speed from meteorological equipment at heliport. No ATS units provided with information.
