# Hong Kong International Airport (VHHH)

## Location and Administration
- **ARP Coordinates:** 221832N 1135453E
- **Location:** 25 km west of Central District, Hong Kong Island
- **Elevation:** 28 ft
- **Reference Temperature:** 34°C
- **Magnetic Variation:** 3°W (2020), annual change 4'W
- **Administration:** Airport Authority Hong Kong
- **Address:** HKIA Tower, 1 Sky Plaza Road, Hong Kong International Airport, Lantau
- **Contact:** Tel +852 2188 7111, Fax +852 2824 0717, website http://www.hkairport.com

## Operations
- **Traffic:** IFR and VFR permitted
- **Operational Hours:** All services (AD Administration, Customs, Health, AIS, ATS, MET, Fuelling, Handling, Security) operate H24
- **De-icing:** Not available

## Runways
- Three runways: 07R/25L, 07C/25C, and 07L/25R

## Facilities and Services
- **Cargo Handling:** Up to 4,500 kg
- **Fuel:** Jet A1 (AVTUR 650) and all oil grades; hydrant refuelling at 500 gpm on all main and cargo stands
- **Hangar Space:** Limited, unheated, available by prior arrangement
- **Repair Facilities:** Major and minor repairs, including engine changes
- **Ground Power:** Fixed 960V 400 Hz at all main and cargo apron stands
- **Passenger Facilities:** Hotels, restaurants, transportation (rail, buses, taxis, ferries), medical facilities, bank, post office, tourist office
- **Fire Fighting:** Category 10, with rescue launches and high-speed rescue boats
- **Aircraft Recovery:** Specialized equipment for up to Code F aircraft, coordinated by Airport Authority Hong Kong
