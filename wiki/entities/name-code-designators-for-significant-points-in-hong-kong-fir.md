## Content
The Hong Kong FIR has a comprehensive list of name-code designators for significant points, each with coordinates and associated ATS routes, PBN routes, SIDs, STARs, IAPs, or holding fixes. Notable points include: ABBEY (221611.00N 1145525.92E) on V512/V522/V591, used for STAR-VHHH and holding; BIGEX (221310.35N 1140148.20E) on multiple routes (A1, A202, A461, A583, B330, G581, G86, L642, M771, P901); BETTY (212910.82N 1143331.92E) on V525/V533/V534/V542/V551/W29, used for STAR-VHHH and holding; CANTO (213902.63N 1134225.09E) on V561/V571, used for STAR-VHHH and holding; CHALI (211745.00N 1133641.00E) on J104, used for STAR-VMMC and holding; COMBI (212937.10N 1132744.30E) on V561/V571, used for holding; DAKTO (205330.89N 1151115.58E) on V525/V533/W29, used for holding; EATON (210724.40N 1154301.10E) on V525/W29, used for holding; FISHA (221657.64N 1152011.61E) on V512/V522/V591, used for holding; GAMBA (211908.12N 1125536.21E) on V561/V571, used for holding; GODEN (203435.35N 1141832.48E) used for holding; HOCKY (203549.35N 1143428.48E) on V534/V542/V551, used for holding; LIMES (220625.60N 1134632.60E) used for STAR-VHHH, IAP-VHHH, and holding; LUDLA (221340.79N 1140428.75E) used for IAP-VHHH, STAR-VHHH, and holding; MYWAY (201146.30N 1124307.70E) on V561, used for holding; OTKUM (220334.20N 1140455.85E) used for holding; RIVMI (222403.96N 1141909.28E) used for IAP-VHHH, holding, and STAR-VHHH; ROCCA (213155.42N 1133451.44E) on V561/V571, used for STAR-VHHH and holding; TAPPO (211920.61N 1141434.62E) on J103, used for holding; TEDUR (221118.77N 1142351.26E) used for STAR-VHHH and holding. Many points are used for SIDs and STARs for Hong Kong International (VHHH) and Macao (VMMC) airports. Some points are marked with symbols: * for helicopter routes reporting points (e.g., CHAKO, DELTA, FATUT, FOVOG, GOGRE, HASAN, HOROT, HOTEL, LEVKE, PEARL, QUBEC, ROMEO, TANGO, WALIN, WAVOS, ZEXEK), ^ for through area waypoints (e.g., BIMIX, ENROM, GOBBI, IGLEG, LEGOD, MADRU, MORTU, NOBAD, SANKU, SULUX), + for significant points for route W130 (LANDA), # for significant points for route R473 (SIERA). See [[ENR 4.4 Name-Code Designators]].
