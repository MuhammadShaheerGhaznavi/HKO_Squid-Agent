## Content
Passenger facilities at VHSS include hotels in the city, restaurants at Shun Tak Centre and in the city, transportation via buses, taxis, and ferries, medical facilities at Shun Tak Centre and hospitals in the city, banks and post office at Shun Tak Centre, and tourist office at Shun Tak Centre. Passenger movement on the helipad is suspended when wind speed exceeds 30 kts. Rescue and fire fighting: heliport category H2, with light rescue equipment; rescue launches with foam/water and life rafts available from Government Department at short notice. Meteorological information is provided by Hong Kong Airport MET Office (H24), with METAR/TAF for HKIA available from AMIDS; self-briefing via AMIDS and telephone consultation; charts (S, U, P, W, T, satellite, radar) available; current temperature, wind direction, and speed from heliport equipment.
