## Content
Hong Kong ATC provides ATS surveillance service to aircraft on departure, approach, and missed approach to/from Macao International Airport while transiting Hong Kong airspace. The communication facilities include:
- APP: Hong Kong Radar, 123.95 MHz, H24
- APP: Hong Kong Radar, 132.225 MHz, Secondary
- DEP: Hong Kong Departure, 122.0 MHz, H24
- DEP: Hong Kong Departure, 122.65 MHz, As advised
- DEP: Hong Kong Departure, 124.05 MHz, Secondary
Refer to AIP Macao for other ATS communication facilities at Macao International Airport.
