## Content
Hong Kong ATC provides ATS surveillance service to aircraft on departure, approach, and missed approach to/from Macao International Airport while they are transiting Hong Kong airspace. The communication facilities include:
- **Hong Kong Radar**: 123.95 MHz (H24), with secondary 132.225 MHz
- **Hong Kong Departure**: 122.0 MHz (H24), 122.65 MHz (as advised), and 124.05 MHz (secondary)

These frequencies are used for coordination and control during transit. For other ATS communication facilities at Macao International Airport, refer to AIP Macao.
